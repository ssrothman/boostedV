## 1. Scope

Q1 asks for existing direct limits on hadronic resonances (qq̄, gg, bb̄, hadronic W/Z), any spin or parity, single, pair or associated production, with the limit form, CL and observed vs expected. The primary window is 10 GeV–1 TeV. Results outside it are kept and flagged `OUT`. Rows are drawn from the 32 Q1 notes. The other 32 notes (polarization and angular-coefficient measurements, energy-correlator theory: 1103.5699, 1104.3829, 1202.5272, 1203.2165, 1410.1154, 1504.03512, 1606.00689, 1902.05759, 2005.03799, 2008.04174, 2009.09429, 2110.11231, 2203.01602, 2207.03511, 2209.14903, 2211.09435, 2310.04350, 2402.16365, 2409.16731, 2503.11317, 2507.03500, 2509.13759, 2603.26463, 2604.25678, 2607.12879, 2607.20246, 2610.38688, hep-ex_0301027, hep-ex_0305025, hep-ex_0312047, hep-ph_9406381, ins335604) were read to their "Quoted results" heading and hold no hadronic-resonance limit. So none of the rows below is a polarization row, and no reference frame applies. Rows from recasts, theory mappings and projections are marked `RECAST`, `THEORY` or `PROJECTION`; they are not experimental limits. "obs/exp inferred" means the note maps HEPData groups to observed/expected from header order or band placement, not from an explicit label. Rows whose lower mass edge is not stated in the note are placed at the end of the table.
