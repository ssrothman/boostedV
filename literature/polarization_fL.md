## 1. Scope and conventions

**Question Q2:** measured longitudinal polarisation of W or Z bosons (f_0, f_L in the "longitudinal" sense, A_0, ρ_00, joint fractions such as f_00), with the frame, definition, binning (especially pT) and uncertainties.

**Notes included** (all notes were read at least to "Quoted results"; those with Q2 content are tabulated):

- (a) 1103.5699, 1104.3829, 1203.2165, 1504.03512, 1606.00689, 2008.04174, 2203.01602, 2509.13759, 2604.25678; theory: hep-ph_9406381, ins335604.
- (a′) theory only: 2610.38688 (pp part), 2207.03511.
- (b) 2209.14903, 2005.03799, 1410.1154, 1202.5272.
- (c) 1902.05759, 2110.11231, 2211.09435, 2402.16365, 2310.04350, 2607.12879, 2607.20246, 2503.11317, 2009.09429, 2507.03500 (no Q2 quantity); theory: 2409.16731.
- (d) hep-ex_0301027, hep-ex_0312047, hep-ex_0305025 (summary talk); theory: 2610.38688 (e+e− part).

**Notes read and excluded from the tables** (no Q2 quantity in the note): all Q1 resonance-search notes (1306.2629, 1302.0531, 1412.7706, 1603.08791, 1710.00159, 1801.08769, 1805.02780, 1805.09299, 1806.00843, 1810.11822, 1901.10917, 1905.10331, 1906.08589, 1909.04114, 1911.03761, 1911.03947, 2002.11325, 2206.09997, 2307.14944, 2403.08547, 2408.00049, 2412.03747, 2507.17884, 2509.01219, 2510.21641, 2603.21965, 2605.28934, 2606.03222, 2609.21850, ins298412, ins355978, ins356676). 1906.08589 only states that its signal samples are "primarily longitudinally polarised" (Sec. 4.2), which is not a measurement. 2603.26463 (H→ZZ* entanglement) measures C_{2,1,2,−1} and C_{2,2,2,−2}, which are off-diagonal-type spin-density observables (Q3), not a longitudinal fraction.

**Conventions used in the tables**

- "f_L" is ambiguous across the corpus. In the LHC/Tevatron W and top papers, f_L (F_L) is the **left-handed** fraction. In OPAL hep-ex/0312047 (as recorded in its note) "f_L" is the **longitudinal** fraction. The tables always name the quantity as the note defines it.
- "▲ highest pT bin" (or "▲ highest mass bin") marks the highest bin reached in that paper.
- "THEORY", "PREDICTION", "SUMMARY TALK" flags mark rows that are not experimental measurements.
- stat/syst: copied as the note gives them. Where the note gives a total only, the stat column says "total only" and the value is in syst as "total ±x". Signed asymmetric errors are copied as written in the note (e.g. "minus -0.0274, plus 0.0285").
- MC/SM predictions printed next to measurements are not tabulated except where a note row is the only quantitative content or where a prediction is needed to read a flag.

## 4. Definitions and frames

Definitions as the notes state them. No relation below is derived here.

**A_0 (lepton angular coefficient)**

- Angular expansion with A_0 (hep-ph/9406381 Eq. (27), CS frame): (3/16π) dσ^{U+L}/dp_T² dy [(1+cos²θ) + ½A_0(1−3cos²θ) + A_1 sin2θ cosϕ + ½A_2 sin²θ cos2ϕ + A_3 sinθ cosϕ + A_4 cosθ] — notes/hep-ph_9406381.md. The same ½A_0(1−3cos²θ) term appears in 1103.5699 Eq. (1), 1504.03512 Eq. (1), 1606.00689 Eq. (1), 2203.01602 Eq. (1), 2604.25678 Eq. (1); 2509.13759 Eq. (1) uses nine harmonic polynomials with A0–A7.
- A_0 = 2σ^L/σ^{U+L} (hep-ph/9406381 Eq. (26); the note says the equation structure is paraphrased because the extracted text is garbled) — notes/hep-ph_9406381.md.
- A0 = 2σL/σU+L (1606.00689 Eq. (16), verifier-noted; also A2 = 4σT/σU+L, A4 = 2σP/σU+L) — notes/1606.00689.md.
- "The coefficients A₀ and A₂ parametrize the longitudinal (L) polarization fraction ... and the interference between its transverse (T) polarization states, respectively" (2604.25678 sec. 0.1); no f_L, f_0 or ρ₀₀ defined — notes/2604.25678.md.

## 5. Caveats

1. **"f_L" means different things.** Left-handed in the LHC/Tevatron W and top papers; longitudinal in OPAL hep-ex/0312047 and in 2409.16731 Eq. (11). The longitudinal fraction is f_0 / F_0 / f0 / ρ_00 / A_0-related elsewhere. Check the quantity column before comparing rows.
2. **Frames differ between classes and are not interchangeable.** Single-boson Drell–Yan A_0 is in the Collins–Soper frame; W+jets f_0 is in the helicity frame (1104.3829, 1203.2165); diboson fractions are in the diboson CM frame; top fractions use the top/b direction; LEP uses the W flight direction. 2607.20246 Sec. 3: "Polarisation fractions are not Lorentz invariant and hence depend on a chosen reference frame." No row has been transformed between frames.
3. **Joint vs single-boson fractions.** f_00 / f_LL / f_X0 / f_0X (2211.09435, 2402.16365, 2607.12879, 2607.20246) are joint diboson quantities; μ_LL and σ(Z_L Z_L), σB(W_L W_L jj) are signal strengths or polarised cross sections, not fractions (2310.04350, 2607.12879, 2503.11317, 2009.09429). f_LL in 2607.12879 is a fraction of qq̄→ZZ + EW ZZjj only (gg→ZZ treated as background).
4. **The highest-pT longitudinal measurements are lepton angular coefficients, not fractions.** The W A0 values above 1 in 2509.13759 (e.g. 1.383, 1.856, 1.280, 1.143) are copied as given; that note gives no relation of A0 to a polarisation fraction and flags that rebinning of the A0 HEPData rows is not stated, and that the neutrino p_z ambiguity "dilutes the sensitivity" to A0. No hadronic (boosted) V longitudinal fraction has been measured in any note; (a′) is theory only.
5. **Open source disagreements carried into rows.** 2607.20246: HEPData vs paper Table 5 uncertainties disagree for f_00, f_0X, f_X0 (unresolved). 1606.00689: HEPData Table 11 A0−A2 disagrees with the paper at low pT (not used here); all 23 A0 pT bins (0–600 GeV) are in the note. 1202.5272: 1D f+ syst 0.030 vs 0.031. 2203.01602: "Total" row read as the central value by inference; fifth y bin missing. 2211.09435: Table 3/5 relative uncertainty totals inconsistent with Table 2/4. hep-ex/0301027 Table 6: W⁻ vs W± label conflict. 2310.04350: 140 fb⁻¹ (text) vs 139 fb⁻¹ (HEPData).
6. **Level of the result differs.** Reconstructed/detector-level fits: 1103.5699, 1104.3829, 1504.03512, 1606.00689, 2110.11231, 1410.1154, 2604.25678 (pre-FSR definition via detector-level templates). Particle/fiducial or Born level: 1203.2165 (generator-level acceptance), 1902.05759, 2211.09435, 2402.16365, 2203.01602 (Born), 2607.20246. Parton level: 2209.14903.
7. **Uncertainty formats vary.** Many diboson results give totals only; 2509.13759 and 2604.25678 give asymmetric syst; 1203.2165 splits syst into instrumental/analysis and template; 2005.03799 uses stat+bkg for ATLAS and stat-only for CMS. No uncertainties were combined here.
8. **Theory/projection rows** (hep-ph/9406381, ins335604, 2207.03511, 2409.16731, 2610.38688, the NNLO top prediction) are predictions only. 2409.16731 states its projection (Eq. 4) is invalid with fiducial cuts or neutrino reconstruction.
9. **Secondary sources.** hep-ex/0305025 is a single-author LEP summary quoting L3 and OPAL notes that were not read; its L3 21.8 ± 2.7 ± 1.6 % and the L3 paper's 0.218 ± 0.027 ± 0.016 (hep-ex/0301027) are listed separately because no note states they are the same result. OPAL hep-ex/0312047 Sec. 6.1 says its 183 and 189 GeV values "differ slightly from those previously published by OPAL".
