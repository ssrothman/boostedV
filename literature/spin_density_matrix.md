## 1. Scope and reading rules

- Included: every note whose quoted results contain more than one element of a W or Z spin density matrix, in any topology. That covers A0–A7 sets, diagonal ρ sets (f0 with fL−fR, fL, fR), off-diagonal or Im ρ combinations, joint diboson fractions, polarisation-state cross sections, and tensor-basis C coefficients. Theory and convention papers are in Sec. 3.
- Borderline rows are included but flagged `BORDERLINE`. In each, the note asks the lead to decide Q2 scope (2607.12879, 2310.04350, 2607.20246, 2009.09429, 2503.11317, 2110.11231).
- Excluded:
  - 2507.03500: the note's "Scope finding" says no Q2 or Q3 quantity is measured.
  - 2605.28934: uses spinning coefficients c^(j) of a resonance X, not W/Z ρ, and is a projection.
  - All Q1 dijet/resonance notes except ins355978 (UA2 dijet): each states that no polarisation quantity is measured.
- Flags used: `THEORY` (prediction, not a measurement), `PROJECTION`, `BORDERLINE` (Q3 fit questioned in the note), `OPEN` (unresolved discrepancy carried from the note), `[verifier …]` (value or statement corrected by a verifier, as tagged in the note).
- Uncertainty notation is copied from the note: "a / b" means stat / syst unless the cell says otherwise. Asymmetric syst is written as minus/plus.
- Frame text is copied from the note. "not stated" means the note says not stated.

## 4. Leptonic vs hadronic reach

Only statements the notes attribute to their sources.

- **Hadronic W decays fold ρ_++ and ρ_--.** OPAL (hep-ex/0312047): "Leptonic decays measure rho_{++} and rho_{--} separately; hadronic decays fold them, so only their sum is available (Sec. 2.3)." The same note says "Off-diagonal SDM elements use leptonic decays only (Sec. 4.1)." — notes/hep-ex_0312047.md.
- **Quark charge not reconstructed.** L3 (hep-ex/0301027), hadronic decays, Eq. (2): the fit uses f± = f+ + f− and f0 with |cos θ*_q|, because the "Sign of the quark charge [is] not reconstructed". θ*_q is approximated by the thrust axis. Leptonic decays, Eq. (1), separate f−, f+, f0. — notes/hep-ex_0301027.md.
- **Energy correlators on hadronic V (theory).** 2207.03511 uses diagonal entries "dρ_00 and dρ_TT ≡ dρ_++ + dρ_--". θ* is defined relative to "the helicity-plus fermion". Off-diagonal entries (|Δh| = 1, 2) appear through e^{i(h−h′)φ} and the A_int asymmetry. — notes/2207.03511.md, Sec. "Diagonal entries", "Off-diagonal entries".
- **Energy flow vs charge flux (theory).** 2610.38688, App. D: "The energy flow is parity even and probes the longitudinal fraction \rho_{00} , the alignment \rho_{+-} through \cos 2\Phi and \sin 2\Phi , and the combination \rho_{+0}-\rho_{0-} through \cos\Phi and \sin\Phi". The energy-flow and charge-flux traces (Eq. 82) follow "the same parity-even and parity-odd split as the angular coefficients of the leptonic decay". The note does not quote what the charge-flux trace probes. — notes/2610.38688.md.
- **Coefficients tied to decay couplings.** CMS 8 TeV Z (1504.03512): A3 and A4 are "also sensitive to the V-A structure of the couplings of the muons". In 2409.16731 (Eqs. 11, 16) the l = 1 coefficients carry the lepton factor η_ℓ ≈ 0.219 (α_{1,0} ∝ η_i(f_+ − f_−); C_{1,m1,1,m3} = 8π γ_{1,m1,1,m3}/η_ℓ²), while the l = 2 relations as written contain no η_ℓ. Mirkes 1992 (abstract): the sign of the "T-odd" helicity cross sections discriminates V−A from V+A. LEP SDM estimator (hep-ex/0305025, Eq. 6): projection operators "assuming V−A decay". — notes/1504.03512.md, notes/2409.16731.md, notes/ins335604.md, notes/hep-ex_0305025.md.
- **Charge-dependent signs in leptonic W.** The A4 ↔ (f_L − f_R) sign depends on the W charge (1203.2165 Eq. 4–5; 2008.04174 Eq. 3; 1104.3829). For W+ the Collins–Soper angle uses the neutrino direction, and the neutrino pz ambiguity "dilutes the sensitivity of the measurement to the coefficients that directly depend on" it (A0 and A4) (2509.13759, Sec. 3). — notes cited.
- **Z decay analysing factor.** In WZ fits the Z distribution carries α = 2c_v c_a/(c_v² + c_a²) (1902.05759, Eq. 2) or α = 0.147 (2211.09435, Eq. 2). The W distribution uses the lepton charge q_W. — notes/1902.05759.md, notes/2211.09435.md.

## 5. Caveats

1. **No pT-differential measurement of any off-diagonal ρ element or full SDM outside Drell–Yan.**
   - pT-binned multi-element results exist for inclusive Drell–Yan A_i: Z up to pT 400 GeV (2604.25678), 600 GeV (1606.00689), or an open last bin of 200–9999 GeV (1504.03512), and W up to 600 GeV (2509.13759).
   - The only pT-binned diboson result is 2402.16365, with two p_T^Z bins and f_0T, f_T0 merged.
   - LEP SDM results are binned in cos θ_W, not pT (hep-ex/0312047, hep-ex/0305025, hep-ex/0301027).
   - H → ZZ* C coefficients (2603.26463), all other joint diboson fractions. W fractions in 1203.2165 are binned in pT^W (35–50, >50 GeV); CMS 1104.3829 is single-bin; CMS 2008.04174 is |y_W|-binned.
2. **No measurement on hadronic or boosted V decays.** Every hadronic-V SDM item in the corpus is `THEORY` or `PROJECTION` (2207.03511, 2610.38688). Hadronic-W measurements exist only at LEP, where ρ_++ and ρ_-- are folded and off-diagonal elements use leptonic decays only.
3. **A_i are not ρ_λλ′.**
   - A_i coefficients are measured in the Collins–Soper frame.
   - Fractions are measured in helicity, "modified helicity", diboson-CM, ZZ-CM, top-decay and LEP W-rest frames. The notes state that polarisation fractions are frame-dependent (2607.20246 Sec. 3; 2211.09435 Sec. 2).
   - Analyses use different leptons: the negative lepton (CMS, ATLAS Z), μ+ (LHCb), the positive lepton (CDF; 2409.16731), or the neutrino for W+ (2509.13759).
   - Stated maps include: A_i ↔ unnormalised H_{mm′} (1606.00689, App. A); α/γ ↔ tensor-basis A, C (2409.16731); helicity ρ ↔ EEC structure functions (2610.38688); f ↔ A_0, A_4 for W (1203.2165); A_0, A_2, A_4 ↔ σ^L, σ^T, σ^P (1606.00689, App. A); f00, fTT, f0T, fT0 ↔ diagonal joint ρ (2211.09435, Sec. 2); C ↔ ρ at LO (2603.26463, Main text); ρ ↔ polarised cross sections (OPAL hep-ex/0312047, Eqs. 10–11); helicity amplitudes ↔ joint and single-W SDM (LEP hep-ex/0305025, Eqs. 4–8); proportionalities only (CMS 2008.04174, CMS 1104.3829); see Sec. 3.1–3.2 for all.
   - Values in different conventions are listed side by side and are not comparable without these maps.
4. **Level and extraction differ.**
   - Detector- or reco-level template fits: 1504.03512, 1606.00689 (regularised), 2604.25678, 1103.5699, 2110.11231.
   - Born or particle level: 2203.01602, 2211.09435, 2402.16365, 2509.13759 (folding).
   - Parton level: 2603.26463, 2209.14903.
   - Several diboson fits fix non-measured fractions or interference to MC (2607.20246, 2402.16365 2-parameter fit, 2607.12879, 2310.04350).
5. **Open data discrepancies carried from the notes.**
   - 1606.00689: HEPData Table 11 A0−A2 vs paper (low pT).
   - 2607.20246: HEPData vs paper Table 5 uncertainties.
   - 2203.01602: "Total" row read as the central value by inference; no HEPData.
   - 2604.25678: HEPData A5 tables named "(A_1)".
   - 1103.5699: Table I in ×10⁻¹ units, read from images.
   - 2211.09435: Table 3/5 relative totals inconsistent.
   - ins335604: abstract only.
   - hep-ex_0301027 (L3): Table 6 caption says W− but column header says W±.
   - hep-ex_0305025 (LEP Fig. 5): text and caption disagree on left/right.
   - hep-ex_0312047 (OPAL): HEPData Tables 5–7 have no units.
   - 2409.16731: printed-table typos are flagged in the note.
