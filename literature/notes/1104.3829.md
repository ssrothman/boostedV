# 1104.3829 — Measurement of the Polarization of W Bosons with Large Transverse Momenta in W+Jets Events at the LHC

- Authors/Collaboration: CMS Collaboration (CMS note EWK-10-014); 2011; Phys. Rev. Lett. 107 (2011) 021802 (per INSPIRE; arXiv text read is v1, 19 Apr 2011); INSPIRE recid 896585; HEPData record: none (https://www.hepdata.net/record/ins896585 returns 404; HEPData title and arXiv-ID searches found no record for this paper)
- sqrt(s) = 7 TeV; integrated luminosity 36 ± 1.4 pb^-1 (text: "corresponding to an integrated luminosity of 36 ± 1.4 pb -1 at a center-of-mass energy of 7 TeV"); data
- Topology / final state: pp -> W + jets, W -> e nu and W -> mu nu (both charges); events with up to three reconstructed jets. Background: ttbar+jets, Z+jets, photon+jets (MC); QCD multijet (electron channel only).
- Selection summary:
  - One electron (p_T > 25 GeV, |eta| < 2.4) or muon (p_T > 20 GeV, |eta| < 2.1); trigger lepton p_T > 22 (15) GeV for e (mu) (Text, selection paragraph; the paper has no section numbers in the HTML)
  - M_T > 50 (30) GeV for e (mu); p_T(W) > 50 GeV (text: "we require p T ( \PW ) > 50 GeV")
  - Second-lepton veto (p_T > 15 (10) GeV, looser isolation); lepton isolation within Delta R = 0.3
  - Jets: particle-flow, anti-k_T with R = 0.5, p_T > 30 GeV, |eta| < 5; at most three jets
  - Event counts in data: 5485 (e), 8626 (mu)
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Observable fitted: lepton projection L_P = p_T(l) · p_T(W) / |p_T(W)|^2 (Eq. 3), with p_T(W) estimated from the vector sum of MET and p_T(l). Stated relation: cos theta* = 2(L_P − 1/2) "in the limit of very high p_T(W)".
  - Angular distribution (Eq. 1) in terms of A_0 ... A_4; integrated over phi* (Eq. 2). Stated: "A 0 ∝ f 0 and A 4 ∝ ± ( f L − f R ) depending on the W boson charge, where by definition f i > 0 and f L + f R + f 0 = 1." The sign mapping of "±" to W+/W- is not stated explicitly.
  - Frame: helicity frame. Text: "the polar angle (theta*) of the charged lepton from the decay in the W rest frame is measured with respect to the boson flight direction in the laboratory frame. The azimuthal angle (phi*) is defined to be zero for the proton which has the smaller theta* in the boson rest frame."
  - Measured quantities: (f_L − f_R) and f_0 for W+ and W-. f_L and f_R are NOT measured separately; the paper does not quote f_L or f_R individually.
  - Fit: binned maximum likelihood fit to L_P, separately per W charge and per channel; templates from MC reweighted to each polarization state's angular distribution in the W rest frame. Fit range 0.0 < L_P < 1.3. Electroweak background normalization fixed to MC; other components floated.
  - p_T binning: single inclusive bin, p_T(W) > 50 GeV. No p_T-differential result is given in the paper.

## Caveats

- Measures (f_L − f_R) and f_0 only. f_L and f_R are not separately quoted; the Abstract states f_L, f_R, f_0 are "determined" but the numerical results are only for (f_L − f_R) and f_0. Do not read f_L from this paper.
- Single inclusive bin p_T(W) > 50 GeV; no p_T dependence in the paper.
- The fit is to the reconstructed lepton projection L_P with MC templates. Whether any unfolding to particle level was done is not stated.
- Electroweak background normalization is fixed to MC in the fit. QCD multijet background (electron channel) is floated and taken from anti-selected data.
- Fit range restricted to 0.0 < L_P < 1.3 (text, "as a result of the minimization of the combined statistical and systematic uncertainties").
- The paper states results are "consistent" between e and mu; text: "With the current sensitivity, the values of (f_L − f_R) and f_0 do not differ significantly for W+ and W−."
- Comparison to theory: text says "When compared to recent standard model calculations [14], the results agree well." [14] = Bern et al., arXiv:1103.5445. No numerical comparison given in the text.
- The Abstract gives the muon results as the most precise. The Abstract and Conclusion values match Table 1 muon rows (checked against the text).
