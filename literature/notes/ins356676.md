# none — Search for Quark Compositeness, Axigluons and Heavy Particles using the Dijet Invariant Mass Spectrum Observed in pp̄ Collisions
- CDF Collaboration (F. Abe et al.), 1993, Phys. Rev. Lett. 71 (1993) 2542-2546 (DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.71.2542; Fermilab-Pub-93/151-E, CDF/ANAL/JET/CDFR/2064 [verifier w2_4]), INSPIRE recid 356676, HEPData record 19713 (ins356676 v1; only Table 1 present, see Open issues)
- sqrt(s) = 1.8 TeV, integrated luminosity 4.2 pb^-1 (text p. 6: "based on an integrated luminosity of 4.2 pb−1"), data
- Topology / final state: two-jet final state (dijet); resonances decaying to light-quark pairs (u,d,s,c,b). Model-based limits only, no detector-level resonance-decay topology beyond the two leading jets.
- Selection summary:
  - Single-jet online triggers, at least one cluster with E_T above 20, 40 or 60 GeV (p. 7).
  - Vertex within 60 cm of detector centre; the two leading jets with |eta| < 0.7 (pp. 7-8).
  - Fixed-cone jet clustering, cone radius R = 1 (p. 7).
  - M_jj^obs = invariant mass of the two leading jets; additional jets not included (p. 8).
  - Detector-effect corrections applied to the measured jet energy/momentum (average 22% at M_jj = 140 GeV/c², 17% above 600 GeV/c²); underlying-event and out-of-cone corrections not applied to data (p. 7).
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Resonance: Breit-Wigner f(M_jj) = S Γ / (2π[(M_jj − M0)² + (Γ/2)²]), with S the cross section for decay products within acceptance (|η| < 0.7) (p. 10). Width Γ = k M0 with k = 0.02, 0.1, 0.2 (p. 10). No spin effect is taken into account (p. 10). Reference frame / polarization: not stated (no angular observables are used).
  - Limit on S (pb) as a function of M0 and Γ (Table 2), i.e. the "observable cross section" times the branching ratio into light quarks.
  - Axigluon: width Γa = N α_s M_a / 6, N = number of open decay channels (p. 11); N = 10 and N = 20 analysed; amplitudes coherently summed with QCD and convolved with structure functions (p. 11).
  - Compositeness (Table 1, contact term Λc) is an indirect bound and is out of scope; not recorded below.

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)
These are stated for the measured dσ/dM_jj and the LO QCD predictions. The paper does not state explicitly how each enters the resonance limit (Table 2); the limit method states only that predictions are convoluted "with both Poisson statistical fluctuations and non-Gaussian resolution functions with their correlated bin-to-bin uncertainties" (p. 9).
- Integrated luminosity: "The uncertainty in the integrated luminosity is 7%" (p. 8, ref. [10]). Type: other/normalization.
- Calorimeter calibration (hadron response, mainly low-energy particles): "The major sources of systematic uncertainty on the differential cross section measurement are determination of the integrated luminosity, calorimeter calibration, and our model of parton fragmentation into jets." (p. 8). Type: syst.
- Parton fragmentation (HERWIG versions): "Fragmentation was studied by comparing different versions of the shower Monte Carlo HERWIG fragmentation scheme." (p. 8). Type: syst.
- Total measurement systematic: "The sum in quadrature of all the contributions ranges from 33% at M_jj = 140 GeV/c2 to 60% at 1000 GeV/c2." (p. 8). Type: syst (measurement; not stated to apply to Table 2 limits).
- LO theory: "The sum in quadrature of these theoretical uncertainties is 14% for M_jj > 500 GeV/c2 and is 36% at M_jj = 140 GeV/c2." (p. 8). Type: theory.
- Structure function and renormalization-scale dependence: used as a choice of PDFs/scales (HMRS-B, MT-B2, μ = pT/2) in the limit (pp. 10-11); no uncertainty value given.

Statistical uncertainty on the limits: not stated numerically.

## Caveats
- Limits are on σ × BR into light quarks with the Breit-Wigner's S defined inside acceptance (|η| < 0.7), i.e. an acceptance-folded quantity; the paper does not state an acceptance factor separately (p. 10).
- Resonance treatment is simplified: incoherent addition to QCD, Breit-Wigner not folded with structure functions, no spin effect (p. 10). Smearing function includes radiation from scattered partons (p. 10).
- Top-quark decays: "we set limits on resonant cross sections times the branching ratio into light quarks (u,d,s,c,b)" because the smearing "depends strongly on the unknown top mass" (p. 10).
- Dijet analysis uses cone R = 1 and the jet energy is the scalar sum of tower energies; the out-of-cone and underlying-event corrections are not applied to the data (p. 7).
- Table 2 masses span 200-900 GeV/c², widths only 0.02, 0.1, 0.2 M0. Table 3 axigluon windows are mass ranges, not cross-section limits.
- Axigluon results depend on structure-function choice (HMRS-B vs MT-B2); the paper gives both (Table 3).
- Paper is 1993, CDF "1988-1989 run" (p.6) [verifier w2_4: "Run 0" removed], so limits are historical; modern LHC/Tevatron results supersede them for most of the mass range (not checked, not in scope).

HEPData contains no limit table and no numeric values for this paper; the HEPData abstract refers to "Abe et al., PR D48 (1993) 998" for numerical values. No HEPData rows were copied.
2. Tables 2 and 3 are scanned images with an OCR text layer (pdftotext -layout returns them on PDF pp. 18-19) [verifier w2_4]; values were transcribed from the page images and checked by the verifier against the page images (all 15 Table 2 rows and Table 3 match). Verify against the published PRL before use.
3. Table 3 vs text conflict for axigluons, N = 10: Table 3 HMRS-B lower edge is 220 GeV/c²; the abstract and text p. 11 say 240 ≤ Ma ≤ 640 GeV/c² is excluded for N = 10. Not resolved.
4. Table 3 vs text conflict for N = 20: text p. 11 gives windows 260 ≤ Ma ≤ 280 and 450 ≤ Ma ≤ 550; Table 3 gives HMRS-B 240-330 and 450-550, and MT B2 260-280 and 420-580. The text's "windows" match neither row exactly. Not resolved.
5. Table 2 caption says "observable cross sections"; the paper states no expected limit and no explicit statement of observed vs expected. The text "CDF data exclude the region above the listed cross sections at the 95% CL" is the only basis for labelling them observed.
6. Table 1 (compositeness Λc limits, Λ 1.3 TeV most conservative) is an indirect contact-interaction bound and was excluded under the "direct searches only" scope. Not recorded.
7. Fig. 1 is figure-only; no values read from plots. Fig. 1 contains curves for axigluon masses 300 and 700 GeV/c² and a 1300 GeV contact-term curve.
8. Reference frame and polarization quantities: none defined in the paper (no angular distribution is used). Recorded as "not stated".
9. Locations use printed page numbers. For the body text, printed page = PDF page − 2 (PDF pp. 8-20 = printed pp. 6-18). Fig. 1 is on PDF p. 21 and has no printed number.
10. Source: the PDF was taken from the INSPIRE document link (file size 610022 bytes, identical to the Fermilab-hosted copy). No arXiv version exists.
