# hep-ex/0301027 — Measurement of W Polarisation at LEP

- L3 Collaboration (author list in paper; first author not transcribed), 2003, preprint (arXiv v1, 16 Jan 2003; paper header: "Submitted to Phys. Lett. B"; published journal reference not verified), INSPIRE recid 606066, HEPData: none (file `.cache/hep-ex_0301027/hepdata/NONE`: "no HEPData record")
- sqrt(s): 182.7–205.9 GeV averaged per energy point (Table 1); text: "centre-of-mass energies s = 183 − 209 GeV" (Sec. Data and Monte Carlo). Tables 2–4 are at average sqrt(s) = 196.7 GeV (Sec. Results). Integrated luminosity: 684.8 pb^-1 (Sec. Data and Monte Carlo; Table 1 per-energy values sum to the same total as listed). Data (L3 detector, whole data set; "supersedes our previous results [3] based on about one third of the data", Sec. Introduction).
- Topology / final state: e+e− → W+W− → ℓν qq̄′ (ℓ = e or μ), semileptonic; leptonic and hadronic W decays analysed separately, then combined.
- Selection summary:
  - Exactly one electron (momentum > 20 GeV) or one muon (momentum > 15 GeV) candidate [verifier w3_2: source says momentum, not p_T].
  - Neutrino = missing momentum; |p_ν| > 10 GeV and |cos θ_ν| < 0.95; m(ℓν) > 60 GeV.
  - Hadronic-system mass between 50 and 110 GeV.
  - Overall: 2010 events selected, efficiency 65.7%, purity 96.3% (Sec. Selection). No jet algorithm or R (not applicable: all non-lepton calorimeter energy assigned to hadronic W).
  - Analysis: binned fit to corrected decay-angle distributions, free parameters f− and f0 (f+ from unitarity); bias correction from EEWW MC.
- Model or observable definition (as stated in the paper):
  - Helicity fractions f−, f+, f0 are for the **W−** boson; "Assuming CP invariance these equal the fractions of the corresponding helicity states + 1, − 1 and 0 of the W+" (Sec. Introduction). Tables 2–4 caption: "All the helicities are converted to W− parameters using CP invariance."
  - Leptonic decays, Eq. (1): (1/N) dN/d cos θ*_ℓ = f− (3/8)(1+cos θ*_ℓ)² + f+ (3/8)(1−cos θ*_ℓ)² + f0 (3/4) sin² θ*_ℓ. θ*_ℓ = angle between the charged lepton and the W direction in the W rest frame. Rest frame calculated from lepton and neutrino momenta.
  - Hadronic decays, Eq. (2): (1/N) dN/d|cos θ*_q| = f± (3/4)(1+|cos θ*_q|²) + f0 (3/2)(1−|cos θ*_q|²), with f± = f+ + f−. θ*_q approximated by the polar angle of the thrust axis with respect to the W direction in the rest frame of the hadronic W. Sign of the quark charge not reconstructed, hence |cos θ*_q|.
  - f0 is the quantity called "longitudinal" (the paper's abstract: "fraction of longitudinally polarised W bosons"). The paper does not define f_L, A_0 or ρ_00.
  - Reference frame: W rest frame for helicity angles (paper's words: "decay angles... in the W rest frame"); the paper does not name the quantization axis explicitly as "helicity". Production angle Θ_W− is in the laboratory frame (Sec. Results).
  - Level: detector-level decay-angle distributions corrected for selection efficiency (KORALW MC) and for resolution-induced bias (EEWW MC, bias function linear in the ratio of other fractions). The paper does not state a particle-level (generator-level) unfolded definition.
  - Measured vs projected: measured on data (684.8 pb^-1). SM comparison from KORALW MC (not a projection).

## Caveats

- Bias correction (resolution migration): "These results include a bias correction of 0.005 on f 0 for leptonic decays and 0.044 for hadronic decays." (Sec. Results). Table 5 lists a systematic of 0.006 (lep) and 0.011 (had) on the bias correction; these are different quantities and must not be conflated.
- Bias correction adds statistical uncertainty: "The bias correction adds 0.003 to the statistical uncertainty on f 0 for leptonic decays and 0.007 to the one for hadronic decays." (Sec. Results).
- Hadronic f0 uses the leptonic ratio: "For the correction of f 0 in the hadronic W decay, the ratio f − / f + is taken from the measurement in the leptonic W decay, as only the sum of f + and f − is known from hadronic decays." (Sec. Analysis of the W helicity states). This makes the hadronic f0 not fully independent of the leptonic measurement.
- Correlation: "The parameters f − and f 0 derived from the fit are about 90% anti-correlated." (Sec. Results). Correlations between f0 values in the tables are not quoted.
- Born-level formulas: "Therefore the Born level formulæ (1) and (2) are applicable after radiative corrections." (Sec. Data and Monte Carlo), based on an EEWW vs YFSWW/KORALW comparison; the paper gives no quantitative radiative-correction uncertainty.
- Energy dependence: the SM f0 falls with sqrt(s) (0.271 at 183 GeV to 0.223 at 206 GeV, MC); the measurements in Tables 2–4 are combined over 183–209 GeV, so they are not per-energy results.
- No p_T dependence: the only differential variable is the W production angle Θ_W− (cos Θ_W− bins, Table 6). No p_T binning exists in this paper; the question asked for p_T "where available", so none is reported.
- Consistency with SM: "The measured W helicity fractions agree with the Standard Model expectations for the leptonic and hadronic decays, as well as for the combined sample." (Sec. Results). The paper's own statement; this extraction did not test the agreement numerically.
- Significance is stated as seven standard deviations (Sec. Results).
