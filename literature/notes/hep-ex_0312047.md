# hep-ex/0312047 — W Boson Polarisation at LEP2

- Authors/Collaboration: OPAL Collaboration (G. Abbiendi et al.), preprint CERN-EP-2003-088, arXiv 17 Dec 2003. Journal ref: Phys. Lett. B 585 (2004) 223-236 (per HEPData record; the PDF cover reads "To be submitted to Physics Letters B"). INSPIRE recid 635790. HEPData record 49660 (ins635790, version 1, 7 tables).
- sqrt(s): eight nominal points 183, 189, 192, 196, 200, 202, 205, 207 GeV; luminosity-weighted mean sqrt(s) 182.7 to 206.6 GeV (paper Table 1). Abstract says "between 183 GeV and 209 GeV" (see Open issues). Integrated luminosity 678.5 pb^-1 (1997-2000 data). Data only; KandY MC used for detector correction and as a generator-level comparison.
- Topology / final state: e+e- -> W+W- -> q qbar' l nu (semileptonic). Lepton l = e, mu or tau. Leptonic and hadronic W decays analysed separately, then combined (BLUE). Off-diagonal SDM elements use leptonic decays only (Sec. 4.1).
- Selection summary:
  - Four stages: pre-selection, likelihood selection, kinematic fit, final selection (Sec. 4.1). 4176 observed events (paper Table 1).
  - Overall selection efficiency 78-81%, purity 93-95% (Sec. 4.2).
  - Events binned in 8 equal-width cos(theta_W) bins per sqrt(s); detector correction p/eps (Eqs. 22-23, Table 2); bias correction (Sec. 4.3, Eq. 24).
  - Jet algorithm and R: not stated in this paper (refers to refs. [6,22,24]). pT / mass / |y| ranges: not applicable, the analysis is differential in cos(theta_W) and has no pT dependence.
- Model or observable definition (frame and convention):
  - Helicity amplitudes F^(lambda,lambda') give rho_{tau tau'} for the W- (Eq. 1). Frame: helicity axes in the W rest frame, z* along the boost direction from the lab frame, y* along e-bar x b (footnote 1). theta_W is the polar angle of the W- relative to the electron beam (Sec. 1).
  - Diagonal elements: rho_{++}, rho_{--}, rho_{00}, with rho_{++}+rho_{--}+rho_{00}=1 (Fig. 1 caption). Leptonic decays measure rho_{++} and rho_{--} separately; hadronic decays fold them, so only their sum is available (Sec. 2.3).
  - Longitudinal and transverse cross sections: dsigma_L/dcos(theta_W) = rho_00 dsigma/dcos(theta_W) (Eq. 10); dsigma_T/dcos(theta_W) = (rho_++ + rho_--) dsigma/dcos(theta_W) (Eq. 11).
  - The longitudinal fraction f_L is referred to as the "fraction of longitudinally polarised W bosons". No explicit formula for the integrated fraction is given in the text read: not stated (see Open issues).
  - CP-odd and CPT-odd observables are built from imaginary parts of off-diagonal elements, Eqs. 14-21: Delta^CP_{+-,+0,-0} = sigma^{W-} - sigma^{W+}; Delta^CPT = sigma^{W-} + sigma^{W+}. Individual Im rho elements are not reported.

## Caveats

- Statistical errors include the bias-correction uncertainty: "are included in the statistical errors of the results in section 6" (Sec. 4.3). The bias shifts (Table 3) are of comparable size to the errors.
- Diagonal estimators are not constrained to [0,1] (Sec. 4.1). rho_++ + rho_-- + rho_00 = 1 is imposed by construction (Fig. 1 caption), so the three diagonal elements are not independent.
- Correlations: Sec. 4.1 states the estimators in different cos(theta_W) bins are uncorrelated, but Sec. 4.2 reports bin-to-bin migration correlations "as high as 40%" in neighbouring bins from full unfolding studies. Treat cos(theta_W) bin errors as not independent.
- Combination: BLUE with systematic sources treated as 100% or 0% correlated between decay modes and energies (Sec. 6.1). The statistical correlation between decay modes is stated as below 10%.
- The 183 and 189 GeV values "differ slightly from those previously published by OPAL" (Sec. 6.1), due to KandY MC, bias correction and reconstruction changes.
- Frame and definitions are those of this paper; the paper compares to the L3 result (ref. 4) for f_L but no numbers from L3 are copied here.
- The CP-odd and CPT-odd observables are model-independent asymmetries; the text notes they are consistent with zero at tree level (Sec. 6.2).

## Open issues

1. Energy range: abstract and HEPData abstract say 183-209 GeV, but the data table maxima are 206.6 GeV mean and 207 GeV nominal (Sec. 3.1, Table 1). Not resolved from this paper.
2. Integrated formula for f_L is not written in the text read. Eq. 10 gives the differential relation dsigma_L/dcos(theta_W) = rho_00 dsigma/dcos(theta_W); whether the integrated fraction equals the luminosity-weighted rho_00 average is an inference, not a stated result.
3. HEPData Tables 5-7 have no units in their headers. Text (Fig. 3 caption, Table 6 caption) gives picobarns. Table 4 headers say [PB].
4. HEPData Tables 5-7 have no integrated (all-cos) row. The integrated Delta values exist only in the text (Table 6) and are copied from it.
5. Individual Im rho_{tau tau'} elements are not reported. Only the CP-odd and CPT-odd combinations (Eqs. 14-21) are measured, so per-element Im values, if any, would be in the supplementary material (ref. [31]). That material was not accessed; only the local HEPData copy was used.
6. Jet algorithm and R: not stated in this paper.
7. Figure values: none are figure-only. Fig. 1 and Fig. 2 values are in HEPData Tables 2-4. Fig. 3 values are in HEPData Tables 5-7.
8. The MC column of Table 5 (text) and the Table 3 bias shifts are text-only and were transcribed from the rendered PDF pages, not from a machine-readable source. Their values were read visually and should be rechecked against the source before use.
9. The full text was taken from the arXiv PDF (CERN-EP-2003-088, 20-page PDF). The HTML version was not checked, as this 2003 preprint has no arXiv HTML.
