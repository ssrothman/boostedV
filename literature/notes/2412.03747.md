# 2412.03747 — Model-agnostic search for dijet resonances with anomalous jet substructure in pp collisions at √s = 13 TeV

- Collaboration: CMS (analysis note EXO-22-026). arXiv v2 (hep-ex), submitted 2024-12-04, revised 2025-06-03. Journal: Rept. Prog. Phys. 88 (2025) 067802, DOI 10.1088/1361-6633/add762 (INSPIRE). INSPIRE recid 2856408. HEPData record ins2856408 (DOI 10.17182/hepdata.156054.v1), 11 tables, all used.
- Full text used: cached arXiv HTML `.cache/2412.03747/arxiv_html.html` and extracted `fulltext.txt` (both already present; not re-downloaded). Numbers are taken from HEPData JSON in `.cache/2412.03747/hepdata/tables/`.
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 138 fb^-1 (2016–2018). Data. Signal shapes and efficiencies from simulation (MadGraph5_aMC@NLO LO signal).
- Topology / final state: dijet final state, A → B C, with B and C each decaying to a large-radius jet with multi-prong substructure. Benchmarks: Q* → q W′→3q (1+2 prong), X → Y Y′→4q (2+2), W′ → B′ t → bZt (3+3), W_KK → R W → 3W (2+4), Z′ → T′T′ → tZtZ (5+5, text only), G_KK → HH → 4t (6+6). Simulated signals are not restricted to hadronic SM decays (text: "we did not enforce hadronic-only decays of SM particles in the signal simulation").
- Selection summary:
  - Jets: anti-k_t, R = 0.8, particle-flow candidates, pileup-per-particle mitigation.
  - Two leading jets with pT > 300 GeV and |η| < 2.5; m_jj > 1455 GeV; |Δη_jj| < 1.3.
  - Triggers: leading-jet pT (450 GeV in 2016 to 500 GeV in 2018) or H_T (800 to 1050 GeV) triggers.
  - Anomaly-score selections per method (VAE-QR: 10% most anomalous for the generic search; 1%, 5–1%, 10–5% categories used only in limit setting for known signals).
  - Resonance mass scan 1.8–6 TeV in 100 GeV steps (fit range); CATHODE/CATHODE-b model-independent limits in HEPData go to 5 or 5.8 TeV (see Quoted results).
- Model or observable definition (frame/convention):
  - Observable: dijet invariant mass m_jj of the two leading jets, fit with a double-Crystal-Ball signal shape (from simulated narrow-width resonances) on a free-floating background parameterization (Eq. 1 of the paper).
  - Limit: 95% CL upper limit on the signal cross section (fb), profile-likelihood test statistic with CL_s and asymptotic formulae (Combine). For weakly supervised methods the efficiency depends on sigma_sig; N_sig = sigma L A eps(sigma) (paper Eq. 2) is inverted numerically.
  - Whether sigma includes branching fractions of the benchmark decays: not stated.
  - Polarization / reference frame: not applicable (no polarization observable).
  - Spin: stated only for G_KK (spin-2 graviton, text). Q*, X, W′, W_KK, Z′ spins: not stated.
  - Scope: 1.8–6 TeV is entirely above the 10 GeV–1 TeV primary window. Direct limits only.

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

Section "Systematic uncertainties" (and Appendix "Study of potential fit bias" / limit-setting appendix):
- Signal selection efficiency, dominant: jet substructure modeling correction (Lund-plane reclustering, ref. [97]); "The resulting uncertainties in the selection efficiency range from 10–25%, increasing for signal models with larger numbers of prongs." Stat/syst classification: not stated beyond "uncertainties".
- b-tagging of subjets (signals producing b jets): "an uncertainty of up to ~5% in the tagging efficiency of signals which produce b jets."
- Signal shape: jet energy scale and resolution; "The uncertainty in the location (width) of the signal mass peak is found to be ~2% (~4%)." Gaussian nuisances in the likelihood.
- Weakly supervised methods, extra: "an additional uncertainty in the selection efficiency of up to ~10% due to stochasticity of their training performance in data."
- Limit-setting appendix (weakly supervised efficiency): "The dominant source arises from the correction to signal modeling based on the Lund plane [97], followed by the stochasticity of signal sampling for the injection." "Other modeling and experimental uncertainties were found to be small." All added in quadrature.
- Other selection-efficiency sources (pileup, PDFs, renormalization/factorization scales): stated to be small; no numbers in text.
- Background shape: free-floating parameters constrained by the data in the signal region ("This allows for the uncertainty in the background shape to be correctly accounted for in all reported results.").
- Luminosity uncertainty: not stated in the extracted text. Theory uncertainties on signal cross sections: not stated.

## Caveats

- Mass range: all results are for m_A between 1.8 and 6 TeV (outside the 10 GeV–1 TeV primary window). Signal-specific benchmark limits exist only at m_A = 3 and 5 TeV.
- Daughter masses for signal-specific benchmarks: 25, 80, 170, 400 GeV (with some combinations absent). The paper states this in Sec. "Signal models".
- Observed vs expected: observed and expected are both reported in HEPData. The observed limit is above the expected one for several points and below it for others; no significance is quoted for the limits.
- The paper reports no significant excess. Largest local excesses (not limits): "The largest excesses of events seen by the CATHODE, CATHODE-b, QUAK, and VAE-QR methods had local significances of 2.2, 2.9, 2.6, and 2.3 sigma at resonance masses of 2.3, 2.3, 4.7, and 4.9 TeV, respectively." The TNT and CWoLa Hunting methods did not report any excess above 1.5 sigma.
- Generic QUAK limit is not independent of the benchmarks: "these benchmark signals are included in the signal prior of the generic QUAK method." The paper says removing one signal from the prior degrades its sensitivity by ~30%.
- Limits are on cross sections (fb) for specific benchmark kinematics, not on couplings. The model-independent tables are for a generic signal shape and are not tied to a model.
- Zero values stored in HEPData (`0.0 / 0.0`), not verified as physical and not discussed in the text: X→YY′ (5000; 25, 400 and 5000; 80, 80) QUAK; W_KK→RW (5000; 170) QUAK. Treat as suspicious.
- CATHODE and CATHODE-b model-independent limits end at 5000 GeV (`.nan` from 5100 GeV); the other four model-independent tables extend to 5800 GeV.
- Some signal-specific entries are `.nan` (no value stored): Q* with m_A = 3000 GeV and TNT, for daughter masses 25, 80 and 170 GeV.
- The paper's improvement-factor table (ratios to the inclusive limit) is not transcribed; it is not a limit.

- Paper table numbers: the HEPData location says "Tables 1 and 2, located on Pages 19 and 20". The extracted text does not show the printed table numbers; the text table captions were located by content, not by number. The section numbers in the extracted text ("0.x") do not match the paper and were not used.
- Z′ → T′T′ limit is text-only (3 TeV, TNT, 400 GeV daughters) with rounded values; no HEPData entry. The 5 TeV Z′ entry is absent.
- The model-independent tables are supplementary HEPData material; the paper body does not describe their construction (only the HEPData description and header).
- Whether the signal-specific cross-section limits include branching fractions: not stated.
- Luminosity and theory uncertainties on signal: not stated in the text.
- The expected 68% bands stored in HEPData (`asymerror`) were not transcribed.
- Zero-valued HEPData entries (see Caveats) were not checked against the paper's figures; they are copied as stored.
