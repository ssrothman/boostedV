# 2510.21641 — Search for dijet resonances with data scouting in proton-proton collisions at √s = 13 TeV

- Authors/Collaboration: CMS Collaboration (CMS-EXO-23-004, CERN-EP-2025-212); year 2026; journal ref JHEP 04 (2026) 133, DOI 10.1007/JHEP04(2026)133 (INSPIRE); preprint arXiv:2510.21641 (v2, 30 Apr 2026 in the cached HTML); INSPIRE recid 3073143; HEPData record ins3073143 (DOI 10.17182/hepdata.159438.v1; tables t5–t8).
- √s = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 117 fb⁻¹ (2016–2018, abstract); measured data (not projection).
- Topology / final state: narrow s-channel resonance decaying to two partons, reconstructed as a dijet (qq, qg, gg categories); pp → jj X. The paper searches for "narrow s-channel dijet resonances" (Sec. 0.1); no pair or associated-production (ISR, W, Z, top) search is described. Analysis range 0.6–1.8 TeV: no result below 600 GeV, so the 10 GeV–600 GeV part of the question is not covered.
- Selection summary:
  - Trigger: online Calo-jets (anti-kT, R = 0.4, Sec. 0.2) with pT > 40 GeV enter H_T; H_T > 250 GeV scouting trigger. Fully efficient for m_jj ≳ 0.5 TeV (Sec. 0.2, Fig. 1).
  - Offline jets: pT > 30 GeV, |η| < 2.5, jet ID (Sec. 0.3). The offline jet algorithm and R are not stated in Sec. 0.3.
  - Wide jets: the two leading jets seed; other jets within ΔR < 1.1 and pT > 30 GeV are added to the nearest leading jet; m_jj is the invariant mass of the two wide jets (Sec. 0.3).
  - |Δη| < 1.3 between the two wide jets (Sec. 0.3).
  - 2017/2018 Calo-jet corrections were not verified in data; scale factors are applied to the 2017 and 2018 dijet-mass spectra (Sec. 0.3).
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Model-independent 95% CL upper limit on σ×B×A (pb) for narrow qq, qg and gg resonances (Sec. 0.6, Fig. 4, HEPData t5–t7). A is the parton-level acceptance of the minimum dijet-mass requirement; "In the case of isotropic decays, the acceptance is A≈0.6 and is independent of the resonance mass" (Sec. 0.6 text).
  - Leptophobic Z′ coupling to quarks only: 95% CL upper limit on the universal quark coupling g_q′ vs resonance mass (Sec. 0.7, Fig. 6, HEPData t8). Sec. 0.1 states that for M_DM > M_med/2 the DM-mediator dijet cross section equals that of this leptophobic Z′ model.
  - Spin/parity: not stated for the model-independent limits (narrow s-channel resonance). Polarization / reference frame: not applicable (no polarization observable).
  - Particle vs detector level: the mjj spectrum is at detector (reconstructed Calo-jet) level; the limit is compared to parton-level predictions with A applied (Sec. 0.6).
  - Confidence level: observed and expected 95% CL, CLs with the asymptotic approximation (Sec. 0.5).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Background-function parameters, left floating in the fit: "The background systematic uncertainties are related to the parameters of the functional forms utilized, that were left unconstrained in the fit, and are by far the dominant ones in these searches." (Sec. 0.5, Statistical procedure). No numerical contribution is given.
- Jet energy scale: "The uncertainty in the JES is within 2% for all values of the dijet mass" (Sec. 0.5); "shifting the dijet mass shape for the signal by ±2%". Gaussian constraint (Sec. 0.5).
- Jet energy resolution: "an uncertainty of 10% in the resolution of the dijet mass" (Sec. 0.5); propagated by increasing and decreasing the reconstructed signal width by 10%. Gaussian constraint.
- Integrated luminosity: "The uncertainties in the integrated luminosities are 1.2% in 2016 [58], 2.3% in 2017 [59], and 2.5% in 2018 [60]" (Sec. 0.5); applied to signal normalization, log-normal, correlated across years.
- Stat vs syst split of the limits: not provided in HEPData or the text.
- Fit-bias test (Sec. 0.4, Signal and background modeling): bias from background-function choice is "much smaller than the combined statistical and systematic uncertainty". Not quantified.

## Caveats

- Limits are on σ×B×A per final state, not on σ alone. Sec. 0.6: "The acceptance of the minimum dijet mass requirement for each considered signal has been evaluated separately for each final state and has been taken into account by correcting the limits. The acceptance is evaluated at the parton level" [lead, from T1 finding R49: the earlier "is not part of A" is not stated in the source]. Model comparison uses lowest-order parton-level predictions (Sec. 0.6).
- Dijet-mass resolution (Gaussian core, Sec. 0.4): "varies from 14.0 (10.5)% at a resonance mass of 0.6 TeV to 8.2 (6.7)% at that mass of 1.8 TeV", with the gg value first and qq in parentheses.
- Coupling sensitivity: the text says "For the first time, CMS demonstrates sensitivity to coupling values as small as 0.04" (Sec. 0.7). The paper does not state how 0.04 is obtained. In HEPData, the observed g_q′ minimum is 0.037342 at 1000 GeV (t8); this is also the minimum over the full 600–1800 GeV table. The text value is not equal to this table value, so use the table value.
- Observed g_q′ outside the primary window: 0.044438 at 1050 GeV, 0.077324 at 1500 GeV, 0.103667 at 1800 GeV (t8), see the outside-window rows.
- Local significance: "The largest local significance is observed to be 2.2 standard deviations at a quark-quark resonance mass of 0.8 TeV" (Sec. 0.8). This is a local significance, not a limit.
- Fig. 4 benchmark curves use g_q = 0.25, g_DM = 1 and M_DM = 1 GeV (Fig. 4 caption). Mass limits from those curves are figure-only and not extracted.
- Fig. 6 limit is for a leptophobic Z′ coupling to quarks only (Fig. 6 caption).

## Open issues

- HEPData labels all four tables as "Data from Figure 4" or "Figure 6", but the paper states that tabulated results are in the HEPData record. The values are used as HEPData gives them; the paper does not separately tabulate them.
- The observed limits have no uncertainty in HEPData (errors hidden). The stat/syst split of the observed limits is not stated anywhere I found.
- HEPData asymerror convention (absolute offsets or relative; units) is not stated in the record. Values are copied as given, not converted.
- Units of g_q′ (t8) are not stated in the HEPData header; the note does not assign one.
- Spin/parity of the model-independent limits is not stated.
- The 10 GeV–600 GeV part of the question's range is not covered by this paper (analysis starts at 600 GeV).
