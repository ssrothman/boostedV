# 2002.11325 — Search for dijet resonances in events with an isolated charged lepton using √s = 13 TeV pp data collected by the ATLAS detector

- ATLAS Collaboration, 2020, JHEP 06 (2020) 151 (DOI 10.1007/JHEP06(2020)151); arXiv:2002.11325 [hep-ex] v2, CERN-EP-2019-276; INSPIRE recid 1782373; HEPData record ins1782373 (record 94787, DOI 10.17182/hepdata.94787.v1, status finished, 12 data tables; all tables read from the local prefetch).
- √s = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 139 fb⁻¹ (Run 2, 2015–2018), 1.7% uncertainty (Sec. 3). Data, with MC signal shapes (Sec. 4).
- Topology / final state: pp → two leading jets (dijet mass m_jj) + one isolated e or μ with p_T ≥ 60 GeV. Each model has the resonance produced with a leptonic W (or, for tbH⁺, H⁺ → tb with a leptonic W from the top decay). See Open issues, item 7.
- Selection summary:
  - Lepton: isolated e (E_T > 7 GeV, tight ID) or μ (p_T ≥ 7 GeV, medium); signal region needs p_T^ℓ ≥ 60 GeV (Sec. 3).
  - Jets: anti-k_t, R = 0.4, topological clusters, p_T ≥ 20 GeV, |η| ≤ 2.4, JVT for p_T ≤ 60 GeV (Sec. 3). Dijet mass from the two highest-p_T jets, m_jj ≥ 0.22 TeV (Sec. 3).
  - Background: five-parameter fit f(x) = p1(1−x)^p2 x^(p3+p4 ln x+p5 ln²x), x = m_jj/√s (Eq. 1; Sec. 5). Background-only fit range 216 GeV–6.3 TeV (Sec. 5).
  - Limits: Bayesian (BAT), flat prior on signal yield, 95% credibility level, expected bands from pseudo-data (Sec. 5).
  - Acceptance "typically 40%" at the lowest mass, "60–90%" at the highest; efficiency "65–75%" (Sec. 5). Not per-point values.
- Model or observable definition (frame/convention):
  - Generic Gaussian resonance X, width σ_X/m_X = 0, 0.05, 0.10, 0.15 (Fig. 4 caption; Sec. 5, Sec. 7). Limit quantity: σ × A × ε × B (Sec. 5; Fig. 4 caption; HEPData Tables 5–8 description).
  - W′→Z′W±→ℓνq q̄ (SSM-like): BR(W′→WZ′) = 0.5, m(W′) − m(Z′) = 250 GeV, Z′ relative width 3.2% (Sec. 4).
  - ρ_T→π_T W±→ℓνq q̄ (technicolor): m(ρ_T) = 2 m(π_T); signal width ≈ 15% of m(π_T) (Sec. 4).
  - tbH⁺ (2HDM type-2, m_h^mod− scenario, four-flavour): HEPData Table 3 states no tanβ [verifier w2_1; lead checked the Table 3 description and headers]; HEPData Table 11 (truth-level acceptance) says the acceptance was computed for tanβ = 1; the text ties the 1.2 TeV exclusion to tanβ = 0.5, and Fig. 5(c) shows both. H⁺ → t b̄ assumed; narrow-width approximation (Sec. 4).
  - Simplified DM, axial-vector mediator Z′ (Sec. 4), leptophobic couplings g_q = 0.25, g_ℓ = 0, g_DM = 1. No coupling scan (Sec. 7.1).
  - Reference frame and polarization quantities: not stated. The paper measures no angular or polarization observable; the only polarization statement is "effects related to W boson polarisation ... are not included" (Sec. 4, tbH⁺).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- JES and JER dominate the total systematic uncertainty for m_jj ≲ 2 TeV; background-description uncertainties become comparable above 2 TeV (Sec. 6). Quote: "The uncertainty associated with the JES and JER dominates the total systematic uncertainty for" (text continues with m_jj ≲ 2 TeV). JES/JER shift dijet masses by as much as ±1.4% (Sec. 6).
- Combined systematics: "The combined effect from all systematic uncertainties leads to a 6% increase in the limits relative to the limits without uncertainties." (Sec. 6). The paper says this is "typically within the 1σ band around the expected limits" (Sec. 6).
- Lepton trigger, identification and reconstruction: constant 1% (Sec. 6).
- PDF-induced signal-shape uncertainty: 1% on the limits (Sec. 6). Quote: "Such shape-related systematic effects on the m_jj distribution are accounted for by assigning a 1% PDF uncertainty to the calculated limits."
- Luminosity: 1.7% (Sec. 3; Sec. 6).
- Background shape: alternative fit function (p5 ln²x replaced by p5/√x) and signal-injection/closure tests (Sec. 5; Sec. 6). Closure-test yields "significantly smaller than the statistical uncertainty" (Sec. 5), treated as a systematic.
- Lepton energy-scale and trigger-shape effects: negligible (Sec. 6).
- Stat vs syst split of the limits: not stated.

## Caveats

- Two different limit quantities are both labelled "σ×B" in the paper. Gaussian limits (Tables 5–8) are on σ×A×ε×B (Sec. 5; Fig. 4 caption). Model limits (Tables 1–4) are "corrected by the acceptance and efficiency" (Sec. 5; Sec. 7.1). Do not compare the two directly. For Tables 1–2 the HEPData descriptions say "times acceptance times branching ratio"; for Tables 3–4 they say "times branching ratio". See Open issue 2.
- Mass window: the search range is 0.22 < m_jj < 6.3 TeV (INSPIRE abstract; Conclusion), and the Gaussian limits are quoted from 0.25 to 6 TeV (Sec. 7). The Q1 primary window (10 GeV–1 TeV) overlaps this paper only from 0.25 to 1 TeV. No limit is given below 0.25 TeV.
- Confidence level: a Bayesian 95% credibility level with a flat prior on the signal yield (Sec. 5), not a frequentist CLs limit. Quote: "The resulting likelihood function is multiplied by a flat prior to give a posterior probability density."
- Systematics are included in all limits: "All calculated limits account for the previously discussed systematic uncertainties." (Sec. 7.1). Observed and expected are both in HEPData for all eight tables.
- Gaussian limits: the paper states the oscillations of the observed limits for wide signals are due to correlations between points (Sec. 7). The Gaussian signal production topology is not stated; the lepton selection is applied to the Gaussian signal as well.
- Model exclusions (Sec. 7.1) use signal shapes from MC: W′, ρ_T at full simulation; tbH⁺ and DM at ATLFAST-II (Sec. 4).
- The DM model has no coupling scan; the exclusion is a Z′ mass bound at g_q = 0.25, g_ℓ = 0, g_DM = 1 (Sec. 7.1). No g_q limit is given in the paper.
- Truth-level HEPData Tables 9–12 (σ, A, ε, σ_vis per mass for ρ_T, SSM, H⁺ at tanβ = 1, DM) exist and were not extracted, since they are inputs, not limits (out of Q1 scope). Their statistical-only uncertainties are stated in their descriptions.

## Open issues

1. Mass-axis definition is not stated in the HEPData headers for Tables 1–4 ("Mass [GeV]"). The text suggests Table 1 is m(π_T) ("values of m_πT below 350 GeV are excluded"), Table 2 is m(Z′) ("Z′ masses up to 2 TeV"), Table 3 is m(H⁺), and Table 4 is m(Z′) ("Z′ masses ... below 1.2 TeV"). This is not confirmed by a table label. Needs the paper's Fig. 5 axis labels.
2. Limit quantity: HEPData Table 1 and Table 2 descriptions say "cross-section times acceptance times branching ratio". Table 3 and Table 4 descriptions and the Fig. 5 caption say "σ × BR" (text: "cross-section (σ) times branching ratio (B)"), while Sec. 7.1 says the model limits are corrected by acceptance and efficiency. Unresolved; the table headers themselves say "σ × BR [pb]" for all four.
3. Observed/expected for the model mass exclusions (350 GeV, 2 TeV, 1.2 TeV, 1.2 TeV) are not tabulated. The crossing with theory cross sections is figure-only, and HEPData has no theory cross-section table for these models. Only the text values are recorded.
4. [verifier w2_1, corrected by lead] HEPData Table 3 does not state tanβ; the "acceptance computed for tanβ = 1" statement is in Table 11 (truth-level). Which Fig. 5(c) curve Table 3 corresponds to is not stated.
5. Gaussian grid: the text says "fine steps in masses that correspond to the bin sizes times two" (Fig. 4 caption), but HEPData uses uniform 250 GeV steps for Tables 5–8. Tables 7–8 start at 500 GeV; the Fig. 4 caption excludes points below 0.3 TeV for widths 10% and 15%. Not resolved whether HEPData is a subsample of the plotted points.
6. The arXiv HTML text of the abstract and of Sec. 5 is truncated at "0.22" (math not rendered). The mass range is taken from the INSPIRE abstract ("0.22 < m_jj < 6.3 TeV") and from Sec. 3/Conclusion ("≥ 0.22 TeV"; "0.22–6.3 TeV"). The PDF was not read.
7. Scope judgment for the user: every model in this paper has the resonance produced in association with a leptonic W (W′→WZ′, ρ_T→π_T W±, DM Z′W) or with a top (tbH⁺, where the lepton comes from the W of the top decay). The question covers associated production with W and top, so these are included, but the H⁺ → tb case is not strictly a fully hadronic final state. The Gaussian limits are generic and do not state a production topology. Flagged, not excluded.
8. Spin/parity as stated: W′ and Z′ are "new heavy gauge bosons" (Sec. 1), the DM mediator is an "axial-vector mediator Z′" (Sec. 4), H⁺ is a charged Higgs of a 2HDM (Sec. 4). The spin of the ρ_T is not stated in the paper text. Gaussian limits are generic in width and do not assume a spin.
