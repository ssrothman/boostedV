# 2503.11317 — Evidence for longitudinally polarized W bosons in the electroweak production of same-sign W boson pairs in association with two jets in pp collisions at √s = 13 TeV with the ATLAS detector

- ATLAS Collaboration, 2025 (arXiv v2, 16 Sep 2025), Phys. Rev. Lett. 135 (2025) 111802, DOI 10.1103/bpln-ccql, CERN-EP-2025-048, STDM-2019-29. INSPIRE recid 2900453. HEPData record: none (prefetch marker `hepdata/NONE`: "no HEPData record").
- √s = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 140 fb⁻¹ (quoted in Abstract and Conclusions); data.
- Topology / final state: EW same-sign W±W±jj, two same-sign leptons (e or μ), missing transverse momentum, two jets (VBS topology). Fiducial measurement at particle level.
- Selection summary:
  - Leptons: p_T > 27 GeV, |η| < 2.5 (muons), |η| < 2.47 excluding 1.37–1.52 (electrons); m_ℓℓ > 20 GeV in SR; E_T^miss > 30 GeV in SR.
  - Jets: anti-k_t, R = 0.4, |η| < 4.5; leading p_T > 65 GeV, subleading p_T > 35 GeV.
  - Fiducial region (particle level, Results section): two leptons p_T > 27 GeV, |η| < 2.5, m_ℓℓ > 20 GeV; two jets with m_jj > 500 GeV and |Δy_jj| > 2.
  - Analysis: inclusive DNN, then signal DNNs separating W_L W_L jj from W_T W_T and W_L W_T jj; binned maximum-likelihood fit. No p_T binning of a polarization observable.
- Model or observable definition (frame/convention):
  - Polarization vectors defined in the W±W± centre-of-mass frame (Introduction; Fig. 3, Table 1, Table 3 captions). An alternative with the initial-state parton–parton CM frame is given in the Appendix (Table 5).
  - Polarization states are W_L±W_L±jj, W_L±W_T±jj, W_T±W_T±jj (polarized on-shell, Sherpa 3, NLO EW corrections). The paper defines fiducial cross sections σB(W_L W_L jj), σB(W_L W± jj), etc.
  - No polarization fraction (f_L, f_0, A_0, ρ_00 or equivalent) is defined or measured. Definition: not stated.

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Paper states (Systematic uncertainties paragraph, unnumbered, before Fig. 2): "none of the considered systematic uncertainties have a significant impact on the sensitivity of this result"; the largest are data-driven background statistics, theory modelling of polarized W±W±jj, and jet/E_T^miss scale and resolution. Quote: "The systematic uncertainties with the largest impact on the result are related to the finite number of data events used for data-driven background estimates, the theory uncertainties in the physics modeling of the polarized W ± W ± j j production, and uncertainties in the jet and E T miss scale and resolution."
- Table 4 (σB(W_L W± jj) measurement, fractional Δσ/σ in %): Data statistical 32.2; Background modeling statistical 7.2; Background, non-prompt 4.2; Jet energy and E_T^miss scale and resolution 3.9; Normalization factors 2.5; W±W±jj EW+QCD modelling 4.7; Experimental and modeling 11.1; Total 34.1. Values copied from the table as printed.

## Caveats

- No polarization fraction is measured. The paper's headline result is evidence (3.3 σ, Conclusions) for W_L± W± jj production with fiducial σB = 0.88 ± 0.30 fb, plus limits on W_L W_L jj. These are not f_L or f_0 measurements.
- The 10% and 30% numbers are SM predictions of cross-section shares, not measured fractions.
- Particle-level fiducial cross sections (Results section). Measured values are compared with Sherpa 3 predictions with NLO EW corrections and off-shell corrections (~10% in SR).
