# 1805.02780 — Constraints on a Light Leptophobic Mediator from LEP Data

- Authors: Manuel Drees, Zhongyi Zhang (Bonn). Year 2018 (submitted 7 May 2018, arXiv v3 11 Sep 2018). Journal ref: preprint (not verified; arXiv abstract page fetched shows none). INSPIRE recid 1672164. HEPData: none (`.cache/1805.02780/hepdata/NONE`: "no HEPData record").
- Nature of results: these are the authors' own recasts of published ALEPH LEP searches (refs [26,27] detector; [38] squark search for jj+MET; [50] LEP1 H-nu-nubar search; [55] ALEPH 183 GeV SM Higgs search for 4-jet). They are not ALEPH collaboration limits on R. Hadron level, no detector simulation (Sec. 3, "neglecting detector resolution effects").
- sqrt(s): 91 GeV (LEP1 jj+MET, Fig. 6); 183 GeV and 209 GeV (4-jet, Fig. 7); 208 GeV (jj+MET LEP2, Figs. 2-5; Sec. 4 projection). Integrated luminosity of the recast analyses: not stated in this paper. Data: published ALEPH cuts and published event counts (data-based recast). Sec. 4 is a projection with SM Monte Carlo background, not applied to data.
- Topology / final state:
  - (a) jj + MET: e+e- -> q qbar chi chibar, R off-shell and R -> chi chibar invisible. NOT a hadronic-resonance decay; recorded for completeness only.
  - (b) 4 jets: e+e- -> q qbar q' qbar' via real or virtual R, R -> q qbar. Hadronic; in scope.
- Selection summary (as stated by the paper):
  - Signal generation: MadGraph + FeynRules UFO + Pythia 8.2 hadronization (Sec. 3).
  - 4-jet (183 GeV, ALEPH [55]): "at least 2 b- jets, N_{\rm ch}>7 , \min(\cos\theta_{ij}+\cos\theta_{kl})<-1.3" (Sec. 3.2, location Sec. 3.2 footnote). Efficiency about 27% (Sec. 3.2).
  - jj+MET LEP2 (ALEPH squark cuts [38], Sec. 7): "The influential cuts are N_{\rm ch}>11 , M_{\rm vis}>15 GeV" (Sec. 3.1.1 footnote); the paper gives no efficiency for the squark cuts [38]; the "about 20%" efficiency sentence (paper.txt L552–557) belongs to the 161/172 GeV neutralino-search paragraph [39] [verifier wR_a].
  - jj+MET LEP1 (ALEPH [50]): Durham k_T jets; "The influential cuts are: N_{\rm ch}>7 , M_{\rm vis}<70 GeV" (Sec. 3.1.2 footnote).
  - Sec. 4 proposed jj+MET cuts (Table 1): "M_{\rm vis} >4 GeV", "p_{T}/E_{\rm vis} >20\%" — projection only, not applied to data.
- Model / observable definition:
  - Lagrangian (Eq. 4): R_mu qbar gamma^mu (g^V_q - g^A_q gamma^5) q + R_mu chibar gamma^mu (g^V_chi - g^A_chi gamma^5) chi. Spin-1 mediator R, Dirac DSP chi (Sec. 2.1). Frame/polarization quantities: not stated (no helicity fractions quoted).
  - Assumptions: g^V_u = g^V_d = 0 (Sec. 2.2); g^A_u = g^A_d = g^A_s = g^A_c = 0 (Sec. 2.2); equal vector couplings for s, c, b (Sec. 2.5); m_R < 2 m_chi (Sec. 2.5; chi-chibar R on-shell forbidden).
  - Bounds are on sqrt(g_q g_chi) (jj+MET, Sec. 3.1), on g^A_b and g^V_q (4-jet, Sec. 3.2), and on the total signal cross section (right frames of Figs. 2-8).
  - Perturbativity (Gamma_R < m_R is Eq. (12), Sec. 2.3; Eq. (14) is the coupling-sum condition) [verifier w2_5] and unitarity (Eq. 15, g^A_f m_f/m_R <= sqrt(pi/2)) are theory constraints, not experimental limits; they appear below as crossover statements.

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

No systematic uncertainty is quantified for any recast limit. Stated approximations only:
- Hadron level, detector resolution neglected: "We apply the cuts defining the relevant ALEPH analyses at the hadron level, neglecting detector resolution effects." (Sec. 3, intro.) The paper says detector effects are "probably smaller than the effects of ignoring detector smearing" for the jet-definition issue (Sec. 3.1.2).
- Jet algorithm (LEP1 jj+MET): "We found that the results differ slightly for different jet algorithms." (Sec. 3.1.2; Durham used.)
- Interference with SM gluon exchange neglected in 4-jet: "We checked explicitly for some combinations of parameters that the interference terms change the total cross section only by a few percent." (Sec. 3.2.)
- Coupling-set assumptions (Sec. 2.2, 2.5): setting g^V_u = g^V_d = 0 and light axial couplings to zero, and equal vector couplings for s, c, b, all change the bounds; results for other ratios are stated to rescale only for the jj+MET channel (Sec. 2.5).
- Sec. 4: "Although we do not include the detector simulation in the test of the background suppression" (Sec. 1). Sec. 4 says "we cannot quote the resulting bounds" for real data.

## Caveats

- These are recasts by the theorists of published ALEPH analyses. They are not ALEPH limits on a resonance R and are not experimental limits in the collaboration sense.
- Only the 4-jet channel (R -> q qbar) is a hadronic resonance decay. The jj+MET results have R off-shell and invisible (R -> chi chibar, forbidden to be on-shell by m_R < 2 m_chi). They are outside the question's hadronic-decay scope and are recorded only for completeness.
- The 4-jet bounds depend on g^A_b (or g^V_q) and on the assumption g_q = g_chi for some comparisons. The limit is on the mediator coupling to quarks only, not on g_chi.
- Unitarity (Eq. 15) and perturbativity (Eqs. 12, 14) are theory bounds. They are plotted with the experimental bounds and appear as crossover statements above, not as experimental limits.
- Sec. 4 numbers are expected sensitivities assuming observed = SM prediction (Sec. 4). They are projections, not existing limits.
- Cross-section statements are ambiguous: Fig. 5 caption says "upper bound on the total signal cross section before cuts at \sqrt{s}=208 GeV", whereas the Fig. 2 caption gives "upper bound on the total signal cross section at \sqrt{s}=208 GeV" with no "before cuts" qualifier. The text in Sec. 4 says "were \>...0.1 pb" without specifying before or after cuts.
- Fig. 8 caption says "for m_{\chi}>70 GeV the perturbativity condition is stronger than our 'experimental' bound", while Sec. 3.2 states the 4-jet constraints are "independent of m_{\chi} and g_{\chi}, as long as m_{\chi}>m_{R}/2". The two statements are inconsistent as written.
- Mass range of the 4-jet results: lower end of m_R in Fig. 7 and Fig. 8 is not stated in the text. Upper end ~70 GeV (crossover). Values below 10 GeV are outside the primary window.
