## Caveats
- This paper is a W/Z -> two-jet measurement at 630 GeV (UA2). It is outside the Q1 target of direct limits on hadronic resonances at 10 GeV-1 TeV, except for the heavy vector boson limit noted below.
- Abstract (INSPIRE): "A limit on the production cross-section of additional heavy vector bosons decaying into two jets is given as a function of the boson mass." This is the Q1-relevant result. Its values, mass range, CL, and observed vs expected are not extractable from any accessible source (see Open issues).
- No HEPData table for the limit exists in the prefetched record (only Table 1 is present).
