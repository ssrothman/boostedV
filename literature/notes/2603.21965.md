# 2603.21965 — Search for low-mass vector and scalar resonances decaying into a quark-antiquark pair in proton-proton collisions at √s = 13 TeV

- Authors/Collaboration: CMS Collaboration (HEPData first author "Hayrapetyan, Aram et al."); 2026; CMS note EXO-24-007; journal ref: preprint (none found in the arXiv HTML); INSPIRE recid 3133152; HEPData record ins3133152 (DOI 10.17182/hepdata.165379), exists.
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 138 fb^-1 (2016–2018, four data-taking periods); real data. Projections: none.
- Topology / final state: one large-radius AK8 jet with two-prong substructure from a resonance produced with hard ISR (pT > 500 GeV). Resonance decays to qq̄ (bb̄ dominant for spin-0; all flavors for Z′, with light/charm in the low-p_bb SR). Hadronic only; no gg or W/Z/H final states searched.
- Selection summary:
  - Trigger: AK8 trimmed mass > 30 GeV and pT > 400 GeV; fully efficient offline for AK8 pT > 500 GeV.
  - Offline: at least one AK8 jet with pT > 500 GeV, m_SD > 30 GeV, |η| < 2.5; if several, highest p_2-prong chosen. Anti-kT R = 0.8 (AK8); soft drop z_cut = 0.1, β = 0.
  - Vetoes: leptons (e pT > 10, |η| < 2.5; μ |η| < 2.1; isolated), hadronic τ (pT > 20, |η| < 2.3, DeepTau tight), ≥ 6 AK4 jets (pT > 30, |η| < 5), DeepJet-medium b-tagged AK4 jet (pT > 30, |η| < 2.5, |Δφ| > 2π/3 to AK8 jet).
  - Categories: p_2-prong WP at 1% QCD mistag (fail = QCD CR); pass split into high-p_bb SR and low-p_bb SR (PN discriminants).
  - Fit: m_SD 40–350 GeV in 62 bins × five pT bins (boundaries 500, 550, 600, 700, 800, 1200 GeV); ρ = 2 ln(m_SD/pT) restricted to −5.5 < ρ < −2.1 (203 bins); the source prints "−5.5>ρ>−2.1" (reversed as printed; the intended order is used here) [verifier w1_2].
- Model or observable definition (frame/convention):
  - Spin-1 Z′: "coupled equally to all quark flavors with a coupling constant" g_q (Z′ cross sections × 1.2 NLO factor; text: "multiplied by a factor of 1.2 to account for NLO contributions"). HEPData Fig. 2a: "The $Z'$ boson is assumed to decay to quark-antiquark pairs with a 100% branching fraction."
  - Spin-0 φ / pseudoscalar A: "quark couplings equal to the SM Yukawa couplings times a flavor-universal scaling factor" g_qφ or g_qA; produced by gluon fusion; "decays dominantly to bottom quark-antiquark pairs".
  - Narrow-width: "the resonance width is negligible compared to the experimental resolution".
  - Mass range of all limits: 50–300 GeV, 5 GeV steps (51 points in each HEPData table).
  - Reference frame / polarization quantities: not applicable (no polarization observables).
  - Limit form: 95% CL upper limits on coupling (g_q, g_qφ, g_qA), and on σ_vis (fb, model-independent, signal = Z′ template), plus recasts to ε² (dark photon) and c_g/Λ (ALP, top-loop).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

Location labels are the unnumbered paragraphs in the arXiv HTML (section numbers are absent in the extraction).

- QCD multijet background modeling is dominant: "The dominant systematic uncertainty arises from the modeling of the QCD multijet background." (Systematic uncertainties paragraph; arises from unconstrained coefficients of T_res, less from T_MC).
- 2-prong tagging efficiency: "ranging from 10 to 28% depending on the data-taking period" (Systematic uncertainties paragraph; constrained nuisance).
- p_bb tagging efficiency and mistag: "a conservative uncertainty of 30%" (Systematic uncertainties paragraph).
- m_SD scale and resolution: "range up to 0.9% and 12%, respectively" (Systematic uncertainties paragraph).
- W/Z/H theory cross sections: missing higher-order and mixed QCD-EW, constrained by SM W/Z (Systematic uncertainties paragraph).
- Luminosity: "an overall uncertainty of 1.6%" (partially correlated, individual 1.2–2.5%) (Systematic uncertainties paragraph).
- JES/JER: "range from 0.5 to 4.0%" (Systematic uncertainties paragraph).
- Efficiency correction for p_2-prong: "ranges from 0.72 to 0.91" with uncertainties "0.07–0.14" (Signal-modeling/corrections paragraph, measured in data).
- Gluon splitting to bb̄bb̄ CR: "a conservative 30% uncertainty is assigned" (corrections paragraph).

Limits are not split into stat / syst / lumi / theory components in the paper or HEPData.

## Caveats

- Mass range of this paper is 50–300 GeV only. It does not cover 10–50 GeV or above 300 GeV, so it does not by itself answer the 10 GeV–1 TeV question.
- Z′ results assume 100% BR to qq̄ (HEPData Fig. 2a caption). Spin-0 results assume bb̄ dominance (text). Light-quark and charm decays enter only through the low-p_bb SR and the Z′ all-flavor model.
- The Z′ cross section carries an NLO factor of 1.2 (text, signal-model paragraph). This normalization enters any reuse of g_q limits.
- Dark-photon and ALP curves are the paper's own recasts (HEPData Figs. A1a/A1b), not independent measurements. The ALP recast assumes production via a top-quark loop (text).
- Expected bands are the paper's ±1σ/±2σ from the background-only hypothesis; observed values are not given with uncertainties.
- Signal efficiencies and model-independent σ_vis limits are at generator level for pT > 500 GeV, |η| < 2.5 (text, Sec. "Model-independent" paragraph); detector-level limits are the coupling results in Figs. 2a/2b.

## Open issues

1. g_qφ text vs HEPData mismatch. The Results text says the spin-0 scalar limits "range from 1.5 to 5.8". HEPData Figure 2b observed values span 1.3404 (55 GeV) to 5.6001 (300 GeV); the expected values span 1.7287 (55 GeV) to 6.6097 (300 GeV). Neither matches 1.5–5.8. The source of the text range is not identified. HEPData values are recorded above; the text range is not reconciled. Needs checking against the paper's Fig. 2b and the CMS source.
2. g_qA (pseudoscalar) limits: no g_qA column exists in HEPData Figure 2b. Only the text range "1.0 to 3.8" is available, unverified against any table. Per-mass values are figure-only, not tabulated, and not read from the plot.
3. Mapping of HEPData groups: group 0 is treated as observed and group 1 as expected, from column headers and the band structure. This is an inference from the header order and the caption, not an explicit HEPData statement.
4. Date: the title page reads "August 24, 2026" while the arXiv identifier is v1, 23 Mar 2026. Not reconciled; no effect on values.
5. No journal reference found in the HTML; the paper is listed as a preprint.
6. Figure 2 and Figure A-series values are taken from HEPData, not from the plots. No values were read off figures.
7. Scope: only the Z′ / scalar / pseudoscalar dijet results are in this note. W′-like and ALP results beyond the recasts above were not searched in this paper and are not extracted.
