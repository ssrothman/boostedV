# 2609.21850 — Search for resonant and nonresonant production of pairs of dijet resonances with b jets in the final state in proton-proton collisions at √s = 13 TeV

- Authors/Collaboration, year, journal ref, INSPIRE recid, HEPData: CMS Collaboration; 2026; preprint (arXiv:2609.21850v1, 18 Sep 2026; CMS-EXO-24-039, CERN-EP-2026-185; no journal ref in INSPIRE record); INSPIRE recid 3205293; HEPData record ins3205293 (inspire_id 3205293, HEPData recid 169576, version 1; 31 data tables).
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 138 fb^-1 (Abstract); measured CMS data, not recast. Projection/simulation is used only for signal shapes and acceptance.
- Topology / final state: pp → XX → (b j)(b j), X a spin-0 dijet resonance decaying to one b jet and one light-flavour jet, B(X → bq) set to 1 (Sec. 0.1, 0.9). Four-jet final state. Nonresonant benchmark: RPV top-squark pair production (Sec. 0.3). Resonant benchmark: Y → XX with the D2D diquark model (Sec. 0.3).
- Selection summary:
  - Anti-kT jets, distance parameter 0.4 offline (0.8 only for the HLT); four leading jets pT > 80 GeV, |η| < 2.4 (2016) or 2.5 (2017–2018) (Sec. 0.4).
  - Pairing: minimise |ΔR1 − 0.8| + |ΔR2 − 0.8| (Eq. 6); ΔR1, ΔR2 < 2.0; Δη < 1.1 (Eq. 7); mass asymmetry < 0.1 (Eq. 8) (Sec. 0.4).
  - b tagging: each dijet has at least one DeepJet "medium" b jet (Sec. 0.4).
  - Nonresonant: m̄_2j = (m1+m2)/2 in three α = m̄_2j/m_4j bins (lower edges 0.15, 0.25, 0.35; width 0.1; overflow above 0.35); mass thresholds 0.35, 0.53, 0.61 TeV for the three bins (Sec. 0.5). Resonant: twelve α bins from 0.12 to 0.34 (step 0.02), overflow above 0.34, m_4j > 1.6 TeV (Sec. 0.5).
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Limit quantity: 95% CL upper limit on σ × B × A (fb) for pp → XX → (bj)(bj) versus the true X mass (nonresonant) or the true Y mass (resonant, α_true = M(X)/M(Y)). B = 1. A = fraction passing the nominal kinematic selection of Sec. 0.4 excluding b tagging, mass threshold and trigger (Sec. 0.6, 0.9).
  - Method: asymptotic approximation of the test statistic, CMS Combine, one fit per signal mass; the text does not describe the CLs construction (see Open issues).
  - Polarization / reference frame: none (X is spin-0, no polarization observable). Reference frame: not stated; dijet pairing uses η–φ separations only (Eq. 5).
  - Particle vs detector level: not stated. A is defined with the reconstructed-jet selection of Sec. 0.4 and the text says it is "independent of detector-specific effects" (Sec. 0.9); the level is not named.

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Background modelling (functional form, discrete profiling): "These uncertainties have an effect on the extracted cross section limits that range from a few percent up to 50%, depending on the mass." (Sec. 0.8). Background is fully data-driven (Sec. 0.7).
- Signal b-tagging: "The systematic uncertainty in the signal is dominated by the b-tagging uncertainty, which ranges from 6 to 33%, depending on the signal mass" (Sec. 0.8).
- Jet energy scale: "The jet energy scale uncertainty is below 2% across all" mass values; applied as ±2% shift of the signal mass distributions (Sec. 0.8).
- Jet energy resolution: "leads to a 10% uncertainty in the resolution of the resonance masses" (Sec. 0.8).
- Luminosity: "the total integrated luminosity has an uncertainty of 1.6%" (Sec. 0.8).
- Dominance: "At lower masses, the uncertainties related to the background modeling have the largest impact on the extracted cross section limit" (Sec. 0.8). The paper gives no per-limit numerical breakdown of these systematics.

## Caveats

- Nonresonant limits cover X masses 0.5–2.0 TeV (HEPData, Sec. 0.9). Only 0.5–1.0 TeV lies in the primary window; 1.05–2.0 TeV rows are flagged. No nonresonant limit below 0.5 TeV is reported in HEPData or text.
- Resonant limits (Y, 2.0–8.0 TeV) lie entirely outside the 10 GeV–1 TeV window and are flagged. Text: "the search is not extended beyond resonance masses of 8 TeV" (Sec. 0.5).
- The nonresonant X is the RPV top squark (Sec. 0.1, 0.3). The σ×B×A limits therefore apply to hadronic RPV stop pair production with B = 1; they are not generic spin-0 dijet limits.
- The highest-mass event (m_4j = 5.5 TeV, m̄_2j = 1.54 TeV) is discussed in Sec. 0.5 and 0.9; it is a single data event and not a limit.
- The paper states it "cannot confirm or exclude" the 8.6 TeV excess of the untagged search (Sec. 0.5).

## Open issues

- Observable: HEPData keywords state observable "CLs" for the limit tables; the text describes only "the asymptotic approximation of the test statistic" and does not state the CLs construction. Not resolved from the text.
- Column mapping: HEPData groups 0/1/2 are inferred from header order and the Fig. 10–12 captions (dashed = expected, black points = observed). Whether the "Exp." median is the median or another expected value is not stated explicitly.

- Expected-band components (stat vs syst) are not stated; the ±1σ/±2σ values are copied as given with no interpretation.
- Figs. 13–14 (local p-values) are figure-only and not tabulated in the records used; no values were read from them.
- The resonant tables were summarised by endpoints only in this note; interior points are in the HEPData JSON files, not transcribed here.
- Source handling: the arXiv HTML and text in the cache directory were reused from a prior download (html.html, text.txt, arXiv:2609.21850v1 by LaTeXML header); they were not re-fetched in this run. Section numbers "0.x" follow that HTML rendering.
