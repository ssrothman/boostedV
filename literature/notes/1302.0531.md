# 1302.0531 — Search for pair-produced dijet resonances in four-jet final states in pp collisions at sqrt(s) = 7 TeV

- CMS Collaboration (CMS-EXO-11-016, CERN-PH-EP/2013-007), 2013 (arXiv v2 dated 30 Apr 2013). Journal ref: preprint; PDF says "Submitted to Physical Review Letters", published reference not in the PDF. INSPIRE recid 1217552. HEPData: none (`.cache/1302.0531/hepdata/NONE`: "no HEPData record").
- sqrt(s) = 7 TeV; integrated luminosity 5.0 fb^-1 (2011 data); data (not simulation). Limits use simulated signal (MadGraph5, Pythia 6.4.26, CTEQ6L1, GEANT4 detector simulation).
- Topology / final state: pp -> two pair-produced narrow resonances, each decaying to a dijet, giving a four-jet final state. Benchmarks: colorons C (gg -> CC -> qqqq), with an optional C -> S8 S8 channel; and R-parity-violating stops decaying to qq̄.
- Selection summary:
  - Trigger: at least four jets, each |eta| < 3.0 and pT > 70 or 80 GeV (run-dependent); 99.5% efficient for four leading jets with pT > 110 GeV.
  - Offline: at least four anti-kT (R = 0.5) particle-flow jets, each pT > 110 GeV and |eta| < 2.5; dijet pair separation ΔR_jj >= 0.7.
  - Pairing: choose the pairing with smallest Δm/m_avg; require Δm < 0.15 m_avg; require Δ > 25 GeV for each dijet.
  - Mass windows: fit range m_avg > 220 GeV; limit computed for m_avg >= 250 GeV; observed spectrum extends to 1200 GeV.
  - Dijet-mass resolution: core rms approximately 4.5% (signal shape: two Gaussians, p. 2).
- Model or observable definition (frame/convention):
  - Observable: m_avg = average mass of the two dijets in the chosen pairing; Δ = sum_{i=1,2}(pT)_i − m_avg (definition quoted from p. 2; no sign-convention issues).
  - Limit quantity: σ × Br² × A (pair production cross section × square of dijet branching fraction × detector acceptance). Axis label of Fig. 3 reads "σ × Br² × Acc (pb)" (figure label only).
  - Polarization / reference frame: not applicable (no angular or helicity analysis); not stated.
  - Coloron: color-octet vector; S8: color-octet scalar with tan θ = 0.3 and m_S8 = 150 GeV in the S8S8-competition scenario (p. 5).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Signal acceptance uncertainty of 10% (p. 4), described as determined by JES (2.2%) and JER (10%) [ref 19] (p. 4). The text does not give a numeric breakdown of these; see Open issues for the wording ambiguity.
- Luminosity uncertainty 2.2% [ref 32] (p. 4).
- Background-function parameters are nuisance parameters with log-normal priors (p. 4); no numerical size is given.
- PDF uncertainty on the expected signal yield: stated as negligible (p. 4).
- Statistical vs systematic split of the limits: not stated.

## Caveats

- The limit range 250–1200 GeV is stated in the text (abstract and p. 4). The part of the range from 1000 to 1200 GeV is OUTSIDE the primary 10 GeV–1 TeV window and is flagged. The 1200 GeV upper endpoint is also outside the window.
- The largest local fluctuation is at m = 1130 GeV, local significance 2.6σ, global 1.2σ (p. 3): "the largest fluctuation in the pair-produced dijet mass spectrum occurs for a hypothetical resonance mass of 1130 GeV and has a local statistical significance of 2.6σ." This is a non-limit result, outside the primary window (flagged).
- Limits assume a narrow resonance: "having a width negligible compared with the experimental resolution" (p. 6).
- The S8 is not constrained: "This analysis is not sensitive to the pair-produced S8, where the color-octet scalars decay exclusively to qq" (p. 5).
- Signal shape is the same for colorons and stops (p. 2), and stop acceptance is larger than coloron acceptance because "the stop production model includes qq interactions and has a different final state angular distribution" (p. 3).
- The paper states the limit is on pair production, Br to dijets and acceptance, and is not a cross-section limit alone.
- Particle vs detector level: acceptance is "after applying all selection criteria" (Table 1, p. 5) on simulated events passed through the GEANT4 detector simulation (p. 2); jet-level unfolding is not described.
- Measured (2011 data, 5.0 fb^-1), not recast or projected.
