## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- No experimental systematics are discussed. The paper states the calculation uses MRS D'_- PDFs with Λ^(4)_MSbar = 215 MeV and a one-loop α_s; renormalization and factorization scales μ_R² = μ_F² = M_V² + p_T²(V) (Sec. III, location: Sec. III, first paragraphs).
- Energy resolution is simulated by Gaussian smearing "on the CDF specifications [16]" (Sec. III). The paper states the smearing "has a non-negligible effect only on the cos θ distribution" for W, and is negligible for Z.
- The paper states the NLO corrections to A_0 and A_2 "are fairly small [6]" (Sec. III). No numerical size given.

## Caveats

- Theory study only. No experimental data, no measured f_L/f_0/A_0 values, no HEPData record. This paper does not answer Q2 with a measurement.
- Values in figures are not tabulated; per the rules, no values have been read off plots.
- The paper states A_3 "has the wrong sign in the figures in Ref. [6]" and that Ref. [6] has "a misprint in the definition of p_T" (front-matter note). Stated by the authors; not verified here.
- Reference to a UA1 measurement: the paper says "it was in fact the study of this relation in W W decays by UA1 which fixed the spin of the gluon [1]" (Sec. III). This is a gluon-spin statement, not a W polarization measurement; not recorded as a quoted result.
- The paper's W polarization fraction language is in terms of helicity cross sections and angular coefficients; no normalization convention beyond Eqs. (14), (26), (27) is given.
