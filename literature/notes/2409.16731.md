- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Frame (ZZ, WZ; Sec. 2.1): "we will employ the di-boson CM frame as Lorentz frame for the helicity-state definition, and the di-boson-system direction in the laboratory as a reference axis to determine azimuthal decay angles." This is the default "unless otherwise stated". Decay angles are for the positive lepton (Sec. 2.1, Eq. 1 text); W- is obtained by charge conjugation.
  - H decay frames (Sec. 4.3): H rest frame ("H-rest") or four-lepton CM frame ("4ℓ-CM"); they coincide at LO. Table 4 and Table 6 captions state which is used.
  - Angular expansion (Eq. 2, Eq. 6, Eq. 7): real spherical harmonics Y_lm, l ≤ 2, coefficients α_lm (single boson, labels 1 and 3) and γ_{l1 m1 l3 m3} (joint). Projection formula Eq. 4: α_lm = ∫ dσ/(dφ dcosθ dX) Y_lm, normalised by dσ/dX. Valid without lepton cuts; not valid with fiducial cuts or neutrino reconstruction (Eqs. 35–37, Sec. 4.4).
  - Joint polarisation fractions f_λλ' (Eq. 8), λ, λ' ∈ {L, +, −} (first index boson 1, second boson 3); sum rules Eq. 10: Σ f_λλ' = 1, Σ_λ' f_λλ' = f^(1)_λ, Σ_λ f_λλ' = f^(3)_λ'.
  - Map to coefficients (Eq. 11, verbatim alttext): α^(i)_{1,0} = (1/4)√(3/π) η_i (f^(i)_+ − f^(i)_−); α^(i)_{2,0} = (1 − 3 f^(i)_L)/(4√(5π)); γ_{1,0,1,0} = 3 η_1 η_3 (f_−− + f_++ − f_−+ − f_+−)/(16π); γ_{2,0,2,0} = (1 − 3 f^(1)_L − 3 f^(3)_L + 9 f_LL)/(80π). η_ℓ = (c_−² − c_+²)/(c_+² + c_−²) ≈ 0.219 (Eq. 9, stated in the (G_μ, M_W, M_Z) scheme for massless leptons).
  - R_c = f_LL /(f^(1)_L f^(3)_L) (Eq. 12; defined as in ATLAS 2211.09435).
  - Map to the quantum-tomography density matrix (Eqs. 13–16): ρ is a 9×9 spin-density matrix ρ = (1/9)[I⊗I + Σ A^(1)_lm (T^l_m⊗I) + Σ A^(3)_lm (I⊗T^l_m) + Σ C (T^l1_m1 ⊗ T^l3_m3)] in the irreducible-tensor basis T^l_m of Ref. [44] (arXiv 2209.13441), with A, C complex. Conversion (Eq. 16, verbatim LaTeX alttext): √(40π) α^(i)_{2,m_i} = A^(i)_{2,m_i}; 40π γ_{2,m1,2,m3} = C_{2,m1,2,m3}; 8π γ_{1,m1,1,m3}/η_ℓ² = C_{1,m1,1,m3}. The paper notes its decay angles are for positive leptons whereas Ref. [44] uses negative leptons (Sec. 2.3).
  - **No explicit map to helicity-basis matrix elements ρ_λλ' is given.** The f_λλ' of Eq. 8 are stated only as joint polarisation fractions; their identification with diagonal ρ entries is not stated.
  - Entanglement (scalar decay, Eq. 17): entangled if γ_{2,1,2,−1} ≠ 0 or γ_{2,2,2,−2} ≠ 0 (stated as "sufficient and necessary").
  - CGLMP Bell operator expectation (Eq. 18–20): I_3 = 1/2 + 4√3/9 − √(5π)(1 − 8√3/9) α_{2,0} − 40π(2/3 + 4√3/9) γ_{2,1,2,−1} + (20π/3) γ_{2,2,2,−2}; claimed bound ≤ 2 (Eq. 18).
  - Tree-level H→ZZ* relations: the Table 4 caption confirms only Eq. 30 numerically at LO; Eqs. 31–32 are discussed only at NLO EW [verifier w4_1].

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

The paper gives no systematic-uncertainty budget. It quantifies only the MC numerical error and the effects of the approximations below.
- Off-shell vs pole/NWA approximation: "off-shell effects have an impact by less than 2% in most of phase-space regions" (Sec. 4.1). For α^(1)_{2,0} the DPA–off-shell difference is "sub-percent", largest "(0.4%)" at NLO EW ("with the largest one ( 0.4\% ) found at NLO EW", Sec. 4.1, Table 2).
- NLO QCD effect on R_c is not covered by LO scale/PDF variation: "not covered by the LO-uncertainty estimate based on QCD-scale and PDF variations" (Sec. 4.2, after Eq. 29).
- Frame choice at NLO EW (H→4ℓ): "The radiative effects are the 6.5% level for \alpha_{2,0} in the 4 \ell -CM frame, while 14% corrections are found in the H-rest frame." (Sec. 4.3). For I_3, "slightly larger effects ( -4\% ) are found in the H-rest frame" (Sec. 4.3).
- Fiducial cuts and neutrino reconstruction (projection method invalid): "leading to large, unphysical modifications to all considered coefficients" (Sec. 4.4, Table 6 text). For W+Z: "The reconstruction effects are remarkably large for almost all considered coefficients." (Sec. 4.4, Table 7 text).
- SM-acceptance extrapolation to SMEFT pseudo-data (Sec. 4.5, Table 8): "with up to 30% discrepancies for polar coefficients, and even a wrong sign for \alpha^{(1)}_{2,2}" (Sec. 4.5 text after Table 8).

## Caveats

- This is a theory/phenomenology paper: no measured element, no data, no measured uncertainty. For Q3 it qualifies only through (a) the stated map between α/γ and the tensor-basis A, C (Eq. 16) and (b) predicted values of several elements with frame dependence.
- The paper states that multiple elements (α_{l,m} with l ≤ 2, γ_{l1 m1 l3 m3}) are needed for entanglement/Bell quantities, and that the l ≤ 2 interpretation fails with fiducial cuts, neutrino reconstruction and at NLO EW for H decay. Table values with cuts are therefore not spin-density-matrix entries in the Eq. 6 sense.
- The paper states the tree-level structure of Eq. 6 "is no longer valid" at NLO EW for H→ZZ* (Sec. 4.3). The paper also says the Eq. 31 relations are violated at NLO EW ("The first tree-level relation of Eq. 31 is mildly violated ... The second relation of Eq. 31 is violated at NLO EW for both frame choices.").
- Uncertainties are numerical only. Table 3 and Table 5 quote errors as printed; the paper does not give an overall theory error for them.
- Ratio/relative-change statements in the text (e.g. "diminished by 7%") were kept as the paper's words, not recomputed.
