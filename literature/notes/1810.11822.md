# 1810.11822 — Search for low-mass resonances decaying into bottom quark-antiquark pairs in pp collisions at sqrt(s) = 13 TeV

- CMS Collaboration (CMS-EXO-17-024), 2018 (arXiv v3 dated 10 Jan 2019); Phys. Rev. D 99, 012005 (2019), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.99.012005; INSPIRE recid 1700756; HEPData record: none (record URL returns 404; HEPData search for arXiv:1810.11822 returns total 0)
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 35.9 fb^-1 (2016 data); data
- Topology / final state: single large-radius jet from a boosted resonance decaying to b bbar, produced with ISR/FSR; search for a narrow peak in the soft-drop jet mass (mSD). Resonance: spin-0 scalar Phi or pseudoscalar A, produced via gluon fusion (loop-induced), decaying to b bbar (minimal flavour violation, couplings proportional to Yukawa with universal g_q).
- Selection summary:
  - Trigger: AK8 jet pT > 450 GeV, |eta| < 2.5, mass > 40 GeV, or CA15 jet pT > 500 GeV, |eta| < 2.5 (Sec. 0.4, "greater than 95% efficient").
  - Jets: leading-pT jet is the candidate; AK8 (anti-kT, R = 0.8) for signal mass < 175 GeV, CA15 (Cambridge-Aachen, R = 1.5) above; soft drop beta = 0 (z_cut 0.1); pT categories 450–1000 GeV (AK8) and 500–1000 GeV (CA15) (Sec. 0.4, 0.7).
  - Vetoes: isolated e (pT > 10 GeV), mu (pT > 10 GeV), tau_h (pT > 18 GeV); MET > 140 GeV vetoed (Sec. 0.4).
  - Substructure: N21,DDT < 0 (26% QCD efficiency); rho window -6 < rho < -2.1 (AK8), -4.7 < rho < -1.0 (CA15); double-b tag passing region (Sec. 0.4).
  - Fit range for mSD: 40–201 GeV (AK8), 82–399 GeV (CA15), 7 GeV bins (Sec. 0.7). Mass scan covers 50–350 GeV.
- Model or observable definition:
  - Lagrangians (Eqs. 1–2): Phi and A couple to fermions with a universal coupling g_q times Yukawa couplings y_f = 2 m_f / v, v = 246 GeV (Sec. 0.1). The prefactor on Phi and A in Eqs. 1–2 is not unambiguous in the text rendering; not recorded (see Open issues).
  - Observables: sigma x B(bb̄) (gluon fusion, LO cross section, no cuts on final state) and the coupling g_q for Phi or A (Sec. 0.3, 0.7).
  - Polarization / reference frame: not applicable (spin-0 resonance; no angular distribution quantities are defined in the paper).
  - Limit method: CLs with profile-likelihood ratio, asymptotic formulae, 95% CL (Sec. 0.7).

## Caveats

- Mass range 50–350 GeV only (not the 10 GeV–1 TeV scope of Q1 in full); spin-0 only (no vector/axial/W' results).
- The limits are on sigma x B(bb̄) for gluon fusion with LO cross section normalization and no NLO K-factor applied; the paper states NLO would improve the coupling sensitivity by about a factor 2 (Sec. 0.3). Scalar / pseudoscalar gluon-fusion production is assumed with couplings proportional to Yukawa couplings (MFV).
- Coupling g_q is the universal quark coupling, not a hadrophilic-specific coupling; the paper's "g_q" applies to the b quark (and other fermions with the same universal factor).
- Limit values from text only: no table of limits; full limit curves exist only in Figs. 7–8.
- Quotes are taken from the arXiv HTML text (LaTeXML rendering); LaTeX macros such as \GeV and \unitpb are rendered as "GeV" and "pb" here, and zero-width spaces are dropped. Numbers are unchanged.
- Notation: the paper writes Phi(bb̄) and A(bb̄). In the quotes, the coupling symbol is written as "g_q" (source: the Phi or A symbol with a quark subscript); this is the only substitution in quotes besides the macro rendering above.

## Open issues

- Observed vs expected is NOT stated for 79 pb, 86 pb, 3.9, 2.5, 4.7, or 4.6. The paper only says "We exclude ... as low as" and "equivalent to the 95% CL upper limit". The text says the largest downward fluctuation in the limits occurs at 175 GeV in AK8, which suggests the 175 GeV observed limit is below expected, but this is inference and is not attributed to the 79/86 pb values. Needs the figure captions or the paper's Figs. 7–8 (not read as values).
- No statistical vs systematic split of the limit values is given.
- No HEPData record exists, so there are no tabulated limits to cross-check.
- The paper does not state whether 79/86 pb and 3.9/2.5 are at exactly 175 GeV or the minimum of the curve; the text says "at a resonance mass of 175 GeV".
- The normalization prefactor on the Phi and A terms in Eqs. 1–2 is not readable unambiguously from the text rendering; check the PDF before using it.
- Table numbers in the HTML version are labelled "Table 0.4" and "Table 0.6" (section-number labelling from the LaTeXML conversion); the original journal numbering was not checked.
