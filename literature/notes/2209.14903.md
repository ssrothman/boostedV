# 2209.14903 — Measurement of the polarisation of W bosons produced in top-quark decays using dilepton events at √s = 13 TeV with the ATLAS experiment

- ATLAS Collaboration, 2023 (preprint CERN-EP-2022-197, arXiv v2 16 Jun 2023); journal: Phys. Lett. B 843 (2023) 137829; INSPIRE recid 2158391; HEPData: none (`.cache/2209.14903/hepdata/NONE`, "no HEPData record")
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 139 fb⁻¹ (2015–2018 data, Sec. 3); data (unfolded to parton level). Simulation (Powheg Box v2 + Pythia 8.230 tt̄, Sherpa V+jets, etc.) used for background, unfolding and validation only.
- Topology / final state: tt̄ → dilepton (e/μ incl. τ→ℓ), ≥2 b-tagged jets, W from top decay (W→ℓν). Observable is the W helicity angle of the W boson from the top decay; no V+jets or diboson measurement.
- Selection summary (Sec. 4, 6):
  - Single-electron/muon triggers; exactly two opposite-charge leptons, pT > 25 GeV (2015) or > 27 GeV (2016–2018); e |η| < 2.47, μ |η| < 2.5.
  - ≥ 2 jets with pT > 25 GeV, |η| < 2.5, anti-kt R = 0.4; ≥ 2 b-tagged (DL1r, 60% efficiency WP).
  - Same-flavour: m_ll outside 80–100 GeV and E_T^miss > 60 GeV; all channels m_ll > 15 GeV.
  - Neutrino-weighting reconstruction of top kinematics; solution found for about 90% of tt̄ events (Sec. 5).
  - Parton-level cos θ* unfolded with iterative Bayesian unfolding (RooUnfold), regularisation parameter 180 (Sec. 6). No p_T binning.
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Observable: cos θ*, θ* = angle between the charged-lepton momentum direction and the reversed b-quark direction (from the top decay), both in the W boson rest frame (Abstract; Sec. 1). The paper does not name the frame as "helicity" or "Collins–Soper"; the axis is defined by the b-quark direction as stated.
  - Eq. (1) (Sec. 1): (1/σ) dσ/dcosθ* = (3/4)(1−cos²θ*) f0 + (3/8)(1−cosθ*)² fL + (3/8)(1+cosθ*)² fR. Helicity fractions f0 (longitudinal), fL (left-handed), fR (right-handed). ρ00 and A0 are not used in the paper (not stated).
  - Fit (Sec. 7): f0 = 1 − fL − fR (unitarity); fL, fR free and not restricted to [0,1]; χ² with full covariance (stat + syst).
  - Parton level: leptons from MC truth record before FSR, and for τ before decay; both top quarks required to decay leptonically at truth level (Sec. 6). The paper uses the term "parton level" (not "particle level").

## Caveats

- Result is for W bosons from tt̄ dilepton events only; it is not a V+jets, diboson or VBS measurement, and there is no p_T-dependent result.
- Fit parameters are not restricted: "are allowed to take unphysical values outside of the [0,1] interval" (Sec. 7). fR = −0.002 is within this unrestricted fit (Sec. 9).
- MC-truth cos θ* is reweighted to Eq. (1) at NNLO fractions because parton-shower reshuffling distorts it "at a few per mille level" (Sec. 6).
- Previous measurements cited in Sec. 1 (not this paper's results): ATLAS 8 TeV lepton+jets (template fit) f0 = 0.709±0.012 (stat. + bkg.) −0.014+0.015 (syst.), fL = 0.299±0.008 (stat. + bkg.) −0.012+0.013 (syst.), fR = −0.008±0.006 (stat. + bkg.) ±0.012 (syst.); ATLAS+CMS combined 8 TeV (total uncertainties): f0 = 0.693±0.014, fL = 0.315±0.011, fR = −0.008±0.007. Quote (Sec. 1): "fR=−0.008±0.006 (stat. + bkg.) ± 0.012 (syst.) using the template fit method."
- Sec. 9: "A small degree of non-closure is observed but the corresponding uncertainty is negligible and does not change the total uncertainties listed in Table 3."

## Open issues

- HEPData: no record exists (`hepdata/NONE`). All numbers are from the arXiv HTML text (`.cache/2209.14903/paper.html`, rendered to `paper.txt`), with LaTeX markup removed from quotes.
- Figures only: per-bin cos θ* cross-section values, bin edges, number of bins, and the migration matrix (Fig. 2) are not tabulated in text; recorded as figure-only, no values read off plots.
- Table 3 vs text (Sec. 9): the text says the largest systematic in all three fractions comes from tt̄ modelling (generator choice). For fR, the printed single-row values are jet reconstruction 0.010 vs tt̄ generator choice 0.004 (and tt̄ radiation and top-quark mass 0.007 each). The claim may hold only for the grouped "tt̄ modelling" category; I have not resolved this and did not compute any group sums.
- Table 3 total for f0 (0.015) is stated by the paper to differ from the quadrature sum of its components because of correlations (Table 3 caption); I did not verify the total.
- Linearity-test non-closure: Sec. 7 says non-closure "is included as a systematic uncertainty". Sec. 9 says a small non-closure (unfolding test) is negligible and does not change Table 3 totals. It is not clear from the text whether the linearity-test term is in the Table 3 totals; flagged, not resolved.
- Table 3 partial-covariance fits: best-fit values shift "by up to a quarter of the total uncertainty" (Table 3 caption); this affects the category breakdown precision.
- Frame naming: the paper does not name the reference frame (helicity or Collins–Soper); the definition is given only by the lepton/b-quark directions in the W rest frame (Sec. 1, Abstract). Not stated otherwise.
- ρ00 and A0 (other polarisation parametrisations) are not used or quoted in this paper; not stated.
- Sec. 1 gives SM values "assuming a top-quark mass mtop = 172.8 GeV" while the MC uses 172.5 GeV (Sec. 3); both are as stated, not reconciled by the paper here.
