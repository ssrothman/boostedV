# 1202.5272 — Combination of CDF and D0 measurements of the W boson helicity in top quark decays

- CDF and D0 Collaborations, 2012. Journal ref: Phys. Rev. D 85, 071106 (2012), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.85.071106 (from INSPIRE; not checked against the journal). INSPIRE recid 1090176. HEPData record: none (`hepdata/NONE`: "no HEPData record").
- sqrt(s): not stated (paper says only "Tevatron proton-antiproton Collider"). Integrated luminosity: CDF lepton+jets 2.7 fb-1, CDF dilepton 5.1 fb-1, D0 5.4 fb-1 (Sec. II). Data (input measurements from Refs. [7], [8], [9]; no new simulation/projection beyond their MC templates).
- Topology / final state: t tbar → W+ W- b bbar, W from top decay. Lepton+jets (CDF, D0) and dilepton (CDF, D0) channels. Observable is the W+ helicity composition (f0, f+, f-) from top decay.
- Selection summary (details deferred to Refs. [7], [8], [9]; not stated in this paper):
  - CDF lepton+jets: "matrix element" method (Ref. [10]) using jets, leptons, and missing E_T.
  - CDF dilepton and D0 (lepton+jets and dilepton): distribution of cos θ* per top decay, compared to MC templates with different helicity admixtures (Sec. II).
  - Neutrino reconstructed by constrained kinematic fit (lepton+jets) or algebraic solution (dilepton) (Sec. II).
  - Jet algorithm, R, pT and |y| ranges: not stated.
  - Constraint assumed in both 2D and 1D fits: f- + f0 + f+ = 1 (Sec. II).
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - f0, f-, f+ are the fractions of W+ bosons from top decay in longitudinal, left-handed, right-handed helicity states (Sec. I). Paper: "hermitian conjugation will be assumed throughout the paper", so W- is not separately measured.
  - Angular distribution, Eq. (1): ω(cos θ*) ∝ 2(1 − cos²θ*) f0 + (1 − cos θ*)² f− + (1 + cos θ*)² f+.
  - Frame: the paper calls θ* the "helicity angle" and defines it as the angle in the W rest frame between the direction opposite to the top quark and the down-type fermion. The name of the frame is not stated beyond this definition.
  - Polarization quantities f_L / A_0 / ρ_00: not used in this paper; the paper uses f0 (= longitudinal fraction, dimensionless).
  - Momentum: no pT dependence is measured or quoted (inclusive in top-decay kinematics).

## Caveats

- Inclusive top decay measurement; no pT dependence and no polarization vs pT is reported. The paper contains no Z or W from V+jets.
- Input CDF values are shifted from their published values to m_t = 172.5 GeV (Table I caption: "The CDF measurements have been shifted from their published values"). Quote (Sec. II): "To facilitate the combination of results, the CDF helicity fractions are shifted to mt of 172.5 GeV/c2". The unshifted published values are not recorded here.
- m_t uncertainty treated as 1.4 GeV, fully correlated (MTOP category). Quote (Sec. II): "This uncertainty corresponds to a 1.4 GeV/c2 uncertainty on mt".
- Weights include negative values. Table IX (2D): the D0 f+ input has f0 weight −22.0%; the CDF lepton+jets f0 input has f+ weight −15.4%. Quote (Sec. V): "In some cases, the weights have negative values".
- SM reference values quoted in the paper (Intro): f0 = 0.688 ± 0.004, f- = 0.310 ± 0.004, f+ = 0.0017 ± 0.0001, from Ref. [4]. Quote: "the expected SM values are f0 = 0.688 ± 0.004, f− = 0.310 ± 0.004 , and f+ = 0.0017 ± 0.0001". These are reference values, not measurements.
- Paper's own statement: "The SM values for the helicity fractions lie within the 68% C.L. contour of probability." (Sec. V, Fig. 1 context). Figure-only.
- Quotes in this note come from the HTML full text (arXiv HTML) and the PDF (arXiv PDF, v1). Spacing in quotes is normalized from the PDF rendering.

- sqrt(s), reference frame name, particle vs detector level, and jet/lepton selection cuts are not stated in this paper (selection details are in Refs. [7], [8], [9], not fetched).

- Fig. 1 contours: figure-only, not tabulated.
