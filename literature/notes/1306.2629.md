# 1306.2629 — Coupling–Mass Mapping of Dijet Peak Searches

- Authors: Bogdan A. Dobrescu, Felix Yu (Fermilab theory). Journal: Phys. Rev. D 88, 035021 (2013); erratum Phys. Rev. D 90, 079901 (2014). INSPIRE recid 1238122. HEPData: none (https://www.hepdata.net/record/ins1238122?format=json returned HTTP 404). Text used: arXiv HTML, the version revised 10 Sep 2014.
- sqrt(s): not applicable (phenomenology mapping). Searches it maps: 0.63, 1.8, 1.96, 7, 8 TeV (Table 2). Integrated luminosity: per cited search (Table 2). Data: existing experimental searches, mapped by MC simulation (MadGraph5 + Pythia 6 + PGS), LO, no new data.
- Topology / final state: dijet (qq̄, bb̄ jets) resonance from s-channel production. Z′_B (baryon-number gauge boson, color singlet) and coloron G′ (color octet, tan θ). Spin 1 only; no scalar, pseudoscalar, ALP, W′ or Higgs-like results in this paper.
- Selection summary (from the mapped searches, Sec. III.1; the paper does not impose its own cuts on data):
  - Searches with m_jj < 200 GeV: UA2 [4] used for 140–200 GeV; UA2/CDF searches below 140 GeV excluded by the authors (Sec. III.1.1).
  - Tevatron: CDF Run I [9] (≈110 pb⁻¹) for 200–260 GeV; D0 [11] competes but the paper chooses to extract the limit from CDF only (paper.txt:722–725) [verifier w1_1]; CDF Run II [10] (1.1 fb⁻¹) above 260 GeV (Sec. III.1.2).
  - CMS 0.13 fb⁻¹ data-scouting [23] for 260–900 GeV; CMS q qq (not q q̄) resonance constraint adopted for all CMS limits (Sec. III.1.2).
  - 900–1000 GeV: ATLAS 1 fb⁻¹ [16]; 1–1.2 TeV: CMS 4.0 fb⁻¹ [24] and ATLAS 4.8 fb⁻¹ [17] / CMS 5.0 fb⁻¹ [22] (Sec. III.1.3). Above 1.2 TeV: CMS 19.6 fb⁻¹ [25] (Sec. III.1.3). ATLAS Gaussian limits: smallest Gaussian width adopted, Gaussian-core efficiency ε_G from Appendix.
  - Mapping: (g_B)_max = 0.2 × [ (σ·B·A)_limit / σ_0.2·B·A ]^{1/2}, Eq. (11); coloron analogous with tan θ. Acceptance A from MC with each search's cuts. The paper's own jet algorithm/R: not stated in the text read.
- Model or observable definition (frame/convention):
  - Z′_B coupling to quarks: (g_B/6) Z′_Bμ q̄γ^μ q, Eq. (6), with α_B = g_B²/(4π). Normalization stated as "using the normalization where the group generator is 1/2".
  - Coloron coupling: g_s tan θ q̄γ^μ T^a G′^a_μ q, Eq. (9), g_s = √(4πα_s). tan θ = ratio of SU(3)_2 and SU(3)_1 gauge couplings (Sec. II.2).
  - Limits are upper limits on the coupling (g_B or tan θ) as a function of mass, derived by this paper. They are not experimental quotes of a coupling limit.
  - Reference frame / polarization quantities: not applicable (no angular analysis; the paper says angular correlations are not discussed).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Dijet mass resolution ~5–10%, "depending on mass [2] as well as experiment" (Sec. III.1).
- Coloron NLO K-factor: "can vary between roughly −30% and +20%" (source typeset as "−30%-30% and +20%+20%"; the text is garbled here, so this is a copy with a typesetting caveat), not included in the mapping (Sec. III.2).
- Z′_B NLO effect: "O(10%)" on g_B, not included (Sec. III.2).
- PDF uncertainties increase at resonance mass approaching √s (Sec. III.2).
- ATLAS Gaussian-core efficiency ε_G: fit parameter ranges given in the Appendix (α within 0.05–3.5, n within 0.5–5.0, x̄ within 70–105%, σ within 0.5–30%); no uncertainty on the final efficiency is quoted.
- Experimental stat/syst uncertainties of the searches: not reproduced in this paper.

## Caveats

- The coupling limits are the paper's derived mapping (Eq. 11), built on MC acceptance computed by the authors, not the experiments' own coupling limits.
- The paper says the mapping is "somewhat model dependent" (Sec. IV).
- Leading-order production only (MadGraph5 v1.5.7, CTEQ6L1, Pythia 6.4.20, PGS v4); NLO not included.
- Sub-200 GeV searches (UA2 and CDF below 140 GeV) excluded by the authors because of WW/ZZ subtraction; coverage below ~140–200 GeV is absent. The question asks about 10 GeV–1 TeV; this paper's experimental coverage is roughly 0.14–5.3 TeV (Table 2).
- Coloron limits above "Wide resonance" line (width/mass > 15%) are "not reliable if set by narrow resonance searches" (Sec. III.2).
- Figure 1 and Figure 2 values are figure-only and not read off plots (per rule). Values for Figure 1 and Figure 2 are not tabulated, so the "maximum/minimum" numbers above come from the text only.
- Scope: this paper gives no limits on scalars, pseudoscalars, ALPs, W′, or Higgs-like states. The coloron (G′) is a color-octet spin-1 boson, not a scalar.

## Open issues

- NO HEPData record (JSON endpoint 404). All values come from the arXiv HTML text; the figure-based limits are not tabulated anywhere.
- Observed vs expected: not stated in the text. The paper maps "experimental limits" without saying whether they are observed or expected for each search.
- Confidence level: 95% C.L. stated in the Fig. 1 and Fig. 2 captions only; the text values (0.6, 0.9, 1.5, tan θ bands) carry no CL statement of their own.
- Antecedent of "For that large coupling, the current mass reach is around 2.8 TeV": the text says the plot is not extended above g_B = 2.5 "because" α_B ≈ 0.5, and then gives the 2.8 TeV reach. The antecedent is likely g_B = 2.5 but this is an inference, not stated.
- Table 2 entries for rows other than [9], [10], [16], [17], [19], [23], [24], [25], [4] are not cross-checked against the text.
- Version: the arXiv HTML is the 2014 revision; erratum PRD 90 079901 is cited in INSPIRE. The acknowledgements mention an earlier error in Eq. (3). Whether the erratum changed any numbers quoted here was not checked.
