# 1902.05759 — Measurement of W±Z production cross sections and gauge boson polarisation in pp collisions at √s = 13 TeV with the ATLAS detector

- ATLAS Collaboration, 2019 (arXiv v2 30 Aug 2019). Journal: Eur. Phys. J. C 79 (2019) 535. INSPIRE recid 1720438. HEPData record ins1720438 (exists; polarisation values in Table 24, which is "Data from Table 5 of the paper").
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 36.1 fb⁻¹ (2015 + 2016); data.
- Topology / final state: pp → W±Z → ℓ′ν ℓℓ, with ℓ = e or μ (all four flavour channels eee, eμμ, μee, μμμ summed for polarisation). Polarisation of W and Z measured separately in W+Z, W−Z and W±Z events.
- Selection summary (fiducial, particle level, dressed leptons unless noted):
  - Leptons |η| < 2.5; p_T > 15 GeV (from Z), > 20 GeV (from W); Z-pair mass within 10 GeV of m_Z^PDG (Sec. 3).
  - W transverse mass > 30 GeV; ΔR(ℓ from W, ℓ from Z) > 0.3; ΔR(Z leptons) > 0.2 (Sec. 3).
  - Particle-level jets anti-k_t, R = 0.4, p_T > 25 GeV, |y|/|η| < 4.5 (Sec. 3; used for jet observables only).
  - Detector level: exactly three leptons, same-flavour opposite-charge pair in Z window, third lepton assigned to W; W-lepton p_T > 20 GeV (Sec. 5).
  - Polarisation uses the Born-lepton fiducial phase space (Sec. 11.1, Table 5 caption), "Born leptons" instead of dressed leptons.
- Model or observable definition (frame/convention, as stated):
  - Eq. (1), W: (1/σ) dσ/dcosθ_{ℓ,W} = (3/8) f_L (1 ∓ cosθ)² + (3/8) f_R (1 ± cosθ)² + (3/4) f_0 sin²θ, upper sign W⁺, lower sign W⁻; f_0 + f_L + f_R = 1. Frame: "helicity frame", θ_{ℓ,W} is the decay angle of the charged lepton in the W rest frame relative to the W direction in the WZ centre-of-mass frame (Sec. 11.1, Fig. 7). Fig. 7 caption: negatively charged lepton for W⁻ and positively charged for W⁺.
  - Eq. (2), Z: generalisation with α = 2c_v c_a/(c_v² + c_a²), c_v = −1/2 + 2 sin²θ_W^eff, c_a = −1/2, sin²θ_W^eff = 0.23152. Helicity frame; θ_{ℓ,Z} is the decay angle of the negatively charged lepton in the Z rest frame relative to the Z direction in the WZ CM frame.
  - Fitted parameters: f_0 and (f_L − f_R); f_R (or f_L) is fixed by the constraint f_0 + f_R + f_L = 1 (Sec. 11.1). For W±Z the templates use q_ℓ · cosθ_{ℓ,W}, with q_ℓ the lepton charge, to combine W⁺ and W⁻.
  - Observable is the fit to the lepton angular distributions (binned profile-likelihood), then corrected per helicity fraction to particle level (Sec. 11.1).
  - Z-boson fractions are "effective fractions, containing the small contribution from γ*" (Sec. 11.1).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- The paper says "Table 6 shows the main sources of uncertainty in the measurement of the helicity fractions" and "The measurements are dominated by statistical uncertainties" (Sec. 11.2). Table 6 is the only ranking given; no separate prose ranking is given.
- W f_0: misidentified-lepton background is the largest listed source (0.031 vs total systematic 0.033); statistical 0.06 (Table 6).
- Z f_0: misidentified-lepton background (0.007) is the largest listed source; total systematic 0.009 (Table 6).
- W f_L − f_R: modelling (0.007) is the largest listed source (Table 6).
- Z f_L − f_R: misidentified-lepton background (0.019) is the largest listed source; total systematic 0.024 (Table 6). Statistical 0.15 dominates (Table 6).
- Modelling: the paper considers generator modelling (Powheg+Pythia vs MC@NLO for helicity template shapes; Sec. 11.1) and a reweighting procedural uncertainty below 0.5% (Sec. 11.1).

## Caveats

- Fiducial, particle-level (Born-lepton) values; not extrapolated to total phase space for the polarisation numbers.
- The Z-boson fractions include a small γ* contribution ("effective fractions", Sec. 11.1).
- The W-boson neutrino longitudinal momentum has a twofold ambiguity resolved by choosing the smaller |p_z|, with the real part of complex solutions used when no real solution exists (Sec. 11.1). This directly affects the W measurement.
- Templates are from Powheg+Pythia (LO electroweak, G_μ scheme, sin²θ_W = 0.2229); the Powheg+Pythia f_L − f_R values are corrected using the difference between MCFM calculations with the G_μ scheme and with sin²θ_W^eff = 0.23152 (Sec. 11.2). Equations (1)–(2) are only valid for full lepton phase space, so the simulated templates are used (Sec. 11.1).
- Paper states: "Good agreement ... with the predictions from Powheg+Pythia and MATRIX is observed" and that "no stringent constraints nor clear inconsistencies ... can be deduced" (Sec. 11.2). Predictions are LO electroweak only.
- Uncertainty components in Table 6 are "absolute uncertainties"; per-quantity totals in Table 5/HEPData are total uncertainties.

## Open issues

- Table 6 W f_0 column: components (stat 0.06, syst 0.033) and total 0.06 are copied as printed; I did not verify that they combine to the total (no computation performed, per rule).
- Sec. 12 quotes SM predictions 0.238 ± 0.003 (W) and 0.230 ± 0.003 (Z), while Table 5 Powheg+Pythia gives 0.2376 ± 0.0031 (W±Z, W) and 0.2294 ± 0.0033 (Z). The text values are not identical to the table values and I have not resolved which the paper intends for the comparison.
- The Z-in-W−Z prediction for f_L − f_R (Table 5, +0.052) has the opposite sign from the Z-in-W+Z prediction (−0.297); I copied both as printed and did not check the sign convention beyond the paper text.
- Stat/syst split is given only for the W±Z-combined values (Table 6). For W+Z and W−Z separate values, only the total uncertainty is available (Table 5 / HEPData).
- The HEPData record provides only Table 24 for polarisation; Table 6 (systematics) is not in HEPData and was taken from the arXiv HTML.
- Table 5 values were read from the arXiv HTML and cross-checked against HEPData Table 24 (all 12 measured values agree).
- Significances (Sec. 11.2) are from text only, not HEPData.
