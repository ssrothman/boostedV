# 2005.03799 — Combination of the W boson polarization measurements in top quark decays using ATLAS and CMS data at √s = 8 TeV
- Authors/Collaboration: ATLAS and CMS Collaborations (CMS note TOP-19-004; ATLAS-TOPQ-2018-02; CERN-EP-2020-012), 2020, JHEP 08 (2020) 051, DOI 10.1007/JHEP08(2020)051 (journal ref from INSPIRE metadata, not from the PDF/HTML text), INSPIRE recid 1795022, HEPData record: none (cache file `hepdata/NONE`: "no HEPData record")
- sqrt(s) = 8 TeV, integrated luminosity 20.2 fb^-1 (ATLAS) and 19.7 fb^-1 (CMS) (Sec. 0.2), data (measurement, not projection/recast)
- Topology / final state: single-lepton (e or μ) top-quark decays; ttbar lepton+jets (ATLAS; CMS e+jets and μ+jets entered as two separate inputs) and t-channel single-top-enriched selection (CMS single top). Input W-from-top polarization measured from the semileptonic-top cos θ* distribution.
- Selection summary:
  - ATLAS ttbar: exactly one reconstructed electron or muon, at least four jets, at least two b-tagged jets; anti-kt R = 0.4 jets from calorimeter energy deposits (Sec. 0.2.1, 0.3.2).
  - CMS ttbar (e+jets, μ+jets): one lepton, four or more jets, at least two b-tagged jets; anti-kt R = 0.5 particle-flow jets (Sec. 0.2.2, 0.3.2).
  - CMS single top: exactly one electron or muon, exactly two jets, one b-tagged jet; orthogonal to the ttbar selection (Sec. 0.2.2).
  - pT and |η| thresholds for leptons/jets: not stated in this paper.
  - Inputs: ATLAS (ℓ+jets), CMS (e+jets), CMS (μ+jets), CMS (single top) (Sec. 0.2.3).
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Frame: the paper defines θ* as "the angle between the direction of the charged lepton and the reversed direction of the b quark, both in the rest frame of the W boson" (Sec. 0.1, Eq. (1)). The paper does not use the words "helicity frame" or "Collins–Soper"; the named frame is "not stated" beyond this definition.
  - Distribution (Eq. (1), Sec. 0.1): (1/Γ) dΓ/dcosθ* = (3/4)(1−cos²θ*) F0 + (3/8)(1−cosθ*)² FL + (3/8)(1+cosθ*)² FR.
  - F0, FL, FR are the fractions of W bosons with longitudinal, left-handed, right-handed polarization; unitarity F0 + FL + FR = 1 is imposed, and the combination gives F0 and FL, with FR = 1 − F0 − FL (Sec. 0.1, 0.5). No A0 or ρ00 notation is used.
  - No pT-binned polarization fractions appear in this paper: the results are single inclusive values per input and for the combination (text gives no pT binning).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)
- Stat+bkg is the dominant uncertainty; then radiation and scales, limited size of simulated samples, and simulation model choice (Sec. 0.5: "The dominant uncertainties are those arising from the statistical uncertainty on data and background estimation (stat+bkg), followed by the uncertainties in the radiation and scales modelling, the limited size of the simulated samples, and simulation model choice."). Total detector modelling is stated as smaller than stat+bkg.
- Table 0.5 values above give the category sizes for the combination.
- Mention in the text: the measurement with highest impact on F0 is ATLAS; CMS (μ+jets) dominates the FL determination (Sec. 0.5).

## Caveats
- All numbers are the combination of four published ATLAS/CMS inputs at 8 TeV, not a single-experiment measurement. Input and combined values are from the same paper and are not independent of the inputs.
- Input ATLAS uncertainties were symmetrized before combination; the Sec. 0.2.3 input table lists the values before those modifications (Sec. 0.2.3, Sec. 0.3). The ATLAS top-quark mass uncertainty was changed from ±0.7 GeV to ±1.0 GeV for the combination.
- Stat vs syst definitions differ by input: ATLAS first uncertainty = stat + background determination; CMS first = statistical only, second = total systematic including background (Sec. 0.2.3). The combination reports "stat+bkg" and "syst" (Sec. 0.5). The paper does not give stat-only numbers for the combination.
- Theory: NNLO QCD predictions (Sec. 0.1): "predict the fractions to be F0=0.687±0.005, FL=0.311±0.005, and FR=0.0017±0.0001 [1], assuming a top quark mass of 172.8±1.3 GeV" [verifier w3_1: was "figure-only"]; also shown in Fig. 2.
- Anomalous-coupling limits (Sec. 0.5.2) and Wilson-coefficient limits are outside the Q2 scope and are not extracted.
- The combined FR is from unitarity, not a direct measurement; the paper states it is allowed to be negative (Sec. 0.5).
- Reference frame: see the definition above; no explicit frame name is given in the paper.

## Open issues
- Reference frame: the paper defines θ* in the W rest frame (lepton vs reversed b direction) but does not name the frame (e.g. helicity). Recorded as stated; not interpreted.
- pT binning: none found in this paper; the polarization fractions are inclusive. Not verified against the source PDF (only the arXiv HTML was read).
- Detector vs particle level: the CMS fit uses reconstructed cos θ* distributions (Sec. 0.2.2, Eq. (3)); the ATLAS description does not state the level explicitly ("not stated").
- Table numbering: the input-summary table is referred to as "Table 0.2.3" in the body text and the combination breakdown as "Table 0.5"; the HTML source does not show a separate table number for the input summary beyond these references.
- [resolved, verifier w3_1] NNLO prediction values are in the text (Sec. 0.1), see Caveats.
- Source used: arXiv HTML (https://arxiv.org/html/2005.03799, v2), saved to `.cache/2005.03799/arxiv_html.html` with plain text in `.cache/2005.03799/fulltext.txt`. HEPData: no record. Journal reference taken from INSPIRE metadata (recid 1795022), not from the paper text.
