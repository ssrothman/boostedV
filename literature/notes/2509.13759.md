# 2509.13759 — Measurement of the W-boson angular coefficients and transverse momentum in pp collisions at √s=13 TeV with the ATLAS detector

- ATLAS Collaboration (HEPData first author: Aad, Georges), 2026. Journal: Eur. Phys. J. C 86 (2026) 483, DOI 10.1140/epjc/s10052-025-15264-w. arXiv:2509.13759v2 (07 May 2026); ATLAS-STDM-2020-07; CERN-EP-2025-188. INSPIRE recid 2970689. HEPData record 166079 (inspire 2970689; DOI 10.17182/hepdata.166079.v1), tables t1–t6.
- √s = 13 TeV. Integrated luminosity: `correspond to a total integrated luminosity of $338.1\pm 3.1$` (Sec. 4.1). Data (2017–2018 low pile-up runs); MC used for folding templates and backgrounds.
- Topology / final state: inclusive W±→ℓ±ν, ℓ = e, μ (Drell–Yan W production). Results are for the full decay-lepton phase space; the folding procedure maps reconstructed to particle-level distributions (Sec. 3.2). No jet selection appears in Sec. 4.2.
- Selection summary:
  - Exactly one e or μ, matched to the trigger lepton (Sec. 4.2).
  - Electrons: |η| < 2.47, excluding 1.37 < |η| < 1.52, pT > 25 GeV. Muons: |η| < 2.4, pT > 25 GeV (Sec. 4.2).
  - E_T^miss > 25 GeV and W transverse mass > 50 GeV (Sec. 4.2).
  - Isolation: pT^cone20 / min(pT, 50 GeV) < 0.1 (Sec. 4.2).
  - pT(W) bin edges [0, 8, 17, 27, 40, 55, 75, 110, 150, 210, 600] GeV (Eq. 2). Jet algorithm and R: not applicable (no jets used).
- Model or observable definition (frame and convention):
  - Eq. (1) expands dσ in nine harmonic polynomials in (cosθ, φ) with coefficients A0–A7 and the unpolarised cross-section σ^{U+L}. Angles are in the Collins–Soper (CS) frame (Sec. 3, citing [1]).
  - z-axis: `the external bisector of the angle between the momenta of the two protons` in the W rest frame (Sec. 3).
  - θ_CS is the angle between the negatively charged lepton momentum and the z-axis (Sec. 3). For W+, `there is no negatively charged lepton and instead the direction of the neutrino is used` (Sec. 3).
  - Polarisation fraction definitions (f_L, f_0, f_R, ρ_00): not stated. Sec. 1 says: `These polarisation fraction measurements are related to the $A_{0}$ and $A_{4}$ coefficients`. No relation or value is given here; none is derived.
  - Spin density matrix: qualitative only — Sec. 1 says the decay is "described by a set of nine helicity density matrix elements" and the A_i "represent ratios of helicity cross-sections relative to the unpolarised one, σ^{U+L}"; no explicit ρ_λλ′ map [verifier w2_6].
  - Quantities: A0–A7 (dimensionless, ratios to σ^{U+L}) and dσ/dpT(W) in pb GeV⁻¹. Fig. 17 caption: `The quoted cross-sections include the branching ratio to a single lepton flavour`; the paper does not further define this normalisation.
  - A_i are extracted separately for W− and W+ (Sec. 4).

## Caveats

- Neutrino pz sign ambiguity dilutes A0 and A4 sensitivity: `This effect dilutes the sensitivity of the measurement to the coefficients that directly depend on` (Sec. 3). The fraction of events without a real pz solution is `It is approximately 15% at low` and `rises to around 60% for` (Sec. 3.1).
- Stated significance: `only the coefficients $A_{0}$ , $A_{2}$ , $A_{3}$ , and $A_{4}$ are measured to be significantly different from zero` (Sec. 4). The sensitivity to A1, A5–A7 and A0−A2 is stated to be limited (Sec. 4).
- Post-fit rebinning: `a post-fit rebinning procedure is applied, reducing the number of measurement bins by a factor of two` (Sec. 6).
- First-measurement claim as stated by the paper, not verified here: `This analysis provides the first direct measurement of the $A_{0}$ and $A_{4}$ coefficients` (Sec. 6).
- Random choice of pz solution, equal probability (Sec. 3.1): `one of the two solutions for the neutrino longitudinal momentum is chosen randomly`.
- Strong bin-to-bin anticorrelations, `reaching about $80\%$ negative correlation` at low pT (Sec. 6). The correlation matrices are not in local files (see Open issues).
