# 2403.08547 — Search for low-mass resonances decaying into two jets and produced in association with a photon or a jet at √s = 13 TeV with the ATLAS detector

- Authors/Collaboration: ATLAS Collaboration (HEPData breadcrumb "Aad, Georges et al."); year 2024; journal: Phys. Rev. D 110 (2024) 032002 (arXiv HTML metadata "Phys. Rev. D 110 (2024) 3, 032002"; CERN-EP-2024-044; ATLAS code EXOT-2019-01); INSPIRE recid 2768375; HEPData record 145799 (ins2768375), version 1, 12 limit/fit tables (t1–t12).
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 140 fb⁻¹ (2015–2018) ("The total integrated luminosity of this data sample is 140", Sec. 3); data.
- Topology / final state: pp → ISR (photon or jet) + Z′ → two resolved anti-kt R=0.4 jets; four channels: γjj, γbb, jjj (trijet, inclusive), jbb (trijet, both selected jets b-tagged). Resonance range 200–650 GeV (Abstract). Signal is a leptophobic axial-vector Z′ benchmark (Sec. 3) and a generic Gaussian bump (Sec. 5.1).
- Selection summary:
  - Photon channels: single-photon trigger, "a single photon with pT>150" required for full trigger efficiency; photon pT>25 GeV, |η|<2.5 excluding 1.37–1.52 (Sec. 4.1–4.2).
  - Photon channels: two leading jets (pT>25 GeV, |η|<2.5) (Sec. 4.1) [verifier w2_5]; "A cut of y∗<0.825" (y* = |y1−y2|/2) (Sec. 4.2). γbb: both leading jets DL1r 77% b-tag WP (Sec. 4.1–4.2).
  - Trijet channels: ≥3 jets, leading jet above 475 GeV (Sec. 4.3); the pair with minimum |Δφ| is taken as the Z′ candidate and events are rejected if that pair is the two leading jets; jbb additionally requires that pair to be b-tagged.
  - Fit: 5-parameter function in x = m_jj/√s; fit ranges γjj 200–875, γbb 200–854, jjj 225–1000, jbb 160–700 GeV ("the fit spans 225 GeV to 1000 GeV and 160 GeV to 700 GeV respectively", Sec. 7); binned profile likelihood with 1 GeV bins (Sec. 5).
  - Pile-up: JVT "tight" for pT<60 GeV, |η|<2.4 (Sec. 4.1).
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Z′: s-channel simplified model with "axial-vector interactions" (Sec. 3); dark-matter fermion much heavier than Z′, so Γ(Z′→DM)=0; coupling to non-top quarks g_q = 0.2 in all signal samples (Sec. 3).
  - Observable: 95% CL upper limit on σ×A×BR (HEPData y-axis "σ × A × BR [pb]"; caption σ_Z′×B(Z′→qq̄)×A). "The signal acceptance is defined as the fraction of simulated events at detector level that pass the analysis selection cuts." (Sec. 6). Limits are "interpolated linearly in the log of the cross-section" (Sec. 7).
  - Generic signal: Gaussian shape, mean = resonance mass, widths 5–15% ("with widths ranging from 5% to 15%", Sec. 5.1); HEPData widths 5, 7, 10, 12, 15%.
  - Mass range of limits: templates 250–650 GeV (Sec. 5.2: "masses between 250 GeV and 650 GeV"); Gaussian jbb table also starts at 200 GeV. No HEPData row lies outside 10 GeV–1 TeV. The paper states higher signal masses fail the spurious-signal test: "higher signal masses do not always satisfy the spurious signal tests, and are not considered further" (Sec. 5.2); no limits are reported above 650 GeV.
  - Spin / polarization: Z′ spin-1 (Sec. 1, "spin-1 vector bosons"); axial-vector coupling. No polarization observable (f_L, f_0, A_0, ρ_00, ...) is measured: "not applicable". Reference frame: not applicable.
  - Statistics: CL "modified frequentist-approach" (Sec. 7, ref. [104]), asymptotic approximation; test statistic is profile-likelihood-ratio (Sec. 7).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Statistical and spurious-signal uncertainties dominate: "the dominant uncertainties arise from the statistical and spurious signal uncertainties" (Sec. 7). The spurious-signal term is "together with the statistical uncertainty, the leading source of uncertainty on the extracted number of signal events" (Sec. 6, "Background modeling"). Implemented as S_modeling × θ_modeling (Sec. 6 equation).
- Luminosity: 0.83%, "treated as a single normalization uncertainty applied as a scale factor to the signal models" (Sec. 6).
- Jet energy scale: "The most significant source of uncertainty in the JES originates from uncertainties on the flavor of the jet." Effects: photon channels ~2% on mass location; trijet channels ~1% on mass location and ~3% on selection efficiency ("the impact on the selection efficiency is around 3%", Sec. 6).
- b-tagging: "ranges from 2–3%" on the signal selection efficiency (Sec. 6).
- PDF (NNPDF2.3lo, signal only): "This uncertainty affects signal samples and ranges from 1–5% across the different channels" (Sec. 6).
- Photon energy scale and resolution: "with a total impact on the efficiency of around 2%" (Sec. 6); the photon ID/isolation sentence gives no number [verifier w2_5].
- Signal-shape systematics: "Systematic uncertainties affecting the signal shape have only a small impact on the result" (Sec. 7).
- No per-source numbers for the limits are tabulated; I did not combine any of these.

## Caveats

- Limits are on σ×A×BR for the stated model and detector-level acceptance A; they are not on σ×BR. The Z′ templates are for g_q = 0.2 (non-top) and "a sample is adequate to also model the shape of signals with slightly different or smaller gq values" (Sec. 3) — a shape claim, not a cross-section scaling.
- Expected bands (1σ, 2σ) are "the (darker band) 1σ and (lighter band) 2σ uncertainty range" around the expected limit (Fig. 5 and 6 captions). The paper does not split their stat / syst content.
- Local excesses reported by BumpHunter (not limits, Sec. 7): "found at 348 GeV with a local significance of 1.9" (γjj); 380 GeV 1.8σ (γbb); 404 GeV 1.8σ (jjj); 522 GeV 1.5σ (jbb). Global significances "are computed from background-only pseudo-experiments", but their values are not stated in the text.
- Signal samples are LO MadGraph + Pythia8 (A14) (Sec. 3). Limits on g_q (Sec. 7) rely on the simulated-sample cross-sections and acceptance; the theory cross-section is not in HEPData.
- Background is a data-driven 5-parameter fit; spurious-signal test requirement S_spur/σ_spur < 0.5 is satisfied for "masses between 250 GeV and 650 GeV" (Sec. 5.2). The jbb channel passes "for resonance masses down to 200 GeV" (Sec. 5.2); the 200 and 225 GeV rows in the jbb Gaussian table are therefore the only sub-250 GeV limit points.

## Open issues

1. Obs vs. exp mapping: HEPData labels the second member of each pair "Fit Z", "Fit Z`" or "Fit Z'" (template tables) or "Fit σ_G/M_G = x%" (Gaussian tables), not "expected". I mapped it to expected because the Fig. 5/6 captions say expected limits are dashed with 1σ/2σ bands around them, and those bands are stored on exactly these rows. This is inferred, not stated in the HEPData file; confirm before using the obs/exp split.
2. x-axis labels in HEPData are inconsistent: t5 and t6 say "m_jj [GeV]", t7 says "m_Z`", t8 says "m_Z'", and the Gaussian tables say "m_G". The Fig. 5/6 captions say "as a function of the Z′ mass" (and Gaussian mass m_G). I took the x column as the Z′ (or m_G) mass per the captions.
3. t9 (γjj Gaussian) has an extra header entry "$$ [pb]" (four headers for three columns of data). Its 10 y-groups carry the same labels as the other Gaussian tables, so I used them, but the header is malformed.
4. Version: the arXiv HTML fetched is labelled v3 (footer "arXiv:2403.08547v3 [hep-ex] 04 Sep 2026"), journal reference PRD 110 (2024) 032002; HEPData record is version 1. I did not verify that HEPData corresponds to v3.
5. g_q limits (Figs. 7, 8) are figure-only, not tabulated; no per-mass values recorded.
6. The 1σ/2σ band type (stat / syst / both) is not stated; recorded as "expected band" only.
7. Dominant systematics: the paper gives qualitative and few numeric values; no table of per-source impacts exists in HEPData (12 tables, none of them systematic breakdowns).
8. Background-only fit tables (t1–t4) and the BumpHunter results are not limit data and were not extracted, except the four local significances quoted in Caveats.
9. Full text obtained from arxiv.org/html/2403.08547 into `.cache/2403.08547/arxiv_html.html` and `.cache/2403.08547/fulltext.txt` (LaTeX alt-text is duplicated in places, so prose quotes were matched against the rendered text).
