# 1710.00159 — Search for low mass vector resonances decaying into quark-antiquark pairs in proton-proton collisions at sqrt(s) = 13 TeV

- CMS Collaboration (Sirunyan, A. M. et al.), 2017 (arXiv v2 Jan 2018); JHEP 01 (2018) 097; CMS-EXO-17-001, CERN-EP-2017-235; INSPIRE recid 1628094; HEPData record: none (https://www.hepdata.net/record/ins1628094 returns 404; HEPData search for arXiv:1710.00159 returns 0 hits)
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 35.9 fb^-1 (2016 data, Sec. 0.1 and Abstract); measured data
- Topology / final state: a leptophobic vector resonance Z′ → qq̄ merged into one AK8 jet with two-prong substructure, recoiling against a high-pT ISR jet; fully hadronic. Z′ mass 50–300 GeV.
- Selection summary:
  - Trigger/offline: at least one AK8 jet with pT > 500 GeV and |η| < 2.5 (Sec. 0.3.2)
  - Lepton veto: electrons, muons, taus with pT > 10 GeV and |η| < 2.5, 2.4, 2.3 removed (Sec. 0.3.2)
  - Jets: anti-kT, R = 0.8 (AK8); soft-drop grooming zcut = 0.1, β = 0; the mass variable is mSD (Sec. 0.3.2)
  - Substructure: −5.5 < ρ < −2.0 with ρ = ln(mSD²/pT²); N21,DDT < 0 (fixed 5% QCD efficiency); fit in five pT bins with boundaries 500, 600, 700, 800, 900, 1000 GeV (Sec. 0.3.2, 0.6)
  - Fit: binned maximum likelihood on mSD in passing and failing regions; CLs with profile-likelihood test statistic, asymptotic approximation; nuisance parameters log-normal and profiled (Sec. 0.6)
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Observable: production cross section of narrow Z′ peak in mSD; limits converted to quark coupling g_q′ via g_q′ = g_B/6, convention of Ref. [37] (Dobrescu & Yu, arXiv:1306.2629), as stated in Sec. 0.6 ("gq′=gB/6.").
  - Simplified DM model (Sec. 0.6, Fig. 8): vector mediator coupling only to quarks (universal g_q = 0.25) and DM (g_DM = 1.0); BR(Z′→qq̄) = 100% assumed for the leptophobic Z′ case (Fig. 8 caption).
  - Reference frame / polarization quantities: not applicable (no spin-density or angular observables in this paper); "not stated" for any frame definition.

## Caveats

- Mass range is 50–300 GeV only (Abstract, Sec. 0.6, Sec. 0.7). It does not cover the 10 GeV–1 TeV range of Q1 below 50 GeV or above 300 GeV.
- The text states "The results obtained for masses from 50 to 100 GeV represent the first direct limits to be published in this range." (Sec. 0.7). Recorded as the paper's own claim; not checked against other papers.
- Sec. 0.6 states a local excess at 2.9 σ near 115 GeV and a global 2.2 σ; no limit is quoted in the text as affected.
- The DM simplified-model limits use g_q = 0.25 and g_DM = 1.0 only (Sec. 0.6, Fig. 8). The paper states that axial-vector limits differ little and are not shown.
- Fig. 8 blue contours (dijet resolved search, early 2016 data, Ref. [35]) are figure-only.
- Recast/projection status: this is a measured result (not recast or projected).
- Particle vs detector level: the paper's limits are on the reconstructed (detector-level, with PF jets and AK8 soft-drop mass) distribution; signal efficiency uses Geant4 simulation (Sec. 0.3.1). No unfolding is stated.
