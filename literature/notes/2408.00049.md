# 2408.00049 — Search for boosted low-mass resonances decaying into hadrons produced in association with a photon in pp collisions at √s = 13 TeV with the ATLAS detector

- ATLAS Collaboration, 2025 (published); preprint arXiv:2408.00049 (CERN-EP-2024-188), 2024. Journal: JHEP 01 (2025) 099, DOI 10.1007/JHEP01(2025)099 (INSPIRE). INSPIRE recid 2813982. HEPData record ins2813982 (hepdata.153849.v1), exists and used for all numbers below.
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 140 fb⁻¹ (2015–2018 data), Sec. 3. Measured data, not projection.
- Topology / final state: γ + (large-R TAR jet from Z′→qq̄), i.e. q q̄ → γ + Z′ → γ + qq̄ (ISR photon). Hadronic resonance, reconstructed as one jet. Z′ width "well below the detector resolution" (Sec. 3) [verifier w1_1].
- Selection summary (Sec. 4–5):
  - Single-photon trigger; leading photon pT > 150 GeV (Sec. 5). Photons pT > 10 GeV, |η| < 1.37 or 1.52–2.37 (Sec. 4).
  - TAR jets: R = 1.0 reclustered from R = 0.2 jets, track-assisted, |η| < 2, pT,J > 150 GeV, 2 mJ/pT,J < 1, Δφ(J, γ) > π/2, ΔR(J, γ) ≥ 1.2 with pTγ/pTJ > 0.1 (Sec. 4). Jets with fewer than three rescaled tracks rejected.
  - Veto: any R = 0.2 jet within ΔR ≤ 1.2 of the leading TAR jet with pT > pTγ; ρ ≤ −5.4 rejected (Sec. 5).
  - Tagged region: D2_DDT < 0 (fixed 13% non-resonant efficiency, Eq. 1); central (|ηγ| ≤ 1.3) is the signal region, forward (|ηγ| > 1.3) and anti-tagged regions are control regions (Sec. 5).
  - Hypothesised Z′ mass range 20–200 GeV (Sec. 3) [verifier w1_1]; no m_J fit range is stated [verifier w1R_a]; search mJ range 20–100 GeV in the Conclusion (Sec. 8).
- Model or observable definition (Sec. 3, 5, 7):
  - Vector Z′ with Z′qq̄ coupling g_q = 0.2 for benchmark (u, d, s, c, b), LHC DM WG benchmark. "The rates for all decay modes except Z′ → qq̄ were set to 0, and the interference between the Z′ and the SM Z boson was neglected" (Sec. 3).
  - Limit is on g_q (dimensionless) at 95% CL, CLs with profile likelihood ratio test statistic (Sec. 7).
  - Observable: large-R TAR jet invariant mass mJ, binned at about one-third of the mZ′ experimental resolution (Sec. 6).
  - Frame / polarization: not applicable (no spin-density or polarization quantity measured). Spin-1 (vector) only.
  - Other couplings: the paper does not give a limit for a Z′ with non-zero BRs to other channels; "The translation of the results to other g q values is done following Ref." (Sec. 3) — this translation is not tabulated here.

Text statements (Sec. 7, Sec. 8):
- "In the range m Z ′ < 100 GeV, hitherto unprobed by ATLAS, g q couplings as small as 0.1 are excluded." (Sec. 7; consistent with HEPData: observed g_q limits at 60 and 70 GeV are below 0.1, and the Conclusion states "excluding g q couplings down to 0.1").
- Exclusion method: "exclusion limits at 95% confidence level (CL) on the g q coupling strength with the C L s formalism" (Sec. 7).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Non-resonant background estimate (data-driven κ_D2DDT(mJ) fit, Bernstein order 5): "The uncertainty in the non-resonant background estimate represents the dominant systematic uncertainty in this analysis, contributing between 80% and 90% of the total systematic uncertainty, depending on the m Z ′ hypothesis." (Sec. 6). Within it, the finite data/MC statistics term contributes "more than 90%" of the non-resonant background uncertainty (Sec. 6).
- Signal and resonant-background MC theory: factorisation/renormalisation scales, PDFs, α_s (Sec. 6, listed without numbers).
- Jet energy scale and resolution for R = 0.2 and R = 0.4 jets: sub-leading (Sec. 6).
- Calibration R_D2DDT = 0.971 ± 0.026 propagated to the fit (Sec. 5). Not a numeric systematic on the limit as stated.
- Photon identification/reconstruction, MC statistics, luminosity: "typically negligible" (Sec. 6).
- Limit values carry no stat/syst split in HEPData. The paper states: "Overall, this search is limited by statistical uncertainties in the data, which typically range between 20% and 50% of the theoretical cross section for the Z′ → qq̄ process, depending on m Z′." (Sec. 6).

## Caveats

- Mass range covered: 20–200 GeV for the Z′ hypothesis (Sec. 3, Abstract) [verifier w1_1]. The paper does not cover 10–20 GeV or 200 GeV–1 TeV. This is a partial match to the requested 10 GeV–1 TeV range.
- Model dependence: limits are on g_q for a DM-WG benchmark with all other Z′ decay rates set to zero and Z′–Z interference neglected (Sec. 3). The translation to other g_q / other BR assumptions follows "Ref." [73] and is not tabulated here.
- Production is γ + Z′ (ISR photon) with Z′ → qq̄; the limit depends on this production mode, not a dijet-only search. Z′ width stated as "well below the detector resolution" (Sec. 3/4).
- Signal simulated at LO (MadGraph5_aMC@NLO v2.9.2, Pythia 8.244) (Sec. 3). Limits are on reconstructed TAR-jet mass; the paper does not give particle-level (unfolded) results.
- Measured (140 fb⁻¹ data), not recast or projected. Observed vs expected limits are both given in HEPData; the expected ±1σ/±2σ bands are figure-only (Fig. 5), not tabulated.
- The σ×B×A HEPData table is in the publication auxiliary material (Fig. 2), with the same stated benchmark; its unit is not stated in HEPData or the text.
- No spin/parity other than vector is probed. No scalar, pseudoscalar, ALP, Higgs-like, or W′ limits in this paper.
