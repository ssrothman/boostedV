# 2507.03500 — Measurements and interpretations of W±Z production cross-sections in pp collisions at √s = 13 TeV with the ATLAS detector

- ATLAS Collaboration (first author "Aad, Georges"), 2025, JHEP 11 (2025) 006, INSPIRE recid 2943646, HEPData record 166315 (DOI 10.17182/hepdata.166315.v1), 38 tables
- √s = 13 TeV; ∫L = 140 fb⁻¹ (2015–2018 data); data
- Topology / final state: pp → W±Z → ℓ′ν ℓ⁺ℓ′⁻ with ℓ, ℓ′ = e, μ (fiducial, dressed level; also Born-level comparisons in HEPData)
- Selection summary (from the text, Sec. 4):
  - Leptons: μ p_T > 15 GeV, |η| < 2.5; electron p_T > 15 GeV; W-associated lepton p_T > 20 GeV, tight ID
  - W transverse mass > 30 GeV
  - Jets: anti-k_t, R = 0.4, p_T > 25 GeV, |η| < 4.5
  - Fourth-lepton veto (loose lepton, p_T > 5 GeV)
- Observable definitions (as stated in the paper):
  - φ*_W and φ*_Z: "the azimuthal angle of the charged (negatively charged) lepton resulting from the W (Z) boson decay between each boson decay plane and the x−z plane in the centre-of-mass of the diboson system", defined following Ref. [113] (Panico, Riva, Wulzer). Sec. 8.3.
  - Triple products p⊥(A,B,C) = p_A · (p_B × p_C)/|p_B × p_C| (Eq. 1), proposed in Ref. [114] (Degrande, Touchèque), used as CP-violation observables. Sec. 8.3.
  - cos θ*_ℓW and cos θ*_ℓZ: "the cosines of the decay angle of the charged lepton in the W or Z rest frame relative to the W or Z direction in the W±Z centre-of-mass frame [16]". Used only as BDT inputs (Table 7), not measured.
  - Polarisation fractions (f_L, f_0, f_±, A_i, ρ_λλ′): not defined and not measured in this paper.

## Scope finding

This paper does not measure any Q2 or Q3 quantity. Full-text search (terms: polari*, helicity, f_L, f_0, A_0–A_7, ρ, density matrix, interference, entangl*, Bell, Collins) and all 38 HEPData table titles/descriptions show no measured W or Z polarisation fraction and no measured spin-density-matrix or angular-coefficient element. Polarisation measurements are cited as prior ATLAS work (Refs. [15]–[17]).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

Not applicable: no Q2/Q3 measurement. For the cross-sections in the paper, the HEPData description states that uncertainties are given as total, with the systematic breakdown in the table headers (e.g. Table 1 description: "the first systematic uncertainty is the combined systematic uncertainty excluding luminosity uncertainty, the second is the modelling uncertainty, the third is luminosity uncertainty").

## Caveats

- The "Sec. 1" and "Sec. 9.2" quotes refer to prior ATLAS work and to an EFT-discriminant study, not to a polarisation measurement in this paper.
- Triple-product and φ* observables are CP-odd probes of anomalous WWZ couplings (Sec. 8.3, Sec. 9.3). They are not presented as spin-density-matrix elements in this paper, and the paper does not map them to ρ_λλ′ or to angular coefficients.
- The paper's Sec. 1 points to Refs. [15]–[17] for polarisation work: [15] = ATLAS, Eur. Phys. J. C 79 (2019) 535 (WZ cross sections and gauge boson polarisation, 13 TeV, 36 fb⁻¹); [16] = ATLAS, Phys. Lett. B 843 (2023) 137895 (joint polarisation); [17] = ATLAS, Phys. Rev. Lett. 133 (2024) 101802 (energy dependence of polarisation fractions). These are references, not extracted here.

## Open issues

- Borderline Q3: φ*_W and φ*_Z are single azimuthal angles in the diboson frame, defined following Ref. [113]. The paper does not state that they are spin-density-matrix elements. Whether they count for Q3 is a scope decision for the lead. If in scope, HEPData Tables 23 and 25 would need extraction; this note does not extract them.
- The paper's HEPData tables are cross-sections only, so no cached HEPData value was needed for Q2/Q3.
- Sec. 8.3 and Sec. 9.3 refer to Figure 8 and Table 7 values; no numbers were read from figures.
- Full text was obtained from https://arxiv.org/html/2507.03500 (HTML). The PDF was not used.
