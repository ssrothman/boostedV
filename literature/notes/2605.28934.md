# 2605.28934 — Bump Hunting Inside Jets with Energy Correlators
- Authors: Lorenzo Ricci, Marc Riembau, Minho Son (theory, no collaboration); 2026; preprint (arXiv:2605.28934, CERN-TH-2026-116); INSPIRE recid 3162400; HEPData: none (ins3162400 returns 404)
- sqrt(s): 13 TeV (CMS photon channel, Sec. III and App. 1; CMS dijet channel, App. 3). Integrated luminosity: 36.3 fb^-1 baseline for the dijet recast (Sec. III "Learning from the CMS measurement"); 300 fb^-1 and 3 ab^-1 are projections (App. 3, Eq. 3.13 text). Photon-channel luminosity: not stated in this paper.
- Data or simulation/projection: simulation only. Parton-level MadGraph + Pythia 8 signal and background; all limits are projected (Asimov, SM = data), with no experimental data analysed. The CMS limit in Fig. 3 is taken from CMS (ref. [49]) and is not re-derived here.
- Topology / final state: Z' → qq̄ as a single boosted jet recoiling against a photon (pp → Z'γ, Sec. III) or against a hard jet (pp → Z'j, Sec. III "Learning from the CMS measurement"). Particle level.
- Selection summary:
  - Photon channel: exactly one photon, p_T(γ) > 200 GeV, |η_γ| < (value garbled in text conversion; see Open issues); anti-k_T R = 0.8 (AK8); leading jet p_Tj > 200 GeV (Sec. III, App. 1).
  - Photon channel, EEC binned in jet p_T in 100 GeV steps up to 600 GeV plus overflow (Sec. III).
  - Dijet channel: anti-k_T R = 0.4 (AK4), p_T > 97 GeV, |η| < 2.1, |Δφ(j1,j2)| > 2.0 rad (Eq. 3.6) [verifier w1_1: was |η|<2.0], seven p_T bins; 0.002 ≤ x_L ≤ 0.35 (App. 3).
  - Optional jet-mass window ±20% of m_Z' (Sec. III, Fig. 3 "long-dashed pink").
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Model (Eq. 3): L_int = Σ_q g'_q q̄ Z̸' q, Z' a hadrophilic vector of mass m_Z'; couplings are to quarks only. The paper states "its transverse production fixes the spinning coefficient to c^(2)=1/2 with all higher coefficients vanishing."
  - Observable: two-point energy correlator (EEC), z' (boosted-frame angle) normalised to z_*; z_* ≡ m_X^2/E^2 (Sec. II).
  - Frame: boosted frame of X. The paper states "For helicity eigenstates the boost axis coincides with the spin quantization axis of X." The spin-quantization-axis convention is not further specified.
  - Polarization quantities (f_L, f_0, A_0, ρ_00 etc.): none used in this paper; the paper uses spinning coefficients c^(j) instead. Definition of c^(j) is given in Sec. II and refers to [46]; no helicity-fraction definitions are stated.

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)
- Z'γ channel (Sec. III, "Associated production with a photon"; App. 1): the paper states "we don't include any systematic uncertainty" and that the sensitivity is "currently limited by statistics."
- Z'j channel (Sec. III, "Learning from the CMS measurement"): "The CMS analysis lists 9 sources of experimental systematic uncertainty, including scale variations, pileup, and unfolding, and 6 sources of theoretical uncertainty, including PDF and α_s variations." Systematics are fully correlated across bins (Σ^ab = σ^a σ^b). "At the current luminosity of 36.3 fb^-1, these systematics are comparable in size to the statistical uncertainties and their inclusion has a noticeable impact on the reach, as shown in Fig. 4."
- App. 3 (Eqs. 3.11–3.13) lists the nine experimental sources (JES, JER, pileup, prefiring, charged/neutral/photon energy scale, track efficiency, unfolding) and six theory sources (parton-shower scales, hard-scattering scale, tune, CNS, PDF, PDF α_s).
- Each asymmetric uncertainty is symmetrised (σ = (σ+ + σ-)/2) (App. 3).

## Caveats
- Projection only: no experimental data are analysed; the recast assumes the CMS measurement coincides with the SM (Asimov).
- The paper says the Z'j channel "is not directly optimized for new physics searches" and that "our signal ECs are at particle level; detector granularity and smearing effects ... may degrade the sensitivity."
- The paper states that CMS α_s-range choices differ: it uses 0.002 ≤ x_L ≤ 0.35 versus CMS 20 GeV/p_T,j ≲ x_L ≲ 0.2, "at the cost of a less controlled theoretical description" (App. 3).
- The Z'γ recast uses soft-drop mass and the CMS selection; the paper states the CMS shape cut "introduces the artificial violation of the scaling" (Sec. III).
- Paper is a phenomenology proposal. For experimental limits this paper cites (CMS 1905.10331, CMS 1710.00159, ATLAS 2408.00049), values must be taken from those papers, not from this note.

- The "O(0.1)" statement for g'_q (Sec. III) has no source, CL, mass, or observed/expected label. Recorded as stated; not a limit.
- Mass range of the Fig. 3 and Fig. 4 curves is not stated in text. The ~100 GeV value is the paper's targeting statement, not a stated range of the plots.
- Photon-channel integrated luminosity is not stated in this paper (only 13 TeV). The 36.3 fb^-1 value belongs to the Z'j recast.
- The photon-channel pseudorapidity cut |η_γ| and the photon |η| text were garbled by the HTML/MathML conversion (the alttext ends in "$|\eta_{\gamma}|"). Not transcribed; check Sec. III directly if needed.
