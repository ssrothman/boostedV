# 1103.5699 — First Measurement of the Angular Coefficients of Drell-Yan e+e- pairs in the Z Mass Region from pp̄ Collisions at √s = 1.96 TeV

- Authors/Collaboration: CDF Collaboration (T. Aaltonen et al.), 2011, Phys. Rev. Lett. 106, 241801 (2011) (journal ref from arXiv abstract page via WebFetch, not checked against the PRL itself; see Open issues). INSPIRE recid 894312. HEPData record: none (prefetched file `.cache/1103.5699/hepdata/NONE`).
- sqrt(s) = 1.96 TeV; integrated luminosity 2.1 fb⁻¹; CDF II data (no simulation-only result; PYTHIA + GEANT used for the detector-level templates). Location: Abstract; p.4 ("The sample used corresponds to an integrated luminosity of 2.1 fb⁻¹").
- Topology / final state: Drell-Yan p p̄ → γ*/Z → e+e− + X, Z mass region, with e+e− pair pT.
- Selection summary:
  - Mass window 66 < M_ee < 116 GeV/c² (p.4, text and Fig. 1 caption).
  - Vertex |z| < 60 cm (PDF p.4); central electrons |η| < 1.1, plug 1.2 < |η| < 2.8 (p.5) [verifier wR_a].
  - Leading electron E_T > 25 GeV (CC, PP), > 20 GeV (CP); second electron E_T > 15 GeV (CC), > 25 GeV (PP), > 20 GeV (CP) (p.4–5).
  - Both electrons must have an associated SVX track; sample about 140 000 events (p.5).
  - Backgrounds: QCD dijets 0.3%; electroweak (WW, WZ, W/Z+jets, Z→ττ) and tt̄ 0.2% (p.5). No jet algorithm (no jets in the analysis).
- Model or observable definition (frame and convention as stated by the paper):
  - Frame: Collins–Soper (CS). Ref. [3], p.7 [verifier w2_6: not a p.4 footnote]: "The CS frame is the center of mass of the γ*/Z, where the z axis is defined as the bisector of the p and p̄ beams. The polar angle is defined as the angle of the positive lepton with respect to the proton direction." (p.4 fn 3)
  - Eq. (1): dσ/dcosθ ∝ (1 + cos²θ) + ½A₀(1 − 3cos²θ) + A₄cosθ (p.4)
  - Eq. (2): dσ/dφ ∝ 1 + β₃cosφ + β₂cos2φ + β₇sinφ + β₅sin2φ, with β₃ = 3πA₃/16, β₂ = A₂/4, β₇ = 3πA₇/16, β₅ = A₅/4 (p.4).
  - Quote (p.4): "The A0 and A4 are extracted from Eq. 1 and A2 and A3 are extracted from Eq. 2 while A5 and A7 are expected to be zero."
  - Polarization fractions (f_L, f_0, ρ₀₀): not stated; the paper does not use these symbols (full text read; no "polarization" term appears).
  - Extraction (p.5): "The angular distributions from the re-weighted MC events are compared to the data in the reconstructed level and the angular coefficients which give a maximum log-likelihood value are determined as the best coefficients to describe the data." Level: reconstructed (detector) level. Unfolding: not stated.

Notes on the table:
- Q2 (polarization fraction f_L, f_0, A_0 as polarization, ρ₀₀): no quantity found in this paper. A₀ here is the angular coefficient in Eq. (1), not a polarization fraction, and the paper does not relate it to f_0 or f_L. Recorded as "not measured".
- Q3 (more than one spin-density element): A0, A2, A3, A4 are measured (p.4 quote: "we report on the first measurement of the angular coefficients A0, A2, A3 and A4, for pp̄ → γ*/Z → e+e− + X events in the Z boson mass region (66 < Mee < 116 GeV/c²)"). A5 and A7 are not measured. The paper does not give ρ_λλ′ or any off-diagonal density-matrix element.
- Correlation between extracted coefficients (p.5): "The correlation between extracted values of A0 and A2, A3 and A4 is negligible." No correlation matrix is given.
- Theory expectation (p.4, Eq. 3, annihilation): "A0 = A2 = P_T²/(M²_{e+e−} + P_T²)", stated for qq̄ → γ*/Z G at order αs. Compton (Eq. 4): A0 = A2 ≈ 5P_T²/(M²+5P_T²), averaged over rapidity (p.4).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Sources considered (p.5): "The systematic uncertainties originating from backgrounds, electron identification efficiency, SVX tracking efficiency, boson P_T and rapidity modeling, and modeling of detector material are considered."
- Dominant source (p.5): "The dominant source is the background estimate."
- Effect on shape (p.5): "the effect of these uncertainties on the shape of the angular distribution is small."
- Per-source sizes are not given; only the combined per-bin syst column in Table I (values above).

## Caveats

- Measurement is of A0–A4 at reconstructed level, fitted to PYTHIA (tuned) templates reweighted in A0–A4 (p.5). Model dependence through the MC is not separately quantified in the text beyond the statements above.
- Lam–Tung relation A0 − A2 = 0 is tested at high pT, with combined uncertainties (p.6). Ref. [11] (p.7) gives the Gottfried–Jackson-frame value: "A0 − A2 ≈ −2 in the Gottfried-Jackson frame" (citing Arteaga-Romero et al., PRL 52, 172 (1984)) [verifier w2_6]. The paper's own A0, A2 are in the CS frame.
- Abstract (printed p.3): "at high values of the transverse momentum, Z bosons are produced via quark anti-quark annihilation and quark-gluon Compton processes" [verifier wR_a: qualifier restored; wording as reported by the verifier]; p.6: "both the annihilation and Compton processes contribute to the cross section"; p.4: the measurements "indicate that there is a significant (≈ 30%) contribution of the Compton process to the production of γ*/Z bosons at the Tevatron" (no uncertainty given) [verifier w2_6].
- Statistics-limited (p.6): "The measurements presented here are statistically limited."
- Fig. 3 values are used only for orientation; no value is read from the figure.
