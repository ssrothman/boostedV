# 1906.08589 — Search for diboson resonances in hadronic final states in 139 fb-1 of pp collisions at sqrt(s)=13 TeV with the ATLAS detector

- Collaboration: ATLAS (HEPData breadcrumb "Aad, Georges et al."); analysis HDBS-2018-31; journal ref JHEP 09 (2019) 091 (arXiv v3 header; header date 11 Jun 2020, see Open issues); INSPIRE recid 1740685; HEPData record ins1740685, DOI 10.17182/hepdata.91052.v2 (local copy in `.cache/1906.08589/hepdata`, 6 limit tables plus 4 acceptance x efficiency tables).
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 139 fb-1; data. Signal samples are MC (MadGraph 2.2.2 + Pythia 8.186, LO NNPDF 2.3, Sec. 4.2). Quote (Sec. 3): "the integrated luminosity was 3.2 fb-1 in 2015, 33.0 fb-1 in 2016, 44.3 fb-1 in 2017 and 58.5 fb-1 in 2018."
- Topology / final state: fully hadronic. Two large-R jets, each identified as a W or Z boson (qq̄ qq̄-type final state from V'->WZ/WW, G_KK->WW/ZZ, radion->WW/ZZ). Search range starts at m_JJ > 1.3 TeV (Table 1).
- Selection summary (Sec. 5.1, Sec. 6, Table 1):
  - Jets: anti-kt, R = 1.0 jets from combined and neutral Track-CaloClusters (Sec. 5.1; quote: "This analysis uses anti-kt, R=1.0 jets reconstructed from both the combined and the neutral TCCs.").
  - Lepton veto: no e or mu with pT > 25 GeV and |eta| < 2.5 (Table 1).
  - Pre-selection (Table 1): >= 2 large-R jets with |eta| < 2.0 and mass > 50 GeV; pT1 > 500 GeV and pT2 > 200 GeV; m_JJ > 1.3 TeV.
  - Topology (Table 1): |Δy| < 1.2 and A = (pT1 − pT2)/(pT1 + pT2) < 0.15; boson tag with D2, n_trk and a W or Z mass window; five non-exclusive signal regions (Sec. 6, quote: "the data is categorised into five non-exclusive signal regions (SRs)").
  - Background: parametric dijet fit, Eq. (1), in 100 GeV bins (Sec. 7).
- Model or observable definition (frame/convention):
  - Observable: m_JJ spectrum; signal strength mu scales the predicted signal normalisation; limits are on sigma x B to diboson final states (Sec. 9.2; Fig. 9 caption: "cross-section times branching ratio"). Whether the quoted limit includes acceptance x efficiency is not stated.
  - Radion (spin 0, gg fusion): quote "In this analysis, the curvature factor is set to kπrc=35, and ΛR=3 TeV is used." (Sec. 4.1).
  - HVT (spin 1, q qbar annihilation, V' = W', Z' degenerate): Model A g_V = 1, quote "Model A with the strength of the vector-boson interaction gV=1"; Model B g_V = 3, quote "Model B with gV=3 is representative of composite Higgs models" (Sec. 4.1). Coupling is g_V, not g_q.
  - Bulk RS graviton G_KK (spin 2, gg fusion): quote "a Kaluza–Klein graviton, GKK, predominately produced via gluon–gluon fusion" (Sec. 4.1); curvature k/M̄Pl = 1 (Fig. 9 caption).
  - Polarisation: no polarisation observable is measured or extracted. Signal samples: quote "the WW and ZZ bosons are primarily longitudinally polarised" (Sec. 4.2). Reference frame and polarisation-fraction definitions: not stated (not applicable).
  - Statistics (Sec. 9.2): quote "The analysis follows the frequentist approach with a test statistic based on the profile-likelihood ratio". Limits: quote "Limits at 95% confidence level (CL) on the production cross-section times branching fraction to diboson final states for the benchmark signals are set with sampling distributions generated using pseudo-experiments." The text does not name CLs; the HEPData observable label is "CLS".

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Dominant signal uncertainties: quote "The dominant sources of uncertainty in the signal modelling arise from uncertainties in the large-R jet tagging efficiency and the jet pT calibration." (Sec. 8). The paper adds that these two, plus the fitted background, are "the only ones significantly affecting the statistical results" (Sec. 8).
- W/Z tagging vs background modelling vs jet pT scale: quote "The uncertainty in the W/Z-tagging efficiency is dominant at lower masses, while the uncertainty in the background modelling has largest impact at high masses." (Sec. 9.2). Quote: "Uncertainties in the jet pT scale are at the percent level but are subordinate across the full mass range." (Sec. 9.2).
- Jet pT scale (J pT S): quote "is between 2.5% and 5% for the full mass range." (Sec. 8).
- Boson-tagging efficiency scale factor: quote "corrected by 0.85−0.21+0.23 per event with the boson-tagging efficiency scale-factor" (Sec. 8); the paper states the scale factor as s_Tag = 0.92 ± 0.13 (Sec. 6.2; the breakdown 0.92 ± 0.04 (stat) ± 0.02 (closure) ± 0.03 (tt̄) ± 0.02 (fit) ± 0.05 (pT range) ± 0.10 (theory) is also given there).
- Luminosity: quote "The uncertainty in the combined 2015–2018 integrated luminosity is 1.7%" (Sec. 8).
- PDF: quote "A constant 1% uncertainty is applied in the case of the RS and radion models, and a pole-mass-dependent uncertainty ranging from 1%–12% is applied in the case of the HVT model." (Sec. 8).
- Signal acceptance (generator level): quote "constant uncertainties of 3% (5%) are applied for the HVT (RS and radion) models." (Sec. 8).
- Per-point limit uncertainties are not tabulated; the paper gives only the qualitative ranking above. HEPData error fields are hidden (symerror 0).

## Caveats

- Mass range: HEPData grids for the V' and G_KK limits run from 1.3 to 5.0 TeV; all limits are at m >= 1.3 TeV (HEPData grids and Table 3). The paper gives no limit below 1.3 TeV, so nothing falls in the 10 GeV–1 TeV window. The lower edge is set by the m_JJ > 1.3 TeV requirement (Table 1).
- Mass grids are sparse and non-uniform (see the HEPData tables). No interpolation was done.
- Radion limits are tabulated only at 2.0, 3.0, 4.0, 5.0 TeV (Table 2, not in HEPData). Table 3 gives an excluded range down to 1.3 TeV that has no tabulated limit values.
- Observed vs expected: the expected-limit background hypothesis is not stated explicitly in the text.
- Signal samples are LO; "all hadronic decays were imposed at the generator level" (Sec. 4.2). Predicted sigma x B values in Sec. 4.1 are signal predictions, not limits, and are not transcribed except the Table 2 prediction column.
- HEPData acceptance x efficiency tables (HVT_WW_, HVT_WZ_, RSG_WW_, RSG_ZZ_ acceptance_times_efficiency) are present locally but are not limits; not used.
- The HVT WW, HVT WZ, RS Graviton WW and RS Graviton ZZ limits come from HEPData tables that cite auxiliary figures (4a, 4b, 5a, 5b). The paper body does not define these individual-channel limits; the channel labels are the HEPData names.
