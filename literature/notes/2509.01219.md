# 2509.01219 — Search for electroweak-scale dijet resonances using trigger-level analysis with the ATLAS detector in 132 fb^-1 of pp collisions at 13 TeV

- ATLAS Collaboration, 2025 (arXiv v2, 04 Dec 2025); Phys. Rev. D 112 (2025) 092015 (DOI 10.1103/15p2-bkg8); CERN-EP-2025-194; INSPIRE recid 2966134; HEPData record 161624 (DOI 10.17182/hepdata.161624.v1; INSPIRE key ins2966134). HEPData tables used: "Z' limits (J50)", "Z' limits (J100)", "Gaussian resonance limits (J50)", "Gaussian resonance limits (J100)".
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 15.0 fb^-1 (J50) and 132 fb^-1 (J100), overlapping samples, not additive (HEPData qualifiers; Sec. 3). Measured data; limits at 95% CL_S.
- Topology / final state: inclusive dijet; two trigger-level anti-kt R=0.4 jets; pp -> jj resonance (hadronic).
- Selection summary:
  - Trigger: TLA stream; J100 = L1 jet pT > 100 GeV (2016-2018); J50 = end-of-fill L1 single-jet pT > 50 GeV and L1Topo dijet J50_DETA20-J50J (Sec. 3).
  - Two trigger jets with pT > 85 GeV and abs(eta) < 2.4; abs(y*) < 0.6 with y* = abs(Delta eta / 2) (Sec. 5; definition in Sec. 3).
  - Events with a leading or sub-leading jet in 1.0 < abs(eta) < 1.6 excluded (Sec. 4, referring to Sec. 5).
  - Calibrated m_jj >= 344 GeV (J50) and >= 481 GeV (J100) (Sec. 5). Search range 375 to 1800 GeV (abstract); J100 used above 600 GeV, J50 below (Sec. 8).
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Benchmark: leptophobic spin-1 Z' with axial-vector couplings to all SM quarks, one common coupling g_q; no interference with the SM; dark-matter decays negligible (Sec. 3). Generation: MadGraph 5 2.2.3 at LO, Pythia 8.210 with EvtGen 1.2.0 and A14 tune, LO NNPDF2.3 PDFs (Sec. 3). Limit observable is g_q.
  - Gaussian-shaped resonance: peak m_G, width 5%, 10%, 15% of m_G (HEPData; Sec. 8). Observable: sigma x A x BR(to jets) in pb.
  - Polarization quantities: not applicable (none measured).
  - Reference frame: dijet rapidity variable y* = abs(Delta eta / 2) as defined in Sec. 3 ("where $|y^{*}|\equiv|\Delta\eta/2|$ is half of the absolute rapidity difference between the two jets"). No other frame stated.
  - Level: trigger jets calibrated to the offline-jet scale by a trigger/offline correction (Sec. 4: "to align trigger jets to the offline jet energy scale"). Limits are set on reconstructed m_jj; no unfolding stated.

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Jet flavour composition (Sec. 7, Fig. 8; figure-only, numbers from text): "the dominant sources of uncertainty in the mean of the signal is the uncertainty in the jet flavour composition ranging from 8 to 20 GeV with rising signal mass".
- Jet flavour response and in-situ (Sec. 7): "both ranging approximately from 2 to 12 GeV".
- Signal width (Sec. 7): "all nuisance parameters having a sub-GeV impact".
- Spurious signal (Sec. 7): "up to $170\%$ of the statistical uncertainty for wide resonances"; impact on the sensitive cross-section "up to an $80\%$ increase, depending on signal shape and mass".
- Background shape: nuisance parameters theta_j in Eq. 1 (Sec. 6), fitted in the limit setting (Sec. 8).
- Luminosity (Sec. 7): "below $1\%$ and have a negligible effect".
- No Z' normalisation uncertainty on the cross-section for a given g_q (Sec. 7): "No other normalisation uncertainties in the $Z^{\prime}$ cross-section for a given coupling constant $g_{q}$ is applied for comparability".

## Caveats

- Mass coverage: the paper covers 375 to 1800 GeV. Within the 10 GeV to 1 TeV window, all rows are extracted and nothing lies below 375 GeV. Above 1 TeV only the reference points listed are extracted.
- Observed excess (Sec. 8): "The largest localised excess is observed for a $Z^{\prime}$ signal with a mass of $m_{Z^{\prime}}=$650 GeV, corresponding to a $3.4\sigma$ local significance", "This translates to a global significance of $2.2\sigma$". Relevant to the observed Z' limit at 650 GeV.
- Comparison with other results (Sec. 8): "The limits from the offline search are stronger as it uses tracking information in the jet calibration" (ATLAS offline dijet search [13], overlapping Z' mass above 1500 GeV; not extracted).
- Sec. 9: "They constitute the strongest published exclusion limits on dijet resonances in the mass range 375 to 425 GeV and 700 to 1500 GeV" (text claim; not checked against other papers).
- Narrow resonances (Sec. 8): "Signals with an intrinsic width much smaller than 5% should be compared to the limit for width equal to 5% of its mass". Gaussian limits apply "when PDF and non-perturbative effects can be safely truncated or neglected".
- Z' limit is a g_q limit for one benchmark (common axial-vector coupling, no DM decays, no interference); not a general limit on hadronic narrow resonances.
- Trigger-level jets have no tracking; flavour uncertainties are "a factor of two larger than for offline jets" (Sec. 4).
- Only the signal region with the stronger expected limit was used for each signal mass (Sec. 8); J50 and J100 overlap and are not combined in the tables.
- Expected-limit fluctuations "arise from the mass-dependent spurious signal systematic uncertainty" (Sec. 8).

- Boundary at 600 GeV: both J50 and J100 tables contain m = 600 GeV with different values (Z' obs 0.077421 J50 vs 0.070669 J100; Z' exp 0.0747 vs 0.051945; Gaussian 5% obs 1.0268 vs 0.88824). The text says J100 above and J50 below 600 GeV, and "stronger expected limit" selects per mass, but the text does not state which table is used at exactly 600 GeV. Not resolved here; both rows are given.
- HEPData Z' tables use the x header "$m_{G}$ [GeV]" while the location and description refer to m_Z'. Assumed x = m_Z' (Fig. 9b location).
- Figure 9 values were not read; the observed/expected mapping is from HEPData headers and group indices, not checked against the plot.
- Gaussian expected 10% and 15% widths have no bands in HEPData (only 5% has bands, as stored).
- Limits are the 95% CL_S values as stored in HEPData; the CL_S procedure itself was not checked.
- HEPData tables "Acceptance", "Cutflow", and "Dijet mass distribution (J50/J100)" were cached but are not limit tables and are not used here.
- The PDF was cached but not read; the HTML (arxiv.html) and paper text were used for quoted text.
- Gaussian and Z' rows above 1 TeV are not complete by design (endpoints and reference masses only), per scope rule.
