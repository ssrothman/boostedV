# 1901.10917 — Search for low-mass resonances decaying into two jets and produced in association with a photon using pp collisions at √s = 13 TeV with the ATLAS detector

- Authors/Collaboration: ATLAS Collaboration (analysis EXOT-2018-05), 2019. Journal: Phys. Lett. B 795 (2019) 56, DOI 10.1016/j.physletb.2019.03.067. INSPIRE recid 1717700. HEPData: record ins1717700 (recid 85763, version 1, Tables 1–16). Text read from arXiv HTML, v3 (13 Jun 2019); journal version not checked.
- √s = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 79.8 fb⁻¹ (single-photon trigger) and 76.6 fb⁻¹ (combined trigger), 2015–2017 data (Sec. 3). Data, not simulation or projection.
- Topology / final state: pp → γ + dijet, with the resonance decaying to two jets; the photon is radiated (ISR) off a colliding parton (Sec. 3). Two categories: flavour-inclusive, and both leading jets b-tagged. Limits are on the dijet-mass resonance (Sec. 6). The paper does not give a per-process label for associated production beyond "photon ISR".
- Selection summary:
  - Triggers: single-photon (E_T,trig^γ > 140 GeV) for m_jj 225–450 GeV; combined (photon E_T,trig^γ > 75 GeV in 2016, > 85 GeV in 2017, plus two jets p_T > 50 GeV) for 450 GeV–1.1 TeV (Sec. 3).
  - Offline: n_jets ≥ 2, n_γ ≥ 1; photon |η| < 2.37 excluding 1.37–1.52, E_T^γ > 150 GeV (single-photon category) or > 95 GeV (combined category); anti-k_t R = 0.4 jets, p_T^jet > 25 GeV, |η^jet| < 2.8; |y*| < 0.75; m_jj > 169 GeV (single-photon) or > 335 GeV (combined) (Table 1).
  - Jets within ΔR = 0.4 of the photon are removed; jets with p_T < 60 GeV and |η| < 2.4 must come from the primary vertex with the highest Σp_T² (Sec. 3).
  - b-tagged categories: two leading jets with DL1 tag at 77% b-efficiency, |η^jet| < 2.5, n_b-tag ≥ 2; the combined-trigger b-tagged category also requires p_T^jet > 65 GeV (Table 1, Sec. 3).
  - Background: smooth fit functions Eq. (1)/(2) of Sec. 4 (Sec. 7 gives the search range 225 GeV–1.1 TeV; 169 GeV is the single-photon threshold, Sec. 3; the BumpHunter scan runs from 169/335 GeV to 1.1 TeV, Sec. 5; individual background-fit windows are 750–1600 GeV, Sec. 4) [verifier wR_a]; no significant excess ("Without including systematic uncertainties, the BumpHunter p-value ... is p>0.5 for all distributions", Sec. 5) [verifier w2_4].
- Model or observable definition (frame / convention):
  - Benchmark: leptophobic axial-vector Z′ with universal quark coupling g_q and a heavy fermion dark-matter candidate, so decay to DM is zero. Width set to the minimum allowed by g_q and m: "3.6% – 4.2% of the mass for m_Z′ = 0.25 – 0.95 TeV and g_q = 0.3" (Sec. 6). Z′–SM Z interference neglected (Sec. 6).
  - Signal MC: MadGraph5_aMC@NLO 2.2.3 LO, NNPDF3.0 LO, Pythia 8.186, A14 tune, m_Z′ 0.25–1.5 TeV at g_q = 0.3 (Sec. 6).
  - Gaussian benchmark: m_G with width σ_m(G)/m(G) = 0.07, 0.10, 0.15, plus an intrinsically narrow "Res." curve with resolution 8%→3% (Sec. 6, Fig. 3 caption). Limit is on the visible cross-section σ×A×ε×B: "the product of the production cross-section, the detector acceptance, the reconstruction efficiency, and the branching ratio" (Sec. 6).
  - A = kinematic acceptance, ε = reconstruction efficiency (Sec. 6). The paper does not state whether A is generator-level; the limit is on the product σ×A×ε×B.
  - Limit method: Bayesian, constant prior on signal cross-section, Gaussian priors on nuisances; expected from background-only pseudo-experiments; "95% credibility-level (CL) upper limits" (Sec. 6). The paper does not describe a frequentist CLs construction.
  - Reference frame / polarization: not applicable (no polarization observable). Rapidity variable: y* = (y₁ − y₂)/2 (Sec. 3).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- b-tagging efficiency (b-tagged categories only): "uncertainties of the bb-tagging efficiency are the dominant uncertainties in each mass distribution." Reweighting 5–15% per jet, depending on p_T (Sec. 6).
- Background fit-function choice: "the largest difference between fits among the variants of Eq. (1) and Eq. (2) that obtain a p-value above 0.05, is taken as a systematic uncertainty" (Sec. 6). Size not stated.
- Background statistical fluctuations: "computed via Poisson fluctuations around the values of the nominal background model" (Sec. 6). Size not stated.
- Jet energy scale: "leading to an average 2% shift of the peak value for each mass distribution" (Sec. 6). Applied as the uncertainty on the Gaussian mean.
- Other detector and normalisation uncertainties (Sec. 6): jet energy resolution 3%; photon identification 2% (single-photon) and 1.4% (combined); combined-trigger efficiency 3%; PDF 1% (Z′ signals only); luminosity 2% (single-photon) and 2.3% (combined).
- Excluded: "further uncertainties of the theoretical cross-section for the Z′ model are not considered" (Sec. 6).
- Statement on inclusion: "All these uncertainties are included in the reported limits" (Sec. 6). HEPData gives only obs and exp, with no stat/syst split.

## Caveats

- Mass range: the abstract and Sec. 1 give 225 GeV–1.1 TeV, and Sec. 7 gives the fit range 169–1100 GeV. The HEPData tables end at 1050 GeV. The 1100 GeV end of the range is not tabulated.
- The Z′ g_q limit (Fig. 2) and its expected curve are not in HEPData. No numerical values are extracted from the figure.
- The Sec. 7 visible cross-sections (12, 5.1, 21, 9.7 fb) are text values, not table values. Their obs/exp status is not stated.
- The quotes come from the arXiv HTML rendering. LaTeX has been rendered to plain text (for example g_q, m_Z′). Each quote was checked against the rendered text, but typography may differ from the journal PDF.
- HEPData Tables 1–4 (dijet m_jj distributions) and 9–16 (Z′ kinematic acceptance and reconstruction efficiency) are not limits and are not extracted. Tables 9–16 may matter for recasting.
- Pre-fetched HEPData was used only from the local files; no network fetch was made to hepdata.net.
