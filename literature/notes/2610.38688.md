# 2610.38688 — Precision Studies of Boosted Polarized Vector Bosons with Energy Correlators

- Authors: Gao, Anjie; Lee, Kyle; Zhang, Xiaoyuan (3 authors per INSPIRE). Year 2026 (INSPIRE preprint_date 2026-10-28). Journal: preprint (arXiv 2610.38688, categories hep-ph, hep-ex, nucl-th). Collaboration: none. INSPIRE recid 3220241. HEPData record: none (https://www.hepdata.net/record/ins3220241?format=json returned HTTP 404).
- sqrt(s): 13.6 TeV for pp→V+X predictions (Sec. IV.2); 162–500 GeV for e+e-→W+W- (Sec. IV.3, Table 1). Integrated luminosity: none. Data or simulation: theory predictions only (NNLO QCD for pp, LO electroweak tree level for e+e-). No measurement is reported.
- Topology / final state: (i) pp→V+X, V=W±,Z, with hadronic V decay (energy-energy correlator, EEC); polarization inputs from NNLO production with leptonic decays used as polarimeter. (ii) e+e-→W+W- semileptonic, EEC on hadronic W decay.
- Selection summary (theory inputs, not a data analysis):
  - pp: pT>100 GeV, |yV|<0.5 (pT bins 100–800 GeV), Sec. IV.2.
  - pp polarimeter: Z→ℓℓ with 66<mℓℓ<116 GeV; W→ℓν with 60<mℓν<100 GeV; no individual lepton cuts (Sec. IV.2).
  - e+e-: unpolarized beams, no ISR, no angular cuts, leptonic W decay integrated inclusively (App. C, Sec. IV.3).
  - Jet algorithm and R: not applicable (the observable is the EEC of the V decay products; no jet definition given for Secs. II–IV).
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Helicity density matrix ρ_λλ' over λ=±1,0, quantized along the boson direction (helicity frame; Sec. II, Eq. 12). Normalization Σ_λ ρ_λλ = 1 (Eq. 39).
  - Definition in the text: ρ_λλ(γ) = (dσ^prod_λλ/dγ) / (dσ^prod/dγ) (Eq. 39). The text calls ρ00 the "longitudinal fraction … defined in the helicity frame for the stated selection."
  - pp polarization fractions extracted "from their decay angular distributions in the helicity frame [73]" (Sec. IV.2). The fit form ρ00(γ) is a quartic polynomial in ln γ; the coefficients are not given in the text.
  - Rest-frame EEC structure functions W^(0,0), W^(2,0), W^(2,2) map onto the diagonal and off-diagonal ρ entries through Eqs. (9)–(10) (helicity basis, rest frame, bisector-frame form in Eq. 11). Not a map to lepton-decay angular coefficients A_0–A_7.
  - Energy-flow and charge-flux one-point traces are given in terms of ρ in Eq. (82) (helicity frame; parity-even and parity-odd split).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- No experimental systematics (no data analysis). Only theory uncertainty is stated: "The scale uncertainty is the envelope of the seven-point variation of \mu_R and \mu_F by factors of two with 1/2\leq\mu_R/\mu_F\leq 2" (Sec. IV.2).
- Fig. 5 and Fig. 6 captions: "Bands denote perturbative uncertainties" (figure-only; band sizes not tabulated).
- Table 1 has no uncertainty column.

## Caveats

- Q2 answer: no measurement of f_L, f_0, A_0, or ρ00 from data is reported. The values above are predictions.
- Q3 answer: no measurement of more than one spin-density-matrix element is reported. The paper provides a theory map from ρ to rest-frame EEC structure functions (Eqs. 9–11) and to one-point traces (Eq. 82). It does not map ρ to lepton-decay angular coefficients A_0–A_7.
- Experimental polarization measurements are cited (refs [31–34], [75, 76]) but not quoted or extracted here.
- pp predictions use Nnlojet with NNPDF3.1 NLO, √s=13.6 TeV, α_s(mZ)=0.118 (Sec. IV.2). The prediction is for stable on-shell bosons, with polarization from leptonic-decay fractions (Sec. IV.2).
