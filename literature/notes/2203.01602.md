# 2203.01602 — First measurement of the Z→μ⁺μ⁻ angular coefficients in the forward region of pp collisions at √s = 13 TeV

- Authors/Collaboration: LHCb collaboration (R. Aaij et al.); year 2022; journal ref: Phys. Rev. Lett. 129 (2022) 091801; arXiv:2203.01602v2 (CERN-EP-2022-030, LHCb-PAPER-2021-048); INSPIRE recid 2045484; HEPData record: none (prefetch file `.cache/2203.01602/hepdata/NONE`, "No HEPData record")
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 5.1 fb⁻¹ (2016–2018); data (not simulation or projection). The Born-level corrections use Pythia8 simulation.
- Topology / final state: Drell–Yan Z/γ* → μ⁺μ⁻ + X, pp, forward rapidity (2 < yZ < 5 in the text; Tables 2 and 4 use yZ > 2; Table 3 uses pT(Z) < 100 GeV).
- Selection summary:
  - Muons: pT > 20 GeV/c, 2.0 < η < 4.5, relative momentum uncertainty < 10 %, isolation z = pT^μ / pT^(μ-cone) > 0.85 (cone δR < 0.5).
  - Opposite-sign pair from a common primary vertex; 818 074 candidates in 50 < Mμμ < 120 GeV/c² (745 343 in 75 < Mμμ < 105 GeV/c²); total background 0.2 %.
  - Angular coefficients from an unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to cosθ and φ, in pT(Z) and yZ intervals, corrected to Born level (FSR via Pythia8 Born/Bare).
  - No jets (no jet algorithm or R). Mass windows: 75–105 GeV/c² for the main result; 55–75 and 105–120 GeV/c² for A2 (Table 4).
- Model or observable definition (frame/convention): Eq. (1) of the paper gives dσ/(dcosθ dφ) ∝ (1+cos²θ) + ½A0(1−3cos²θ) + A1 sin2θ cosφ + ½A2 sin²θ cos2φ + A3 sinθ cosφ + A4 cosθ + A5 sin²θ sin2φ + A6 sin2θ sinφ + A7 sinθ sinφ. θ and φ are the polar and azimuthal angles of μ⁺ in the **Collins–Soper frame** ("...the polar and azimuthal angles of the μ+ lepton in the Collins-Soper frame"). A0–A7 are the frame-dependent angular coefficients; the paper does not define f_L, f0 or ρ00. A5–A7 are fixed to zero; A4 is not reported, only ΔA4 = A4 − (its mean). The paper's own Lam–Tung statement is "The equality A0 = A2 is known as the Lam-Tung relation". The paper does not state a polarization fraction f_L, f0 or ρ00 relation for A0; A0 is included here only because it is listed in the Q2 scope.

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)
- Finite simulated sample (bootstrap method; "MC Stat" rows in the supplemental tables). Main text, systematics paragraph: "This uncertainty varies from 0.0016 to 0.0402 in different kinematic regions, and is the dominant systematic uncertainty."
- Efficiency corrections (control-sample size and kinematic dependence). Main text: "Another significant uncertainty arises from the selection efficiencies."
- Other sources, as named in the main text: background (varied by ±σ), FSR (Photos vs Pythia8), PDF (58 CT18NNLO sub-PDF sets). The muon momentum scale and smearing effect is described as "negligible". Per the paper, all systematics are uncorrelated and "the total systematic uncertainty is taken as their sum in quadrature."

## Caveats
- Q3: the paper measures A0, A1, A2, A3 and ΔA4 (with A0−A2 in the tables). A5–A7 are fixed to zero and A4 is not reported. No density-matrix elements ρ_λλ′, off-diagonal terms or entanglement observables are reported.
- Q2: only A0 is in scope. The paper does not mention f_L, f0 or ρ00 (checked in the full text). A0 is an angular coefficient in Eq. (1); no map from A0 to a polarization fraction is given.
- Lam–Tung: main text: "the LHCb results are compatible with zero only in the highest pT interval, but there the uncertainty is relatively large." Not a number.
- Compared predictions (ResBos, DYTurbo, Powheg-BOX+Pythia8, Pythia8 LHCb tune): agreement statements are the paper's own; no values transcribed. The paper's sentence "The p-value between measurements and theoretical predictions is calculated to be 0.06." does not name the prediction in that sentence.
- Figures 1–3 plot the coefficients; their values are figure-only and not transcribed.
- Quotes were taken from the arXiv v2 HTML text with LaTeX markup removed. Numbers are verbatim; the surrounding markup is dropped.

## Open issues
- Title: the HTML `<title>` of the arXiv page is a figure caption. The title above is the paper's own heading.
- "Total" row meaning: the supplemental tables label the first row "Total" without stating whether it is the central value or a total uncertainty. It is read as the central value because the entries are signed (e.g. negative A0 in the first pT bin) and Stat and Syst are listed separately. This is an inference, not a stated fact; it needs confirmation from the published supplemental material.
- The caption phrase "regularised uncertainties" (Tables 2–4) is not explained in the text.
- Table 3 (yZ): the arXiv HTML supplemental table has four yZ bins (2–2.7, 2.7–3, 3–3.25, 3.25–3.6). The main text ("five intervals of yZ") and Table 1 (yields for 3.6–4.5: 118714) indicate a fifth bin, 3.6–4.5. Its coefficient values are absent from the HTML and were not transcribed.
- Source version: values come from the supplemental tables in the arXiv v2 HTML page. The published PRL supplemental file was not checked.
- Frame: Collins–Soper is stated for the angles in Eq. (1). The paper does not separately state the frame for the tabulated coefficients.
- Table 1 (event yields) and the per-source systematic breakdown beyond MC Stat, Stat and Syst were not transcribed (outside the Q2/Q3 request).
- No HEPData record exists (prefetch file NONE). The arXiv HTML fetch returned HTTP 200 and was used; no download failures.
