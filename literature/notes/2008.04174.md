# 2008.04174 — Measurements of the W boson rapidity, helicity, double-differential cross sections, and charge asymmetry in pp collisions at sqrt(s) = 13 TeV

- CMS Collaboration (first author Sirunyan, Albert M), 2020; Phys. Rev. D 102 (2020) 092012, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.102.092012 (HEPData `journal_info`); INSPIRE recid 1810913; HEPData record ins1810913 v1 (HEPData recid 94180, DOI 10.17182/hepdata.94180.v1)
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 35.9 fb^-1 (2016 data); data (no projection).
- Topology / final state: inclusive W -> l nu (l = mu or e), no jets. Helicity (L, R, 0) states of the W boson, at the W-boson level, extracted from a 2D fit to the lepton (pT, eta) distribution. Full text read from the arXiv HTML (`.cache/2008.04174/arxiv_html.html`; plain text in `fulltext.txt`); TeX markup simplified in the quotes below.
- Selection summary:
  - Muon pT > 26 GeV, |eta| < 2.4; electron pT > 30 GeV ("Their pT is required to exceed 30 GeV", Sec. 0.4).
  - Transverse mass m_T > 40 GeV; no direct MET requirement (Sec. 0.4).
  - Extra-lepton veto: additional loose leptons with pT > 10 GeV rejected (Sec. 0.4).
  - Helicity fit: \|y_W\| bins of width 0.25 up to 2.5 (10 bins per charge); \|y_W\| > 2.5 fixed to MC (Sec. 0.6.2).
  - Lepton 2D templates: pT^l up to 45 GeV in 1 GeV bins; eta^l in 0.1 bins (Sec. 0.6.2).
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Angular distribution (Eq. 1, Sec. 0.6.1): theta* and phi* are "the polar and azimuthal decay angles of the lepton in the Collins–Soper frame of reference", with the lepton the charged lepton for W- and the neutrino for W+. Integrated over phi* (Eq. 2): A0 and A4 terms only.
  - Helicity decomposition (Eq. 3): f_L, f_R, f_0 are the helicity fractions, "where by definition f_i>0 and f_L+f_R+f_0=1"; A0 ∝ f_0 and A4 ∝ ∓(f_L − f_R) (sign depends on the W charge; proportionality only, no constant stated). f_i depend on pT^W, \|y_W\| and charge (Sec. 0.6.1).
  - Measured observables: helicity-differential cross sections sigma(W_L), sigma(W_R) vs \|y_W\| (f_0 is NOT floating); A4 vs \|y_W\| shown in an appendix figure (not tabulated).
  - Polarized charge asymmetry A^pol(\|y_W\|) (Eq. 7) and unpolarized asymmetry are also measured (not in scope).
  - Not stated: f_L, f_0, A0, rho00 as functions of pT^W (no pT^W-differential polarization measurement; pT^W enters only as a reweighting variable for the templates).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- The paper does not rank the uncertainty groups in text for the helicity cross sections or for A4. Groups are listed in Sec. 0.7.3: "statistical — the statistical uncertainty in the data sample"; "PDFs ⊕ αS — 60 Hessian variations of the NNPDF3.0 PDF set and αS"; "μF, μR, μF+R"; "QCD bkg."; "lepton scale"; "other experimental"; "other bkg"; "FSR"; "MC sample size" (Sec. 0.7.3 list).
- Correlated normalization: "the 2.5% uncertainty in the integrated luminosity [58], is fully correlated across all the rapidity bins, thus it cancels out when taking the ratio to the total W cross section" (Fig. 9 discussion).
- Strong correlated uncertainties between rapidity bins; the text quantifies overlap-induced statistical correlations: "in the range 50–80% for adjacent W boson rapidity bins" (Sec. on Fig. 10).
- Impacts: "The impact of uncertainties that are strongly correlated among all the rapidity bins mostly cancel when considering either the cross section normalized to the total cross section or in the charge asymmetry." (Sec. 0.7.3). Impact breakdowns for normalized and absolute helicity cross sections are in HEPData (Impacts Figure 3a/3b, A10–A12) and are not transcribed here.
- Longitudinal component: 30% normalization uncertainty on the MC prediction (Results, "Differential cross sections in \|y_W\|"); this uncertainty is not a measured quantity.

## Caveats

- Q2: no measured f_L, f_0, A0 or rho00 is given. f_0 is fixed to MG5_aMC@NLO, so the L and R cross sections depend on that assumption. Measured L and R cross sections are not f_L; no conversion was made here.
- Q3: the paper measures two helicity cross sections (L, R) per charge, plus A4 vs \|y_W\| (figure-only). A0 is not measured. A4 is linked to f_L − f_R only by proportionality (Eq. 3 text).
- Binning is in \|y_W\| only (10 bins of 0.25). No pT^W-differential measurement of polarization or A_i is presented.
- Templates use pre-FSR generator leptons for the helicity states (Sec. 0.6.1: "The generated leptons are considered before any final-state radiation"). Dressing (dR < 0.1) is used only in the double-differential measurement (Sec. 0.6.3). The helicity cross sections are W-boson-level results from a fit to reconstructed leptons.
- Helicity states are reweighted MC: "Each simulated event is reweighted three separate times to obtain pure samples of left-handed, right-handed, and longitudinally polarized W bosons." The helicity separation is therefore model-dependent (MG5_aMC@NLO-based templates).
- Normalized (Fig. 9) and absolute (Fig. 10.1) values are different quantities; do not interchange.
- Intro fractions (65/60%, 28/33%, 7/7%) are background statements, not measurements; no citation is shown in the extracted text.

## Open issues

- A4 central values are figure-only (appendix Fig. 10.5/10.6); not in HEPData. Not read off plots. Paper numbering of these figures is not verified (the arXiv HTML labels them ".10.5"/".10.6"; the HEPData impacts table naming "Impacts Figure A16" suggests the PRD label is A5/A6, unverified).
- HEPData Impacts Figure A16a and A16b are both described as "for W^{+}" in the HEPData metadata, while the paper caption for Fig. 10.16 says upper = W+ and lower = W-. Charge assignment of A16b is therefore unresolved.
- HEPData Figure 9a/9b and A1a/A1b: charge mapping (9a = W+, 9b = W-; A1a = W+, A1b = W-) is inferred from the caption left/right ordering, not from the table headers (which do not state the charge).
- HEPData Figure 9 table title says "divided by bin width", while the caption says "normalized to the total cross section"; the table itself does not state the normalization. Caption taken as authoritative; flagged here.
- "Measurement uncertainty" in HEPData does not say whether it is total or stat-only; no stat/syst split was found for the helicity cross sections in the text.
- The HEPData "Figure A4" is the charge asymmetry, not the A4 coefficient. Do not confuse them.
- The arXiv HTML section numbers (e.g. "0.6.2") are not the PRD section numbers. Locations here use HTML numbering; PRD numbering was not checked.
- Only the arXiv HTML was read; the PDF/PRD version was not compared to it.
- HEPData theory-uncertainty and expected-value columns were not transcribed; the aMC@NLO prediction is not a measurement.
