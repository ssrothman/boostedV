# 2603.26463 — Measurements of Z-boson pair entanglement in decays of Higgs bosons at the ATLAS experiment

- Authors/Collaboration: ATLAS Collaboration (analysis group atlas-higp-2024-25; author list not extracted). Year 2026 (arXiv v2, 05 Oct 2026). Journal: Phys. Rev. Lett. 137 (2026) 111804, DOI 10.1103/y1nh-1b82. INSPIRE recid 3136105. HEPData: none (`hepdata/NONE` present; `tables/` empty).
- sqrt(s): 13 TeV (Run 2, 2015–2018) and 13.6 TeV (Run 3, 2022–2024). Integrated luminosity: 140 fb-1 (13 TeV) and 164 fb-1 (13.6 TeV). Data (pp collisions); MC used for corrections and the hypothesis test.
- Topology / final state: H → ZZ* → ℓ+ℓ−ℓ+ℓ− (4e, 4μ, 2e2μ). Inclusive production (ggF, VBF, VH, ttH/tH/bbH in MC).
- Selection summary:
  - At least four isolated leptons (e or μ); e pT > 7 GeV, |η| < 2.47; μ pT > 5 GeV, |η| < 2.7 (2.5 in Run 3) (Main text, event selection).
  - Three highest-pT leptons above 20, 15, 10 GeV.
  - 50 < mZ1 < 106 GeV, 12 < mZ2 < 115 GeV, 115 < m4ℓ < 130 GeV.
  - Two signal regions for the hypothesis test: mZ2 < 30 GeV and mZ2 > 30 GeV.
  - Jets: not used in the selection (not stated). Jet algorithm and R: not applicable/not stated.
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Frame: lepton angles (θ, φ) of each negatively charged lepton in its parent Z rest frame; z-axis along the Z1 momentum direction in the Higgs rest frame (Main text and Fig. 3; reference frame is described but not named as helicity or Collins–Soper).
  - Estimator (Main text, unnumbered equation): c_{2M1 2M2} = 40π Re(Y_2^{M1}(θ1,φ1) × Y_2^{M2}(θ2,φ2)), with M1 = −M2 ∈ {1, 2}. Observables C_{2M1 2M2} = ⟨c_{2M1 2M2}⟩, the mean over selected events.
  - Map to the spin density matrix (leading order, stated assumptions; paper cites Aguilar-Saavedra et al., PRD 107 (2023) 016012 [ref. 11], not independently checked): the four elements that are products of |0,0⟩ and |±,∓⟩ equal (1/3) C_{2,1,2,−1} (TeX `\frac{1}{3}C_{2,1,2,-1}`); the two products of |+,−⟩ and |−,+⟩ equal (1/3) C_{2,2,2,−2}. Only nine of 81 elements are non-zero at LO.
  - Measured coefficients are parton level (leptons before QED radiation), obtained by linear calibrations from SM MC per channel and run period (App. B).
  - Hypothesis test: test statistic q̃ = −2 ln(L_|00⟩ / L_QE), using c_{2,2,2,−2} distribution; CLs method.
  - Not measured in this paper (from text): A_0–A_7, diagonal elements, or any p_T-differential SDM elements.

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Fake-lepton (reducible) background: largest listed component in Table 1 for every coefficient (±0.16, ±0.16, ±0.12 for C_{2,1,2,−1}; ±0.11, ±0.18, ±0.12 for C_{2,2,2,−2}). Table 1 row "Fake-lepton bkg." Also Appendix A: 39% (Run 2) and 46% (Run 3) total uncertainty on the reducible background in the signal region.
- Irreducible background: Table 1 row "Irreducible bkg." (±0.05, ±0.04, ±0.04 for C_{2,1,2,−1}; ±0.01, ±0.01, ±0.01 for C_{2,2,2,−2}).
- Interference (4e+4μ only, and combined): Table 1 row "Interference" (—, ±0.04, ±0.02 for C_{2,1,2,−1}; —, ±0.03, ±0.02 for C_{2,2,2,−2}). Appendix B: 50% of ΔC^interf assigned as symmetric systematic for 4e and 4μ.
- EW NLO effects: Table 1 row (±0.02, ±0.01, ±0.01 for C_{2,1,2,−1}; <0.01 for C_{2,2,2,−2}). Appendix B: 1.1% and 1.4% multiplicative.
- Migration: Table 1 row (±0.01, ±0.02, ±0.01 and ±0.01, ±0.02, ±0.01).
- Statement in Results text: "The systematic uncertainties are all substantially smaller than the statistical uncertainties."
- Hypothesis test (Appendix B): dominant theoretical uncertainty is higher-order QCD terms, "approximately 10% of the predicted yield"; sub-leading: MC parton shower and fake-lepton uncertainties; experimental "conservative overall 3% uncertainty".

## Caveats

- Only two SDM elements (C_{2,1,2,−1}, C_{2,2,2,−2}) are measured as numbers. The hypothesis test is a model-dependent test, not an element measurement: it relies on the scalar nature of the Higgs boson, SM chiral couplings in Z decays, and SM background angular distributions (Introduction).
- No p_T or other differential binning of the SDM elements is reported. Results are inclusive over the mass windows given above. The "uncertainties vs p_T" requested by the scope cannot be provided from this paper.
- Parton-level values: calibrated to leptons before QED radiation, using linear calibrations from SM MC (App. B). Not detector level.
- Interference correction applied to 4e and 4μ only; Table 1 caption states the 4e+4μ values include ΔC_interf corrections.
- Sensitivity statements (text, not numbers in tables): full-distribution test gives "about 20% higher expected sensitivity" than c_{2,1,2,−1}; mZ2 split gives "about 15%" improvement in rejecting the |00⟩ hypothesis.
- Introduction (paper.txt:82): "small (<2%) higher-order corrections [42]" (not EW-specific); App. B gives EW effects of 1.1% and 1.4% for the two elements [verifier w4_1].
- The SDM map (1/3 factors and nine non-zero elements) is the paper's stated LO result, cited to ref. 11; I did not verify ref. 11 directly.

## Open issues

- SM expected row in Table 1: the text gives one value per coefficient (−0.97; 0.64) with no channel column stated. Whether these are combined or another column is not stated. Do not assume.
- Table 1 "Fake-lepton bkg." row: listed under "Leading uncertainty components" alongside "Statistical", but Appendix B says the fake-lepton estimate statistics are part of the statistical uncertainty. Whether the fake-lepton row is systematic or statistical is not stated.
- Table 1 "Migration" row: the paper does not explicitly identify it with the unfolding systematic that Appendix B mentions. Not confirmed.
- Confidence level of the quoted uncertainties (68% vs other) is not stated.
- "Expected 4.9 σ": the expectation definition (which hypothesis or ensemble) is not stated.
- Combined total uncertainties in abstract/table (±0.45, ±0.44) are as printed; the paper does not give a breakdown table of the total in the text beyond Table 1.
- Figure 5 individual-channel values and χ² p-values are figure-only; not read from the plot.
- HEPData: no record (hepdata/NONE). The values are from the arXiv HTML rendition (v2), which I did not compare against the PDF.
- Author list not extracted from the HTML; collaboration is ATLAS per the paper's header and text.
