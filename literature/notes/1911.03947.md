# 1911.03947 — Search for high mass dijet resonances with a new background prediction method in proton-proton collisions at √s = 13 TeV
- Collaboration: CMS (HEPData first author "Sirunyan, Albert M et al."; CMS-EXO-19-012). Year: 2019 (arXiv; HEPData creation 2019-11-10). Journal: JHEP 05 (2020) 033, DOI 10.1007/JHEP05(2020)033 (from HEPData `journal_info`; INSPIRE not queried). INSPIRE recid 1764471. HEPData: `ins1764471`, record id 91059 v1, status finished, 10 data tables (all 10 cached under `.cache/1911.03947/hepdata/tables/`).
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV (HEPData title; Sec. 0.7). Integrated luminosity 137 fb⁻¹, 2016–2018 (Abstract; Sec. 0.1; Sec. 0.7). Data.
- Topology / final state: dijet resonances (qq, qg, gg; RS graviton as weighted average of qq and gg, Sec. 0.6.1), narrow and broad (spin-1 qq up to 55% width; spin-2 qq and gg up to 30% width; Sec. 0.6.2). Mass scan 1.8–8.7 TeV in 100 GeV steps (Sec. 0.6).
- Selection summary:
  - Anti-kT R = 0.4 particle-flow jets (FastJet); two leading jets with pT > 30 GeV and |η| < 2.5, plus jet ID (Sec. 0.3).
  - Wide jets: other jets within ΔR = sqrt((Δη)² + (Δφ)²) < 1.1 are added to the nearest of the two leading jets; m_jj is from the two wide jets (Sec. 0.3).
  - Signal region |Δη| < 1.1; m_jj > 1.5 TeV offline (Sec. 0.3).
  - Ratio-method control regions: CR_middle 1.1 < |Δη| < 1.5 and CR_high 1.5 < |Δη| < 2.6 (Sec. 0.3).
  - Background: fit method for resonance masses 1.8–2.9 TeV, ratio method for 3.0–8.7 TeV (Sec. 0.6).
- Model or observable definition:
  - Observable: 95% CL upper limit on σ×B×A [pb] (Sec. 0.6; HEPData units [pb]). B includes only the five light quarks and gluons (top excluded); A is evaluated at parton level (Sec. 0.6.1). Limits use the "modified frequentist criterion ... using the asymptotic approximation of the test statistic" (Sec. 0.6).
  - Spin-1 qq, spin-2 qq and gg: width-dependent limits (Fig. 10; HEPData also tabulates width columns: spin-1 qq at 1/10/30/55%, spin-2 qq and gg at 1/10/30%) [verifier wR_a] [verifier w2_2]. Frame / polarization: not applicable; no polarization or angular quantity is measured or quoted, and the signal is assumed to have a vector-resonance angular distribution (Sec. 0.5). Reference frame: not stated.
  - Leptophobic Z′ (universal quark coupling g_q′, no DM coupling): Γ = 3(g_q′)² M / (2π), Eq. (5); g_q′ = g_B/6 relative to Ref. [65] (Sec. 0.6.2).
  - Leptophobic vector mediator with DM (universal quark coupling g_q): m_DM = 1 GeV, g_DM = 1.0, Dirac DM; Γ ≈ (18 g_q² + 1) M / (12π), Eq. (6) (Sec. 0.6.2).
  - Benchmark parameters for Table 1: RS graviton k/M̄_Pl = 0.1; color-octet scalar k_s² = 1/2; DM mediator m_DM = 1 GeV (Table 1).
  - Theory normalisation (Sec. 0.6.1): LO CTEQ6L1 in the narrow-width approximation; K = 1 + 8παS/9 ≈ 1.3 for the W′ model; K = 1 + (4αS/6π)(1 + 4π²/3) ≈ 1.2 for Z′ and DM mediator; axigluon/coloron K varies from 1.1 (0.6 TeV) to 1.3 (8.1 TeV).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)
- Jet energy scale: "The uncertainty in the jet energy scale is within 2% for all values of the dijet mass"; propagated by shifting the signal m_jj shape by 2% (Sec. 0.6).
- Jet energy resolution: "translates into an uncertainty of 10% in the resolution of the dijet mass" (Sec. 0.6).
- Integrated luminosity: "The uncertainty in the integrated luminosity is 2.5% in 2016 [55] and 2018 [56], and 2.3% in 2017 [57]"; applied to signal normalisation (Sec. 0.6).
- Background-function parameters: profiled; the paper states the background systematic "decreases as the resonance mass increases" (Sec. 0.5, Sec. 0.6).
- Stat vs syst split of the limits: not stated. HEPData gives only expected ±1σ and ±2σ bands.

## Caveats
- The paper calls these "model-independent" limits (Abstract; Sec. 0.6.1). Model mass limits (Table 1) depend on the theory curves in Figs. 6–7 and on the K-factors above; those curves are not part of the copied HEPData columns, so Table 1 cannot be re-derived from the values in this note.
- Signal contamination in the control regions assumes a vector-resonance angular distribution; the paper says other benchmark angular distributions change the extracted signal negligibly (Sec. 0.5).
- Spin-1 qq limits are not presented for m > 6 TeV with Γ/M > 0.1 (Sec. 0.6.2). In the HEPData spin-1 table, the 10%, 30% and 55% width cells are "-" at 7, 8 and 8.7 TeV; 6 TeV has values.
- Coupling tables: the observed limit is "-" from 4.9 TeV onward in both tables.
- Coupling limits in Fig. 11 are for the stated benchmark (m_DM = 1 GeV, g_DM = 1.0 for left; no DM coupling for right). They are not valid for other m_DM or g_DM.
- The mass scan is 1.8–8.7 TeV in 100 GeV steps (Sec. 0.6); no limit is reported below 1.8 TeV.
