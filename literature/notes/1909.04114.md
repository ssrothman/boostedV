# 1909.04114 — Search for low mass vector resonances decaying into quark-antiquark pairs in proton-proton collisions at √s = 13 TeV

- Authors/Collaboration: CMS Collaboration, 2019. Journal: Phys. Rev. D 100 (2019) 112007 (DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.100.112007; per INSPIRE). INSPIRE recid 1753375. CMS-EXO-18-012, CERN-EP-2019-176. HEPData record: none found (direct URL https://www.hepdata.net/record/ins1753375 returns 404; HEPData search for arXiv:1909.04114 and CMS-EXO-18-012 returns no match).
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV. Integrated luminosity: 41.1 fb^-1 (2017 data, the analysis on which the 2017 limits are based); 77.0 fb^-1 (2016 + 2017 statistical combination, quoted for the combined limits). Data (measured), not projection.
- Topology / final state: single large-radius jet (AK8 or CA15) recoiling against initial-state radiation; Z′ → qq̄ reconstructed as one jet; detector-level measurement (reconstructed m_SD) with simulated signal shapes; lepton veto (e, μ, τ).
- Selection summary:
  - Trigger/offline: at least one AK8 jet with pT > 525 GeV or CA15 jet with pT > 575 GeV, |η| < 2.5 (Sec. 0.3, "Events are selected by requiring").
  - Jet algorithms: anti-kT R = 0.8 (AK8) and Cambridge–Aachen R = 1.5 (CA15) (Sec. 0.3).
  - Substructure: soft-drop (β = 0, z_cut < 0.1) mass m_SD; N2^1,DDT < 0 (5% QCD efficiency); ρ = ln(m_SD²/pT²) in −5.5 < ρ < −2.0 (AK8), −4.7 < ρ < −1.0 (CA15) (Sec. 0.3).
  - Fit categories (Sec. 0.6): pT bin edges 525, 575, 625, 700, 800, 1500 GeV (AK8); 575, 625, 700, 800, 1500 GeV (CA15); passing/failing regions; ttbar control region.
  - Signal mass range searched: 50–450 GeV. AK8 used below 175 GeV, CA15 above 175 GeV (Sec. 0.6).
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Leptophobic vector Z′ coupling only to quarks, narrow width; benchmark model of Ref. [24] (Dobrescu & Yu, arXiv:1306.2629). Coupling is g′_q with the stated relation g′_q = g_B/6 to the convention of Ref. [24] (Sec. 0.6). Signal generated at LO with MG5_aMC@NLO 2.4.3 (Sec. 0.3); NLO QCD K-factor from Z boson assumed valid (Sec. 0.3); NLO EW not applied.
  - Observable: soft-drop jet mass m_SD, binned maximum-likelihood fit. Limit set on g′_q (coupling), not on σ×B.
  - Limit method: modified frequentist CLs, profile likelihood ratio test statistic, asymptotic approximation (Refs. [60–62]), 95% CL (Sec. 0.6).
  - Polarization / reference frame: not applicable (spin-1 resonance, no polarization quantities measured). Not stated: any helicity/CS frame, because none is used.

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Sec. 0.5: "The dominant uncertainty in this analysis is the uncertainty in the fit for R_p/f, ... (1–3%), arising from the parameters a_kℓ, and the statistical uncertainty on the data in the N2^1,DDT < 0 region."
- Sec. 0.6: "The dominant uncertainty on the g′_q limit arises from the fit parameters of the R_p/f followed by the theoretical uncertainties on the signal yield due to missing NLO QCD corrections."
- Table 0.5 (systematic summary, labelled "Table 0.5" in the HTML conversion), Z′ rows, AK8/CA15:
  - NLO QCD corrections (signal): 10% (AK8), 10% (CA15).
  - N2^1,DDT selection efficiency: 10% (AK8), 7% (CA15).
  - Jet mass resolution: 10% (AK8), 7% (CA15).
  - Jet mass scale: 1% (both).
  - Integrated luminosity: 2.3% (all rows).
  - Trigger efficiency: 2%; pileup 1–2%; lepton veto 0.5%; simulation sample size 1–12%.
- Shape vs normalization: nuisances are log-normal (normalization) and Gaussian (shape), profiled in the limit (Sec. 0.6).

## Caveats

- Mass coverage: this paper's limits cover 50–450 GeV only. It does not cover the 10 GeV to 50 GeV part of the question scope or the 450 GeV–1 TeV part. The paper itself states that earlier searches probed 10–300 GeV (refs [19–22], Sec. 0.1); those are other papers and were not extracted here.
- Model: leptophobic Z′ coupling only to quarks, narrow width, with the Z′ modelled as ISR-recoil jet; the limit is on g′_q (coupling), not σ×B. Mapping to other couplings or to hadronic H/W/Z/gluon final states is not given in this paper.
- Signal modelling: LO generation with NLO QCD correction from the Z boson applied; NLO EW corrections not applied (Sec. 0.3). Parton-level HT > 400 GeV filter at generation (Sec. 0.3).
- The 20% sensitivity loss versus the 2016 search is attributed to the higher pT threshold from the 2017 trigger turn-on (Sec. 0.6).
- Observed and expected limits: the text states only observed exclusion thresholds (0.4, 0.2). Expected limit values are not given in the text.
