# 1504.03512 — Angular coefficients of Z bosons produced in pp collisions at √s = 8 TeV and decaying to μ+μ− as a function of transverse momentum and rapidity

- Authors/Collaboration: CMS Collaboration (Khachatryan, V. et al.); 2015 (INSPIRE date 2015-09-01; arXiv v2 14 Sep 2015); Phys. Lett. B 750 (2015) 154–175, DOI 10.1016/j.physletb.2015.08.061; CMS note SMP-13-010; INSPIRE recid 1359451; HEPData record ins1359451 (2 tables, DOI 10.17182/hepdata.69285.v1)
- sqrt(s) = 8 TeV; integrated luminosity 19.7 fb^-1 (text: "corresponds to an integrated luminosity of 19.7 \fbinv"); data (no projection)
- Topology / final state: pp → Z → μ+μ− (Drell–Yan Z peak), inclusive in jets (no jet requirement stated); muon channel only
- Selection summary:
  - Trigger: single isolated muon, "requiring \pt > 24 \GeV and" (η cut |η| < 2.1 in the same sentence, alttext `\abs{\eta}<2.1`)
  - Muons: "The leading (subleading) muon is required to have \pt>25\,(10) \GeV"; relative isolation "less than 0.12 (0.5)" for leading (subleading); the η cut is written "\abs{\eta}<2.1~(2.4)"
  - Opposite-charge pair, invariant mass 81–101 GeV; pair "must satisfy \abs{y} < 2.1" (quote tail: "since at higher the acceptance varies rapidly")
  - Binning: 8 qT bins and 2 |y| bins (|y|<1 and 1<|y|<2.1); fit in 12 × 12 equidistant bins in cos θ* and |φ*|
  - Templates from simulation (MadGraph, reweighted to data in qT and y); A5–A7 "are set to zero and excluded from the fit"
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Frame: Collins–Soper. Quote: "In this analysis we choose the Collins–Soper (CS) frame" ; target momentum: "the momentum of the beam proton closest in rapidity to the Z boson as the “target momentum”"
  - Angular distribution, Eq. (1) (arXiv HTML alttext, verbatim): `\begin{split}\frac{\rd^{2}\sigma}{\rd\cos\theta^{*}\rd\phi^{*}}\propto\Bigl[(1+\cos^{2}\theta^{*})+A_{0}\frac{1}{2}(1-3\cos^{2}\theta^{*})+A_{1}\sin(2\theta^{*})\cos\phi^{*}+A_{2}\frac{1}{2}\sin^{2}\theta^{*}\cos(2\phi^{*})\\
+A_{3}\sin\theta^{*}\cos\phi^{*}+A_{4}\cos\theta^{*}+A_{5}\sin^{2}\theta^{*}\sin(2\phi^{*})+A_{6}\sin(2\theta^{*})\sin{\phi^{*}}+A_{7}\sin{\theta^{*}}\sin{\phi^{*}}\Bigr].\end{split}`. Quote: "are the polar and azimuthal angles of the negatively charged lepton in the rest frame of the lepton pair."
  - Polarization: quote "are related to the polarization of the Z boson, whilst" (A0, A1, A2); A3, A4 "also sensitive to the V-A structure of the couplings of the muons". A0 = A2 is the Lam–Tung relation: quote "The relation $A_{0}=A_{2}$ is known as the Lam–Tung relation".
  - Difference A0−A2 is also reported (HEPData column "A0-A2"); quote: "as well as the difference $A_{0}-A_{2}$ are presented along with".
  - f_L, f_0, ρ_00 (or any helicity-fraction / density-matrix definition): not stated in the paper (grep for f_L, rho, helicity, longitudinal, density matrix: no hits). Q2 is answered only via A0, the paper's Eq. (1) coefficient.
  - Measurement level: reconstruction-level 2D fit with templates "based on reconstructed muons"; no unfolding or particle-level correction is stated. Values are therefore not labelled as particle-level.

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

Locations are the analysis text (the arXiv HTML has no section numbers) and Fig. 4. Per-coefficient breakdown is figure-only, not tabulated, so no values are given.

- Muon efficiency (trigger, tracking, isolation, ID) is the largest: "the muon efficiency that includes the trigger, track reconstruction, isolation, and identification." (analysis text, systematics paragraph; Fig. 4 for the breakdown)
- Template statistics: "Another significant uncertainty stems from the statistical precision of the templates, which is estimated using pseudo-experiments." (analysis text)
- Pileup: "varying the cross section of the minimum bias events by" ±5% (analysis text)
- Background: "varying the normalization scale factor of the" eμ sample by 10% and WZ and ZZ yields by 50% (analysis text)
- Acceptance dependence on assumed A_i: "the acceptance uncertainty, related to the values of" A_i, by reweighting with the fitted values (analysis text)
- Dominance: "the statistical uncertainties dominate in the highest bins in qT, whilst the systematic uncertainty in the efficiency tends to be the most important elsewhere." (analysis text)

## Caveats

- Lepton-level angular analysis of muons only; the sign conventions are those of Eq. (1) as written.
- Statistical and systematic uncertainties are reported separately in HEPData. The paper does not state whether the HEPData "sys" is a quadrature sum of all sources (text: "total systematic uncertainties" is used only for the cross-check).
- Cross-check with an independent 1D analysis, for A0, A2, A3, A4 only (not A1): quote "Both results are consistent within their total systematic uncertainties, excluding uncertainties common to both analyses." Its numbers are not extracted.
- The paper compares to MadGraph, POWHEG, and FEWZ; no theory numbers are extracted (not in scope).
- The paper's conclusions: "We find that $A_{0}>A_{2}$, especially for high $q_{\mathrm{T}}$." (not a numeric result; quoted for context).

## Open issues

- Q2 asks for f_L / f_0 / ρ_00. The paper does not report any of these; A0 is the only entry in scope, and no mapping of A0 to f_L is given. Confirm whether A0 alone is acceptable for Q2.
- Particle-level vs detector-level: not stated; the fit uses reconstructed-level templates.
- Paper table numbering: the results paragraph says the values are "provided in Tables" (number unreadable in the HTML extraction). The HEPData tables are named "Table 1" and "Table 2" with location fields "Data from P 7, Table 1 / 2". Mapping to paper table numbers is not confirmed.
- The |η| cut "\abs{\eta}<2.1~(2.4)" is paired with leading (subleading) pT as "25 (10)"; the pairing is inferred from the typography, not stated explicitly. The HTML math is partly garbled in the text, so numbers were taken from HEPData where available.
- Only the arXiv HTML was read for the text; the PDF was not checked (HTML fetch succeeded, so the PDF step was skipped per job).
- Fig. 4 systematic breakdown and Figs. 1–5 values are figure-only and not extracted.
