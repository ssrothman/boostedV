# 2607.12879 — Observation of pair production of longitudinally polarized Z Z bosons in four-lepton final state with the ATLAS detector

- ATLAS Collaboration, 2026 (arXiv v1, 14 Jul 2026), CERN-EP-2026-191, STDM-2024-01; journal ref: preprint (ATLAS journal field reads PRL, not published as of this note), INSPIRE recid 3180332, HEPData: none (cache marker `hepdata/NONE`)
- sqrt(s) = 13.6 TeV; integrated luminosity 164 fb^-1 (Run 3); statistically combined with Run 2 (13 TeV, 140 fb^-1, ref. [15]). Data.
- Topology / final state: qq̄→ZZ (plus gg→ZZ as background, EW ZZjj as signal-like) with ZZ→ℓ+ℓ−ℓ'+ℓ'−, ℓ,ℓ' = e or μ. Leptonic Z decays, no jets in selection.
- Selection summary:
  - Two SFOC pairs, |m_ℓℓ − m_Z| < 10 GeV for both, m_4ℓ > 180 GeV, m_ℓℓ > 5 GeV, ΔR_ℓℓ > 0.05 (Sec. "Event selection")
  - Leading lepton p_T > 27 GeV, subleading > 20 GeV; electrons |η| < 2.47 (dressed p_T > 7 GeV), muons |η| < 2.5 (p_T > 5 GeV) at particle level
  - Signal regions: low-mass 180 < m_4ℓ < 240 GeV; high-mass m_4ℓ > 240 GeV
  - BDT on Δφ_ℓ1ℓ2, Δφ_ℓ3ℓ4, cosθ1, cosθ2, cosθ*_Z1 (Sec. "BDT"); fit to BDT score
  - Jets (anti-k_t R = 0.4, p_T > 30 GeV, |η| < 4.5) used only for the non-prompt background study. No jet cut in the signal selection.
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - f_LL = fraction of Z_L Z_L production in the inclusive qq̄→ZZ + EW ZZjj sample (Eq. 2; N_incl^SM normalization). f_TX = fraction of Z_T Z_X, not free; fixed by unity f_LL + f_TX + κ√(f_LL f_TX) = 1 (Eq. 3), κ SM-predicted (Eq. 3 text).
  - μ_LL, μ_TX: signal-strength normalizations of the Z_L Z_L and Z_T Z_X templates relative to SM (Results text, before Table 1). These are not polarization fractions; listed in the table for completeness.
  - Frame: "The Z Z boson polarization is defined in the center-of-mass frame of the two Z Z bosons." Templates parameterized in cosθ1 (θ1 = angle between negative lepton in Z1 rest frame and Z1 flight direction in four-lepton rest frame). Helicity vs Collins–Soper: not stated.
  - Quantities not used by the paper: f_0, A_0, ρ_00, f_L (for single bosons). Not stated whether these map onto f_LL.

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- "The measurement is limited by statistical uncertainty of the data and the leading systematic uncertainty is from the theoretical modeling of the polarization templates." (Res-text, f_LL paragraph)
- "The statistical uncertainty of the data is the dominant source of uncertainty, while the modeling of the polarization templates constitutes the largest systematic uncertainty." (Res-text, combination paragraph, before Table 2)
- Largest named item in Table 2: Sherpa 3 shape difference for qq̄→ZZ, 4.4% of μ_LL (Table 2).
- Theory templates: scale variation by factor two (excluding combinations differing by factor four), parton shower (Pythia 8 vs Herwig 7), NLO EW prescription (additive vs multiplicative MoCaNLO) (Sec. "Systematic uncertainties"). Quote: "These uncertainties are treated as uncorrelated between the different polarization states and can reach up to 11%." (Sec. "Systematic uncertainties"; antecedent not explicit, see Open issues).
- Experimental: electrons 3.7% (Table 2); luminosity 1.9% (text) and 1.3% of μ_LL (Table 2).

## Caveats

- Templates: polarized qq̄→ZZ from MadGraph5_aMC@NLO (Pythia 8) and Sherpa 3 (ME+PS@NLO) with NLO QCD+EW reweighting (MoCaNLO). Run 3 uses Sherpa 3 interference template; the interference is scaled to √(μ_LL μ_TX) in the nominal fit and fixed to SM in the alternative (End Matter, Eqs. 1, 4; Tables 3, 4). Quote: "The normalization of the interference term is updated to be scaled with μ LL μ TX" (Res-text, combination paragraph; text quotes "updated" wording).
- gg→ZZ is treated as a background (no polarization) in this analysis: "the inclusive g g → Z Z process is treated as a background in this analysis to be more conservative" (Sec. "Simulation"). Run 2 used a reweighting method for gg→ZZ polarization, which the paper says showed discrepancies (Sec. "Simulation"). This affects comparability with the Run 2 f_LL / μ_LL inputs.
- Interference-normalization treatment changes significance: combined 6.5σ (scaled) vs 5.5σ (fixed) (Table 3). Central values near-identical, total uncertainties +6–8% in the fixed treatment (End Matter text).
- Particle vs detector level: the fiducial cross-section is at particle level (dressed leptons, Res-text fiducial paragraph). Whether f_LL and μ_LL are extracted at detector level is not stated explicitly in the text (reco-level BDT fit inferred from the BDT/template description; not stated).
- The paper gives no p_T dependence; the only differential split is in m_4ℓ (two signal regions). Q2 asks for p_T binning; not available here.
- f_LL is a fraction of the inclusive qq̄→ZZ + EW ZZjj sample (Eq. 2), not of all ZZ including gg→ZZ.
