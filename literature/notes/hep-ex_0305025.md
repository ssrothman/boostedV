Journal ref: preprint (none stated in the text; the conference is not named). INSPIRE recid 618701. HEPData record: none (prefetch file `.cache/hep-ex_0305025/hepdata/NONE`: "no HEPData record").
- sqrt(s): 161–209 GeV (LEP2). Integrated luminosity: "approximately 700 pb−1 was recorded per experiment" (Abstract). Data, not projection. SDM figures: L3 at 183–209 GeV (Fig. 6) and 189–209 GeV (Fig. 4); DELPHI 189 GeV (Fig. 5); OPAL 189 GeV (text, Sec. 3).
- Topology / final state: e+e− → W+W−, W decays qq̄ℓν (ℓ = e, μ). Semileptonic channels only for the SDM results (L3 qq̄eν and qq̄μν; DELPHI qq̄μν; OPAL qq̄ℓν).
- Selection summary: not stated in this paper. It defers to the experimental papers [11]–[14]. Jet algorithm, pT, mass and |y| ranges: not stated. The text says the reconstructed SDM elements "need to be corrected for detector acceptance, resolution effects and background contamination".
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Helicity amplitudes F^λ_{τ1τ2} for e+(λ′) e−(λ) → W+(τ2) W−(τ1), with τ = ±1 transverse and τ = 0 longitudinal (Eq. 3 and text, Sec. 3).
  - Joint SDM: ρ_{τ1τ1′τ2τ2′}(s, cosθ_W) = Σ_λ F^λ_{τ1τ2}(F^λ_{τ1′τ2′})* / Σ_{λ,τ1,τ2}|F^λ_{τ1τ2}|² (Eq. 4).
  - Single-W SDM: ρ^{W−}_{τ1τ1′}(s, cosθ_{W−}) = Σ_{τ2} ρ_{τ1τ1′τ2τ2}(s, cosθ_{W−}) (Eq. 5). Diagonal elements sum to 1 (text, Sec. 3). Diagonal elements are real; off-diagonal elements are complex.
  - Experimental estimator: ρ_{ττ′}^{W−}(k) = (1/N_k) Σ_i Λ^{W−}_{ττ′}(θ*_f, φ*_f)_i, with the projection operator applied event by event, assuming V−A decay (Eq. 6). Binning is in cosθ_{W−}, with N_k events in bin k.
  - Frame for the SDM analysis (Sec. 3): not stated [verifier wR_c]. The quote "in the corresponding W rest frame" (Sec. 2) refers to the coupling-extraction angles. The angle definition is in Fig. 2 (left), figure-only.
  - CPT and CP relations for tree-level W-pair production (Eqs. 7–8): CPT: Im(ρ^{W−}_{ττ′}) + Im(ρ^{W+}_{−τ−τ′}) = 0. CP: Im(ρ^{W−}_{ττ′}) − Im(ρ^{W+}_{−τ−τ′}) = 0.
  - Longitudinal fraction: "The fraction of longitudinally polarised W bosons" (Sec. 3). Its exact definition (σ_L/σ_total, or in terms of ρ_00) is not stated.

## Caveats

- Single-author LEP summary (Delmeire). The SDM results are quoted from experimental papers I did not read: L3 Note 2793 [11], OPAL Eur. Phys. J. C19 (2001) [12], DELPHI CONF 526 [13], CERN-EP/2002-092 [14].
- The paper is a summary. The figure values (Figs. 4, 5, 6) are not tabulated here, so none are recorded.
- The abstract [verifier w3_2: not Sec. 1] says "Limits are set on CP-violating couplings by a Spin Density Matrix analysis". Sec. 3 presents the same OPAL couplings as "results" with two-sided errors. The CL / interval definition is not stated.
- Only the stat/syst split is given for OPAL; for L3 it is not stated.
- Fig. 5: the text says the CPT test is "(right)" and the CP test is "(left)". The caption says the sum (CPT) is "(left)" and the difference (CP) is "(right)". The text and caption disagree. Not resolved here.
