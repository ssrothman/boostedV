- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Density matrix dρ^V_{h'h} for V = W, Z with helicities h, h' = +, -, 0 (Eq. 1, Sec. "Energy correlators & decay density matrix"). The decay part is dρ^V_{h'h} ∝ e^{i(h-h')φ}, so off-diagonal entries (|Δh| = 1, 2) carry the azimuthal dependence (Sec. "Off-diagonal entries").
  - Frame: vector-boson rest frame for the decay amplitudes (Eq. 6). θ* is "the angle between the direction of flight of the vector and the helicity-plus fermion". Lab-frame LO amplitudes in terms of the positive-helicity quark energy fraction x (Eq. 8). Azimuthal angle φ is the one defined with respect to the plane formed with the beam (Fig. 1 caption).
  - Amplitudes (Eq. 6, LO): A_± = g_V m_V e^{±iφ} (1 ± cos θ*)/2, A_0 = −g_V m_V sinθ*/√2.
  - Diagonal entries used: dρ_00 and dρ_TT ≡ dρ_++ + dρ_-- (Sec. "Diagonal entries"). The paper gives dρ_00 ∼ x(1−x) and dρ_TT ∼ x² + (1−x)², with no normalization stated.
  - f_L, f_0, A_0–A_7 (angular coefficients) and their definitions: not stated; the paper does not use them.
  - Asymmetries (theory/projected observables): A_LT = (A_+ − A_-)/(A_+ + A_-), with A_± the energy accumulated at z above/below z^peak (Sec. "Diagonal entries"). A_int = (N_+ − N_-)/(N_+ + N_-), with N_+ = f(π/4 ≤ |φ| < 3π/4) and N_- = f(0 ≤ |φ| < π/4) + f(3π/4 ≤ |φ| < π) (Sec. "Off-diagonal entries").

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

None assessed. The paper states: "This, together with the assessment of the different theoretical and experimental uncertainties is postponed for future work." (Conclusions and outlook.) The Fig. 6 projection assumes 10% (solid) and 3% (dashed) systematic uncertainty on the inclusive cross-section measurement, and negligible systematics for A_int (Sec. "Off-diagonal entries", Fig. 6 text). This is an assumption, not a result.

## Caveats

- Theory and projection only. No measured polarization fraction, no measured angular coefficient, no measured density-matrix element. Q2 and Q3 are answered "none measured" for this paper.
- Q3 map: the paper gives the density-matrix amplitudes in the vector rest frame (Eq. 6) and in the lab frame (Eq. 8), but no map from A_0–A_7 to ρ_{λλ'}. Its decay density matrix is in terms of energy-correlator variables (z, φ), not angular coefficients.
- Predictions are LO in z* = m_V²/E², with parton-level analytic formulae; hadronic corrections are stated to be O(z*^{1/2}) at the LHC (Sec. "Towards the LHC").
- The paper says the LHC predictions are "immediately imported" with "mild effects" expected for the azimuthal distribution of off-diagonal entries because of boosts along the beam axis, and that "the assessment of the sensitivity requires further study."
- INSPIRE lists categories hep-ph and hep-ex; the paper is theory (hep-ph) with no experimental data.
