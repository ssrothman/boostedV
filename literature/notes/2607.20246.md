# 2607.20246 — Evidence for longitudinally polarised Z bosons in electroweak W±Z production in association with two jets from pp collisions at 13 and 13.6 TeV with the ATLAS detector

- ATLAS Collaboration, 2026 (arXiv v1 submitted 22 Jul 2026; arXiv:2607.20246 [hep-ex], CERN-EP-2026-213, ATLAS STDM-2024-03). Journal ref: preprint (the LaTeX source sets `\AtlasJournalJHEP`, which is a template macro, not a confirmed journal reference). INSPIRE recid 3182511. HEPData record ins3182511, DOI 10.17182/hepdata.180113.v1 (v1).
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV (Run 2, 140 fb-1) and 13.6 TeV (Run 3, 164 fb-1); 304 fb-1 combined (Conclusion). Data only; no projection.
- Topology / final state: EW production pp → W±Z jj, fully leptonic (W→ℓν, Z→ℓℓ, ℓ = e or μ), with two tagging jets. Background WZjj-QCD (67% of pre-fit SR per Sec. 6), tt̄+V, tZj, ZZ, misidentified leptons. Signal: WZjj-EW (about 13% of SR).
- Selection summary:
  - Fiducial, particle level (Sec. 3): dressed leptons |η|<2.5; p_T(ℓ from Z) > 15 GeV; p_T(ℓ from W) > 20 GeV; |m_ℓℓ − m_Z^PDG| < 10 GeV; m_T^W > 30 GeV; ΔR(ℓ_W, ℓ_Z) > 0.3; ΔR(ℓ_Z, ℓ_Z) > 0.2.
  - Fiducial jets (Sec. 3): anti-k_t, R = 0.4; p_T > 40 GeV; |η| < 4.5; ΔR(j, ℓ) > 0.3; two highest-p_T jets in opposite hemispheres with m_jj > 500 GeV.
  - Detector level (Sec. 5): exactly three leptons (e/μ) with p_T > 15 GeV (W lepton > 20 GeV, tight ID/isolation); fourth loose lepton vetoed; Z pair within 10 GeV of m_Z; m_T^W > 30 GeV; anti-k_t R = 0.4 jets, p_T > 25 GeV, |η| < 4.5, with jet-vertex and forward-jet-vertex tagging; tagging jets p_T > 40 GeV, opposite η sign.
  - Signal region: m_jj > 500 GeV and no b-tagged jet (Sec. 5). Run 2 and Run 3 combined in a simultaneous profile-likelihood fit (Sec. 7).
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Joint polarisation fractions f_00, f_0T, f_T0, f_TT of the W and Z bosons, "composed of diagonal elements of the full spin density matrix" (Sec. 3). Single-boson fractions f_0X (W longitudinal, Z any), f_X0 (Z longitudinal, W any), f_TX, f_XT (Sec. 3). Normalisation: f_00 + f_0X + f_X0 + f_XX = 1, with f_XX = both bosons transverse (HEPData Table 5 description).
  - Frame: "fix the reference z-axis to the direction of the corresponding boson in the diboson centre-of-mass frame [19]. This definition is used for this measurement." (Sec. 3, ref [19] Denner and Pelliccioli). Whether this is called the helicity frame is not stated.
  - Fractions are "effective fractions" containing non-resonant contributions in the fiducial phase space (Sec. 3). Interference between polarisation states is not considered separately (Sec. 3).
  - Fiducial, integrated over the fiducial region; no p_T or other differential polarisation result (none in text or HEPData).
  - Fitted quantities: each fit uses the targeted fraction and sigma_WZjj-EW (Sec. 7); the paper reports mu_WZjj-EW = sigma/sigma_Sherpa, not extracted here.

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

Relative uncertainties in % for f_X0 (Table 3, Sec. 8; HEPData Table 3). The paper states "The uncertainty related to data statistics is dominant in the measurement of the fX0 polarisation fraction and determines its precision." (Sec. 9)

- Statistics: 26.6 % (total 27.1 %). Quote: "Statistics | 26.6 | 13.8" (HEPData Table 3).
- Jets: 3.6 % (largest systematic). Quote: "Jets | 3.6 | 6.6" (HEPData Table 3).
- MC statistics: 2.4 %. Quote: "MC statistics | 2.4 | 1.1" (HEPData Table 3).
- WZjj-EW theory modelling: 1.7 %; WZjj-QCD theory modelling: 1.6 %. Quotes: "$WZjj\text{-EW}$ Theory modelling | 1.7 | 4.9" and "$WZjj\text{-QCD}$ Theory modelling | 1.6 | 5.0" (HEPData Table 3).
- Electrons: 1.3 %. Quote: "Electrons | 1.3 | 1.6" (HEPData Table 3).
- All systematics: 5.3 % in paper Table 3 (Sec. 8) and 5.2 % in HEPData Table 3 (see Open issues).
- Luminosity: 0.4 %. Pile-up: 0.5 %. Muons: 0.8 %. Other backgrounds: 0.8 %. Misid. leptons background: 0.6 %. b-tagging: 0.5 %. PDFs: 0.3 %. E_T^miss: 0.3 %. Each from Table 3 (Sec. 8) and HEPData Table 3.
- Text (Sec. 8): "The dominant source of uncertainty is the jet energy calibration and the modelling of pile-up." This is a general statement and is not specific to f_X0; Table 3 gives the f_X0 breakdown.
- Shape modelling of Voronoi-region templates (Sec. 8): global modelling uncertainty of polarised WZjj-EW templates "by at most 20%" in low S/B-score regions, and NLO EW shape effect "of maximum size of 20%". Not separately listed in Table 3.

## Caveats

- Fractions depend on the reference frame (Sec. 3: "Polarisation fractions are not Lorentz invariant and hence depend on a chosen reference frame.").
- Each fraction is extracted with the other polarisation proportions fixed to MC: "For each polarisation fraction measurement, the respective proportions of polarisation states among the non-measured ones are taken from the MC prediction." (Sec. 7). The paper states fits with floating proportions "yield consistent results" (Sec. 7); those results are not tabulated.
- f_X0 uncertainty is dominated by statistics (Table 3, 26.6 %), so the precision is far coarser than the SM prediction uncertainty quoted in Table 5 (±0.0008).
- The W-longitudinal and both-longitudinal results are upper limits and an observed significance is claimed only for f_X0.
- Signal samples are Sherpa 3 at LO with up to one extra parton (Sec. 4); MadGraph5_aMC@NLO is used for alternative modelling uncertainties. The paper states interference between polarisation states (about 2% per NLO EW, Sec. 3) is "effectively absorbed in the measured polarisation fractions".
- Results are quoted for the particle-level fiducial region (Sec. 3). The fit parameter is "the targeted particle-level fraction" (Sec. 7). No unfolding step is described in the sections read.

## Open issues

1. HEPData vs paper Table 5 uncertainties disagree. f_00: HEPData stat +0.053/−0.000 vs paper ±0.134. f_0X: HEPData stat +0.093/−0.000 vs paper ±0.150; HEPData syst +0.009/−0.007 vs paper ±0.018. f_X0: HEPData stat +0.091/−0.084 vs paper ±0.086; syst +0.017/−0.018 vs paper ±0.017. The local files do not say why (possibly one-sided or asymmetric likelihood-scan intervals). Both sets are copied as given; neither was reconciled.
2. Table 3 "All systematics" for f_X0: 5.3 % (paper Sec. 8 table) vs 5.2 % (HEPData Table 3). Not resolved.
3. Q3 applicability: the paper measures three joint or single-boson fractions (f_00, f_0X, f_X0), which the paper describes as diagonal elements of the spin density matrix. It does not measure off-diagonal elements, angular coefficients A_0–A_7, or entanglement observables, and it treats interference as absorbed (Sec. 3). Whether three diagonal elements satisfies "more than one element" is for the lead to decide.
4. The CL method for the upper limits is not stated in the paper text.
5. The helicity-frame name is not stated; the frame is described only as in Sec. 3 quote above.
6. Journal reference unconfirmed (preprint only).
7. Not extracted (outside Q2/Q3): cross-section fb value from HEPData Table 1 and the paper (0.374 fb with stat/syst/lumi), mu_WZjj-EW (HEPData Table 5 row, 1.06 with stat 0.15 / syst 0.11 / lumi 0.01), event yields (HEPData Tables 2, 4), and distribution tables (HEPData Tables 6–25).
8. The arXiv HTML rendering was used for the text. The PDF was not read. Math quotes use the HTML alttext rendering.
9. Figures 5 and 6 repeat Table 5 values; no figure-only values were recorded.
