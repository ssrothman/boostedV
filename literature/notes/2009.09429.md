# 2009.09429 — Measurements of production cross sections of polarized same-sign W boson pairs in association with two jets in pp collisions at 13 TeV

- Authors/Collaboration: CMS Collaboration (Sirunyan, A. M. et al., 2352 authors per INSPIRE); preprint 2020-09-20 (arXiv v2, CMS-SMP-20-006); journal: Phys. Lett. B 812 (2021) 136018 per INSPIRE `publication_info` (year field 2021, free text "Phys. Lett. B 812 (2020) 136018"; year discrepancy noted under Open issues); INSPIRE recid 1818160; HEPData record ins1818160 (prefetched; `record.json` has no title or inspire_id field)
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV, integrated luminosity 137 fb^-1 (2016–2018, three data sets analysed independently), data (no projection). Simulation used for templates and signal modelling.
- Topology / final state: same-sign WW → ℓ±ν ℓ±ν (ℓ = e, μ) plus two jets, EW VBS production; polarization-resolved (W_L W_L, W_L W_T, W_T W_T). Fiducial cross sections quoted as σ×B(WW→ℓνℓν).
- Selection summary (from Sec. 0.4 text):
  - Exactly two same-sign leptons (e or μ); leading lepton p_T > 25 GeV, subleading > 20 GeV; m_ℓℓ > 20 GeV; Z-veto (|m_ℓℓ − m_Z| > 15 GeV) for ee only; MET > 30 GeV.
  - Two highest-p_T jets: m_jj > 500 GeV, |Δη_jj| > 2.5; jets with p_T > 50 GeV required (text: "at least two jets with $\pt^{\mathrm{j}}>50\GeV$"); b-jet veto.
  - Jet clustering at reco level: not verified in this pass. Generator-level fiducial jets: anti-k_T, R = 0.4 (stated in Sec. 0.8 and HEPData Tables 6/7).
  - Multivariate: inclusive BDT (EW vs background) then two signal BDTs (LL vs XT; LX vs TT), TMVA; fit uses 5×5 bins in SR (text: "five bins in the inclusive BDT and five bins in the corresponding signal BDT").
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Observable: fiducial cross sections σ×B for helicity combinations W_L W_L, W_X W_T, W_L W_X, W_T W_T, where X = either polarization state. Paper: "Ideally, we would measure all three contributions separately, but the current data sample size is too limited" (Sec. 0.1 text, line 49).
  - No polarization fraction (f_L, f_0, A_0, ρ_00 or equivalent) is measured or quoted in this paper. Per the question scope these are the closest quantities; they are not fractions and no fraction has been computed here.
  - Reference frames: two sets of results, helicity eigenstates defined in (a) the WW centre-of-mass frame, or (b) the initial-state parton–parton centre-of-mass frame. The paper's exact mathematical definition of the helicity vectors in each frame is not stated; it refers to Ref. [68] (Ballestrero, Maina, Pelliccioli, PLB 811 (2020) 135856, "Different polarization definitions in same-sign WW scattering at the LHC").
  - Fiducial region (generator level, dressed leptons with ΔR = 0.1 photon recombination): leptons p_T > 20 GeV, |η| < 2.5, m_ℓℓ > 20 GeV; jets p_T > 50 GeV, |η| < 4.7, anti-k_T R = 0.4; m_jj > 500 GeV, |Δη_jj| > 2.5; jets within ΔR < 0.4 of a lepton excluded (HEPData Tables 6/7 description).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Statistical uncertainty dominates for all four channels (Table 4: 123% LL, 15% XT, 42% LX, 22% TT).
- Among systematics for LL, the paper's largest entries in Table 4 are limited sample size (38%) and nonprompt-lepton rate (17%), followed by JES/resolution (11%). Text (Sec. 0.7): "The uncertainties associated with the limited numbers of simulated events and of data events used to estimate the nonprompt lepton background are also included as systematic uncertainties with the latter being the dominant contribution."
- For LX, the largest systematics are limited sample size (14%) and nonprompt-lepton rate (9.3%).
- Theory (scale and PDF) is 6.8% for LL and 4.0% for LX (Table 4).

## Caveats

- No polarization fraction (f_L, f_0, A_0, ρ_00) is reported. Measured quantities are fiducial cross sections and 95% CL upper limits on the polarized LL cross section, not fractions. Per the question scope, these are reported as the closest quantities only.
- The LL and LX results are measured with a small signal yield: the LL signal-region yield is 16.0 ± 18.3 events in the SR (HEPData Table 5, "Yields in WW signal region"; paper's yield table with best-fit normalisation). For LL the paper reports a 95% CL upper limit (no significance is quoted for LL).
- The WW-CM-frame and parton-parton-frame results are two separate sets with different cross-section values; the paper notes the parton frame "changes the respective contributions" of the polarized states.
- Level: the cross sections are fiducial, defined at generator level (dressed leptons, anti-k_T R = 0.4 jets). Detector-level selection efficiency and unfolding are not described in the extracted text; the signal extraction uses simulated templates in the fit.
- Signal modelling: MadGraph5_aMC@NLO 2.7.2 polarized LO (O(α^6)) with NLO-type corrections O(α_s α^6) and O(α^7) applied to the LO cross sections for the WW processes; Phantom 1.5.1 used for cross-checks. Off-shell and nonresonant contributions (1–2% in the fiducial region) are not included in the signal samples.
- Interference between EW and QCD diagrams is included separately (Interference-WW: 8.4 ± 0.6 events in SR, Table 5 / text table).
- Measured on data (not projection). Upper limits and significances are from binned profile-likelihood fits with the paper's own systematic model.
