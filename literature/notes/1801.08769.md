# 1801.08769 — Search for light resonances decaying to boosted quark pairs and produced in association with a photon or a jet in pp collisions at √s = 13 TeV with the ATLAS detector

- Collaboration: ATLAS (analysis code EXOT-2017-01). Year: 2019 (INSPIRE). Journal: Phys. Lett. B 788 (2019) 316 (INSPIRE; DOI 10.1016/j.physletb.2018.09.062). Text read: arXiv:1801.08769v2 (HTML, 11 Dec 2018), not the journal version. INSPIRE recid 1650592. HEPData record: none (HTTP 404 for ins1650592; search for arXiv:1801.08769 returned 0 hits).
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 36.1 fb⁻¹ (Abstract; Sec. 3: "The data used correspond to an integrated luminosity of 36.1 fb-1."); real data.
- Topology / final state: Z′ → q q̄ reconstructed as one trimmed large-R jet, produced with a hard ISR jet or ISR photon. Two channels: ISR jet and ISR γ, combined for the g_q limit.
- Selection summary:
  - Large-R jets: anti-kt R = 1.0, |η| < 2.0, trimmed with R_subjet = 0.2 and f_cut = 5% (Sec. 4: "The trimming parameters optimised for this search are Rsubjet=0.2 and fcut=5%").
  - Resonance candidate: p_T^J > 2 × m_J, τ21^DDT < 0.50, ρ^DDT > 1.5 (Sec. 4: "τ21DDT<0.50 to suppress backgrounds and ρDDT>1.5").
  - ISR jet: leading narrow (anti-kt R = 0.4, |η| < 2.4) jet with p_T > 420 GeV; ISR photon: leading photon with p_T > 155 GeV (Sec. 4: "The ISR jet is the leading narrow jet with pTj>420 GeV").
  - Δφ ≥ π/2 between the candidate and the ISR object (Sec. 4: "azimuthal angular separation of at least Δϕ=π/2").
  - Signal region mass windows scale with m_Z′; background from a transfer-factor method with a Gaussian-process interpolation (Sec. 5).
- Model or observable definition (frame/convention): Leptophobic axial-vector Z′ with only hadronic couplings. Sec. 3: "The coupling of the Z′ to quarks, gq, is set to be universal in quark flavour and equal to 0.5." Samples: "A set of samples was generated with mZ′ between 100 and 220 GeV, in 30 GeV steps." Sec. 3: "The corresponding total width ΓZ′ is negligible compared to the experimental resolution, which is about 10% of the boson mass." Limits on g_q are reported as a function of m_Z′; "The narrow width approximation is valid for the gq range tested" (Sec. 6). Polarization / reference frame: not applicable (no polarization observable in this analysis).

## Caveats

- Mass range: the quoted g_q and σ × A limits cover m_Z′ = 100–220 GeV only (Sec. 6). They do not cover the 10 GeV–1 TeV range of Q1.
- The model is a single benchmark (leptophobic axial-vector, universal g_q). Limits are not given for other spins, couplings, or decay modes.
- Acceptance times efficiency and the cross-section limits are model-dependent per the paper ("it can vary significantly for various theoretical models", Sec. 6).
- Interpolation: samples at 30 GeV steps, with interpolation in 10 GeV steps between them (Sec. 3). Values at intermediate mass points are not quoted in text.
- The Sec. 6 significance statements are about excesses, not limits: 2.5σ local (1.1σ global) at 150 GeV in the ISR jet channel with a Z′ fit; 2.2σ local (0.8σ global) at 140 GeV in the ISR γ channel.
- The text cites g_q limits from CMS as 0.1–0.25 over 50–300 GeV (Sec. 1); these are as the paper states them and were not checked against the CMS papers.
