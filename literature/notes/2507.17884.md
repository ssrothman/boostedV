# 2507.17884 — Search for resonant production of pairs of dijet resonances through broad mediators in proton-proton collisions at sqrt(s) = 13 TeV

- Collaboration: CMS (EXO-24-038). Year: 2025 (INSPIRE preprint date 2025-07-23). The cached arXiv HTML header reads "arXiv:2507.17884v2 [hep-ex] 27 Oct 2026". Journal ref: preprint (INSPIRE publication_info empty). INSPIRE recid 2954159. HEPData record 159918, version 1 (DOI 10.17182/hepdata.159918.v1; INSPIRE key ins2954159).
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 138 fb^-1 (2016–2018, Sec. 0.1). Data: reinterpretation of the data of the narrow-resonance search of Ref. [1]. Simulation: MadGraph5_aMC@NLO 2.6.5, Pythia 8.205, GEANT4 (Sec. 0.2) for the benchmark. No projection.
- Topology / final state: pp -> Y -> XX -> (jj)(jj), all-hadronic, four resolved jets (Eq. 1). Y = scalar color-sextet diquark S_uu or S_dd, mass M_Y (= M_S), width Gamma/M_Y = 1.5, 5, 10%. X = vector-like quark (chi or omega) [verifier w2_2: was "charm"] decaying at one loop to g + q (Eq. 2), with narrow intrinsic width. alpha_true = M_X/M_Y; main results at 0.25, other alpha_true (0.11–0.42) in Figs. 9–10.
- Scope check: hadronic final state with pair-produced hadronic resonances (in scope for Q1). Mass range 2.0–10.0 TeV, entirely OUTSIDE the 10 GeV–1 TeV primary window. The paper states the mass is "massive ( > 2.0 TeV )" (Sec. 0.1).

Selection summary:
- Event reconstruction, selection and jet pairing are "identical to those of Ref. [1]" (Sec. 0.3). Jet algorithm and R are not stated in this paper. Requirements stated here: dijet mass > 0.2 TeV and four-jet mass > 1.6 TeV (Abstract).
- Observable: m_4j in 13 bins of alpha = mbar_2j / m_4j: 12 bins of width 0.02 for 0.10 < alpha < 0.34, plus one bin for alpha > 0.34 (Sec. 0.3).
- Signal efficiency: "epsilon is the fraction of signal events satisfying m 4j > 1.6 TeV" (Sec. 0.3). A: "the fraction of generated events passing the kinematic selection criteria" (Sec. 0.3).
- Fit: multibin Poisson counting likelihood, simultaneous over the 13 alpha bins; background from parametric functions (ModDijet-3p/5p primary), discrete profiling of the function choice (Sec. 0.4–0.5).
- Mass scan: M_Y from 2 to 10 TeV in 0.1 TeV steps, Gamma/M_Y = 1.5, 5, 10% (Sec. 0.5).

Model or observable definition (frame/convention): limit on sigma x B x A (cross section x branching fraction x acceptance) in pb at 95% CL for pp -> Y -> XX. The paper states "Limits are quoted within the acceptance, as presented in Fig. 4, and corrected for the efficiency" (Sec. 0.5). Frame / polarization: not applicable (scalar Y; the paper reports no angular or polarization observables; "not stated"). No coupling limit g_q is given in this paper. Mass limits on S_uu / S_dd are derived by comparison with a leading-order prediction (Sec. 0.5).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Background modelling: "The main source of the uncertainty in the background modeling comes from the choice of the background function and the range of possible parameter values of the background function" (Sec. 0.5). Discrete profiling is used for the function choice (Sec. 0.5).
- Jet energy scale ±2%, four-jet and dijet mass resolution ±10%, integrated luminosity ±1.6% (Sec. 0.5). Quote: "the jet energy scale ( ± 2%), mass resolution of the four-jet and dijet resonances ( ± 10%) and the integrated luminosity ( ± 1.6%)". Implemented as Gaussian constraints for JES and resolution, log-normal for luminosity (Sec. 0.5).
- Systematics "identical to those presented in Ref. [1]" (Sec. 0.5). No stat / syst split and no magnitude per source is given for any limit value. Observed HEPData entries carry no uncertainty (hide: true). Expected bands are the only uncertainties in HEPData.

## Caveats

- Mass window: all limits are at M_Y = 2.0–10.0 TeV (OUTSIDE the 10 GeV–1 TeV window). Nothing in this paper falls inside the window.
- Width: the paper states that "The data in both searches were used to constrain narrow mediators, with natural widths less than 0.5 % of their mass", where both CMS [1] and ATLAS [2] are meant (Sec. 0.1). This analysis extends to widths of 1.5–10% of M_Y.
- Limit form: sigma x B x A (pb), with A "the fraction of generated events passing the kinematic selection criteria" (Sec. 0.3). Mass limits use a leading-order prediction adjusted by an efficiency factor: "we adjust the cross section from the theoretical prediction using an efficiency factor that isolates the m 4j range where the expected significance exceeds 99% of that obtained without the correction" (Sec. 0.5).
- Observed vs expected: "the observed limit is greater than the expected limit at resonance masses of around 2, 4, and 8 TeV, and less than the expected limit near a resonance mass of 3 TeV" (Sec. 0.5).
- Excesses (significances, not limits): "For a width of 1.5%, the most significant signal hypothesis occurs at a four-jet resonance mass of 8.6 TeV", local 3.9 s.d., global 1.6 s.d. (Sec. 0.5). "A second excess is observed at a four-jet resonance mass of 3.6 TeV, alpha true = 0.29, and 10% width with a local and global significance of 3.9 and 2.2 s.d. respectively" (Sec. 0.5). Best-fit sigma x B x A values for these excesses are in a text table and were not extracted.
- Level: particle vs detector level is not stated explicitly; the signal is simulated with the CMS detector model (GEANT4, Sec. 0.2). Measured on data (not projected, not a recast of other experiments).
- Model dependence: limits are on sigma x B x A for the diquark benchmark topology (alpha_true dependence in Figs. 9–10), not on a generic coupling g_q.

## Open issues

- HEPData group mapping: the JSON headers list the columns in order (x; observed; expected; S_uu; S_dd) and group indices 0–3 follow that order. HEPData gives no explicit rule. Cross-check: the observed-vs-width tables group 2 (10%) equals the 10%-width tables group 0 at 2.0 TeV and 10.0 TeV for alpha_true = 0.11 (t35 g2 "0.0018484638" and "0.0001317288" = t23 g0). Other alpha were not cross-checked point by point.
- HEPData "-" entries (no value in HEPData; copied as "-"): t24 x=8.0 group 0; t34 x=5.0 group 0; t36 x=5.0 group 1; t36 x=8.0 group 2; t46 x=5.0 group 1; t46 x=5.0 group 2. Entries at other masses were not inspected.
- Paper table numbers for the mass-limit table and the excess best-fit table are unknown (LaTeXML labels only). Quotes are from the text dump.
- Jet algorithm, R, pT and eta cuts for this paper are not stated here (refer to Ref. [1]); not extracted.
- Full mass-point rows at 2.1–10.0 TeV (other than the reference masses above) and the S_uu / S_dd predicted-cross-section columns were not extracted, per the window rule.
- Local p-value tables and acceptance / efficiency tables in the HEPData record were not extracted (not limits).
- Stat vs syst split and per-source magnitudes for the limits are not provided by the paper.
- The HEPData record is version 1; the arXiv HTML header is v2 (27 Oct 2026). Values were not checked against any other version.
