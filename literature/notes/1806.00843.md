# 1806.00843 — Search for narrow and broad dijet resonances in proton-proton collisions at √s = 13 TeV and constraints on dark matter mediators and other new particles

- CMS Collaboration (CMS-EXO-16-056), 2018. JHEP 08 (2018) 130, DOI 10.1007/JHEP08(2018)130 (INSPIRE). arXiv v2 dated 01 Sep 2018. INSPIRE recid 1676214. HEPData record ins1676214 (DOI 10.17182/hepdata.80166.v1), tables t1–t5 (see Open issues for which are used).
- √s = 13 TeV. Integrated luminosity up to 36 fb⁻¹; low-mass search uses 27 fb⁻¹ (Sec. "Trigger and minimum dijet mass"). Data, not simulation. Limits are from data, with MC-based signal shapes.
- Topology / final state: s-channel dijet resonances decaying to qq̄, qg, gg (single production). Narrow (benchmark models) and broad spin-1 and spin-2 (width up to 30% of mass). Dark-matter mediator (vector and axial-vector) and leptophobic Z′ coupling-limit scans.
- Selection summary:
  - Anti-kT R = 0.4 jets; "wide jets" formed by adding jets within ΔR < 1.1 of the two leading jets. Jets pT > 30 GeV, |η| < 2.5. Jet ID applied (Sec. "Offline calibration and jet identification"; Sec. "Wide jet reconstruction and event selection").
  - Dijet |Δη| < 1.3 between the two wide jets.
  - High-mass search (1.6 TeV and above): offline PF-jets, 36 fb⁻¹, m_jj > 1.25 TeV, at least one vertex required; trigger = logical OR of five HLT paths (HT > 800 GeV early, HT > 900 GeV later, and three single-jet paths: PF-jet pT > 500 GeV, Calo-jet pT > 500 GeV, PF-jet R = 0.8 pT > 450 GeV).
  - Low-mass search (0.6–1.6 TeV): HLT Calo-jets via data scouting, HT > 250 GeV (Calo-jets pT > 40 GeV in HT), m_jj > 0.49 TeV, 27 fb⁻¹.
  - Both searches fit background with dijet parameterizations (Eq. 1 for high-mass, Eq. 2 with extra P4 ln²x term for low-mass); no narrow excess (largest local excess 0.79–0.89 TeV, local significances 1.2/1.6/1.9 σ for qq/qg/gg at 0.85 TeV, text quote in Sec. "Signal shapes, injection tests, and significance").
- Model or observable definition:
  - Limit observable: 95% CL upper limit on σ × B × A for narrow resonance of parton pair qq̄ (or qq), qg, gg, Sec. "Limits on the resonance production cross section". Kinematic requirements |Δη| < 1.3 and |η| < 2.5 per jet. Minimum-m_jj acceptance is corrected into the limit, not into A. Predictions in narrow-width approximation, LO CTEQ6L1 with NLO K-factors (Sec. "Limits on the resonance mass for benchmark models").
  - Method: asymptotic CLs (modified frequentist), multi-bin Poisson likelihood, mass steps 50 GeV (low) and 100 GeV (high), truncation at 150% of resonance mass on the high tail.
  - Coupling limits: g_q′ for leptophobic Z′ (width formula Eq. 5, not transcribed; see Open issues); g_q for DM mediator with g_DM = 1.0, m_DM = 1 GeV, Dirac DM, vector or axial-vector mediator (Sec. "Limits on a dark matter mediator" and "Limits on broad resonances").
  - Broad resonance: relativistic Breit–Wigner with PYTHIA 8 mass-dependent width replacement; spin-1 (qq̄) and spin-2 (qq̄, gg) shapes (Sec. "Breit–Wigner distributions").
  - Reference frame / polarization: not applicable (no polarization observable measured or quoted; no helicity or Collins–Soper frame stated).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Background parameterization (profiled in the likelihood): "For almost all resonance mass values, the background systematic uncertainty produces the majority of the effect on the limit shown here." (Sec. "Systematic uncertainty and statistical methodology", Fig. 10). Largest for gg, smallest for qq; most severe at lowest masses in each search.
- Jet energy scale: "The uncertainty in the jet energy scale in both the low-mass and the high-mass search is 2%" (Sec. "Systematic uncertainty and statistical methodology"). Applied as ±2% shift of signal shape. Nuisance with Gaussian constraint.
- Jet energy resolution: "The uncertainty in the jet energy resolution translates into an uncertainty of 10% in the resolution of the dijet mass" (same section). Gaussian constraint.
- Integrated luminosity: "The uncertainty in the integrated luminosity is 2.5%" (same section). Log-normal constraint.
- Statistical type: each of the above is stated as a nuisance parameter in the likelihood. The paper does not split these into stat-only, syst-only and lumi-only limits in tables; the statistical-only effect appears only as Fig. 10 (ratio of limits with systematics to limits without), figure-only.

Category note: stat vs syst split for the tabulated limits is not given in HEPData or in text tables. Observed and expected bands in HEPData (Coupling tables) are the 1 and 2 s.d. ranges as given by the paper. The paper does not state how the bands were computed beyond "the expected limits ... and their variation at the 1 and 2 standard deviation levels" (Fig. 13 and Fig. 19 captions).

## Caveats

- Parton-level σBA: "All upper limits presented can be compared to the parton-level predictions of σ B A , without detector simulation," (Sec. "Limits on the resonance mass for benchmark models"). Limits are for σ × B × A with |Δη| < 1.3 and |η| < 2.5. Minimum-m_jj acceptance is corrected into the limit.
- Interference: "Interference between the signal and the background processes is model dependent and not considered in this analysis." (Sec. "Signal shapes, injection tests, and significance"). For broad resonances, t-channel exchange and interference are also not considered (Sec. "Validity tests of the limits").
- Diquark signal: "tending to make the quoted limits in the quark-quark channel conservative when applied to diquark signals" (Sec. "Signal shapes, injection tests, and significance").
- Narrow-width validity: narrow-resonance limits are approximately valid only up to width ~10% (coupling g′_q < 0.45 for Z′; g_q ~0.4 for DM mediator). Above that, the broad-resonance results apply.
- Model predictions: LO CTEQ6L1 in the narrow-width approximation, with NLO K-factors (W′ K ≈ 1.3, Z′ K ≈ 1.3, axigluon/coloron K from 1.08 to 1.33). Limits are not recomputed for different PDFs.
- Mass limits (Table in Sec. "Limits on the resonance mass for benchmark models") are derived by comparing theory cross sections to the observed limits; they are not HEPData values.
- Low-mass search uses trigger-level Calo-jets with a calibration to PF-jet response (Sec. "Calibration of wide jets in the low-mass search"). It covers 27 fb⁻¹ only.
