# 2310.04350 — Evidence of pair production of longitudinally polarised vector bosons and study of CP properties in ZZ→4ℓ events with the ATLAS detector at √s = 13 TeV

- Authors/Collaboration: ATLAS (first author Aad, Georges et al.), 2023; JHEP 12 (2023) 107, DOI 10.1007/JHEP12(2023)107; INSPIRE recid 2709671; HEPData record 143611 (INSPIRE ins2709671, version 1), single table "Table 1".
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 140 fb⁻¹ (text, Sec. 3 and abstract). HEPData qualifier says 139 fb⁻¹ (see Open issues). Data (Run 2, 2015–2018); simulation for templates (MadGraph5_aMC@NLO polarised samples, Sherpa, MoCaNLO reweighting).
- Topology / final state: pp → ZZ → 4ℓ (ℓ = e or μ), SFOC pairs; 4e, 4μ, 2e2μ. Q2 = polarisation fraction of LL events (fiducial, particle-level after unfolding for the cross-section). Q3 = one CP-sensitive optimal observable (not a set of spin-density elements).
- Selection summary (fiducial, particle level, Sec. 4.1 and 4.3):
  - Dressed prompt leptons: e pT > 7 GeV, |η| < 2.47; μ pT > 5 GeV, |η| < 2.7; photon dressing cone ΔR < 0.1.
  - Leading and sub-leading leptons pT > 20 GeV; ΔR_ℓℓ > 0.05; m_ℓℓ > 5 GeV for any SFOC pair.
  - Two SFOC pairs on-shell at Z mass; m_4ℓ > 180 GeV. On-shell window |m_ℓℓ − m_Z| < 10 GeV for each SFOC pair (arxiv.html alttext "|m_{\ell\ell}-m_{Z}|<10", Sec. 4.1/4.3) [verifier w3_3; lead checked alttext].
  - Jets: anti-kT, R = 0.4, pT > 15 GeV (reco level; not used in the fiducial definition for the polarisation result).
  - Z1 is the pair with larger |y_ℓℓ| (Sec. 4.3).
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Polarisation of the ZZ bosons is defined in the centre-of-mass frame of the two Z bosons (Sec. 3: "The ZZ-boson polarisation is defined in the centre-of-mass (CM) frame of the two ZZ bosons"; repeated in Sec. 6.1).
  - Polarisation states: Z_T Z_T, Z_T Z_L, Z_L Z_L, plus an interference term among polarisation states (Sec. 6.1, Steps 1–3).
  - f_L, f_0, A_0, ρ_00: not stated. The paper does not define any of these. The measured quantity is the signal-strength μ_LL, the normalisation of the Z_L Z_L template relative to the SM expectation (Sec. 6.1, verbatim: "The signal strength is the ratio of the measured signal contribution relative to the SM expectation.").
  - Angular inputs to the BDT (Sec. 6.1): cos θ1, cos θ3 (decay angles in the Z rest frame of the four-lepton system), cos θ*_Z1 (production angle in the 4ℓ rest frame), Δφ_ℓ1ℓ2, Δφ_ℓ3ℓ4.
  - CP observable (Sec. 6.2.1): T_yz,1(3) = sin φ1(3) × cos θ1(3); optimal observable O_{Tyz1 Tyz3} built from 2D grouping of (T_yz,1, T_yz,3), 30 bins. Reference frame for φ: x′y′z′ frame with z′ along Z1 direction in the 4ℓ rest frame, x′ in the reaction plane (Fig. 2). Note the frame is stated for the azimuthal angles; θ is defined as in Sec. 6.1.

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)
- Q2 (Sec. 8.1, Table 2, relative % of σ_fid(Z_L Z_L) from the fit): qq̄→ZZ interference modelling 6.9 %; NLO reweighting observable choice 3.7 %; PDF, αs and parton shower 2.2 %; gg→ZZ modelling 1.4 %; the paper states "with the leading contribution from the theoretical modelling of the polarisation templates."
- Q2 per Sec. 8.1: μ_LL systematic 0.11 (syst.) against stat 0.27; the paper states the increase of Z_L Z_L yield uncertainty is "mainly due to the statistical uncertainty of the signal strength after the fit."
- Q3 (Sec. 6.2.2 and Sec. 7): intrinsic unfolding bias (< 1 % per bin, taken as systematic); generator choice in unfolding negligible; theory effect on unfolded cross-section "minor." HEPData Table 1 gives per-bin syst.; no breakdown by source is given in HEPData.

## Caveats
- The Z_L Z_L result is the polarised-template signal strength μ_LL, relative to the SM template, not a measured polarisation fraction f_L; no f_L, f_0, A_0 or ρ_00 is defined or given in the paper.
- The fiducial cross-section is for the Z_L Z_L state only (2.45 fb), with the normalisation of the theoretical template removed in the conversion (Sec. 8.1).
- Q3: the only HEPData table is one CP-sensitive optimal observable, not multiple spin-density elements. Included because the paper frames it as interference-sensitive; this is a borderline Q3 fit, flagged for the lead to decide.
- The paper's fZ4 / fγ4 aNTGC limits (Table 3, Sec. 8.3) [verifier w3_3] are BSM couplings, not spin-density elements. Out of scope; not recorded.
- Figure-only values: none recorded. The pre/post-fit yields (Table 1 in the text; not in HEPData) are event yields, not polarisation elements; not recorded.
