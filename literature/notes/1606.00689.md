# 1606.00689 — Measurement of the angular coefficients in Z-boson events using electron and muon pairs from data taken at √s = 8 TeV with the ATLAS detector

- Authors/Collaboration: ATLAS Collaboration, 2016. Journal: JHEP 08 (2016) 159 (INSPIRE, DOI 10.1007/JHEP08(2016)159); this note reads arXiv v1 only (CERN-EP-2016-087). INSPIRE recid 1466778. HEPData record ins1466778 (76 tables, read in full).
- sqrt(s) = 8 TeV; integrated luminosity 20.3 fb^-1 (Sec. 3.2: "correspond to an integrated luminosity of 20.3 fb−1"); data (no projection).
- Topology / final state: pp → Z/γ* → ℓ⁺ℓ⁻ (ℓ = e, µ), Drell–Yan. Channels eeCC, µµCC, eeCF (Sec. 4.1). Measures the full A0–A7 set (all eight) and A0−A2, i.e. multiple density-matrix elements jointly.
- Selection summary:
  - Dilepton mass 80 < m_ll < 100 GeV (Sec. 4.1); central leptons |η| < 2.4, forward electron 2.5 < |η| < 4.9 (Sec. 3.1).
  - Lepton pT > 25 GeV (central), forward electron pT > 20 GeV (Sec. 4.1).
  - p_T^ll < 600 GeV (eeCC, µµCC) and < 100 GeV (eeCF) for the highest bin (Sec. 4.1).
  - yZ-integrated and yZ-binned in |y_ll| < 1, 1–2, 2–3.5 (Sec. 4.1). Measured in 23 pT^Z bins from 0 to 600 GeV (HEPData x-bins). Jet algorithm and R: not stated (no jet requirement appears in the Sec. 4.1 text read).
- Model or observable definition (frame/convention):
  - Frame: Collins–Soper (CS). z-axis is the "external bisector of the angle between the momenta of the two protons" in the Z rest frame; y-axis normal to the proton plane; θ_CS, φ_CS measured w.r.t. the negative lepton (Sec. 2).
  - Sign convention: "this paper adopts the recent convention ... whereby the coefficients A1 and A3 are positive" (Sec. 2).
  - Eq. (1): dσ/(dpT dy dm dcosθ dφ) = (3/16π)(dσ^{U+L}/dpT dy dm) × {(1+cos²θ) + ½A0(1−3cos²θ) + A1 sin2θ cosφ + ½A2 sin²θ cos2φ + A3 sinθ cosφ + A4 cosθ + A5 sin²θ sin2φ + A6 sin2θ sinφ + A7 sinθ sinφ}.
  - Appendix A (Eqs. 12–16) gives the density-matrix map. H_{mm'} = ε*_μ(m) H^{μν} ε_ν(m') (Eq. 12), with Z polarisation vectors in the Z rest frame (Eq. 13), in the CS frame. Helicity cross-sections σ_α are linear combinations of H_{mm'} (Eq. 15: e.g. "σU+L ∝ H00 + H++ + H−−", "σL ∝ H00", "σT ∝ 1/2(H+− + H−+)"). The paper writes H_{mm'}, not normalised ρ_{λλ'}; no ρ-notation map is given.
  - A_i are ratios of helicity cross-sections to σ^{U+L} (Eq. 16). The PDF text extraction of Eq. 16 is garbled (fractions and prefactors unclear), so the exact prefactors are not verified here; see Open issues.
  - Coefficients are "effective", including the γ* and its interference, within the 80–100 GeV window (Sec. 2).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Sec. 6.6 (Summary of uncertainties): "The dominant uncertainty in the measurements of the Ai coefficients is in most cases the statistical uncertainty, even in the most populated bins at low pT".
- Sec. 6.6: "The exception is the A0 coefficient where PDF and electron efficiency uncertainties dominate for pZT values below 80 GeV."
- Sec. 6.6: "The experimental uncertainties are dominated by the lepton uncertainties and are the leading source of systematic uncertainty for low values of pZT for the A2 coefficient."
- Sec. 6.5 (Regularisation): "The only non-negligible source of uncertainty in the methodology was found to be the regularisation bias, which can have a size approaching the statistical uncertainty of A0 and A2." This is the reg_bias column in HEPData.
- Sec. 9 (Summary): "the PDF uncertainties, which are the dominant source of theoretical uncertainties, are below 0.004 in all cases."
- Breakdown in Tables 4–7 is "Summary of regularised uncertainties expected" (MC-expected, not measured). Not extracted.

## Caveats

- Lam–Tung relation: the paper states "The data confirm that the Lam–Tung relation (A0 − A2 = 0) does not hold at O(α2s)" (Sec. 8). A2 is the main outlier relative to DYNNLO and Powheg+MiNLO (Sec. 8).
- Fit is at detector level: "Templates of the nine polynomials folded to the detector level are fitted to the data to extract the angular coefficients in the full phase space of the Z boson." (Sec. 9). The coefficients are not unfolded to particle level in the sections read.
- Lepton-level theory convention: Sec. 2 says "Throughout this paper" the leptons are defined at the Born level "when discussing theoretical calculations or predictions at the event-generator level"; Sec. 4 separately distinguishes reconstructed m_ll, y_ll, p_T^ll from Born-level m_Z, y_Z, p_T^Z [verifier w1R_b]: "the leptons from Z-boson decays are defined at the Born level, i.e. before final-state QED radiation" (Sec. 2) [verifier w1_5].
- Regularisation: the headline values are regularised. "the Ai spectra are regularised by multiplying the unregularised likelihood by a Gaussian penalty term, which is a function of the significance of higher-order derivatives of the Ai with respect to pZT" (Sec. 5.2; details referenced to App. C) [verifier w1_5]. Unregularised values are in HEPData Tables 1–9 (e.g. A0, pT 132–173 GeV, unregularised 0.882853, which differs from regularised 0.856275). Paper text Table 9 matches the regularised HEPData values at displayed precision.
- Uncertainty ranges: the summary states "These uncertainties are small and range from 0.002 at low pT to 0.008 at pZT = 150 GeV" (Sec. 9). It does not say which coefficient.
- Higher-order Pi terms: "any possible breaking of the nine Pi polynomial decomposition is beyond the sensitivity of the analysis" (Sec. 7.3).

## Open issues

1. HEPData Table 11 ("A0-A2 ... (regularised)") disagrees with the paper's own text values for A0−A2 in several low-pT bins. For 5.0–8.0 GeV, HEPData gives 0.015138 while paper Table 9 and Table 11 give 0.018 ± 0.003 ± 0.007. For 0.0–2.5 GeV, HEPData gives −0.0422871 vs paper Table 11 −0.039 ± 0.010 ± 0.008. Agreement is good at high pT (e.g. 22.0–25.5 GeV and 132–173 GeV). Unresolved; I used HEPData per the rule, and this needs a human check of which is correct.
2. Whether the paper's quoted syst. in Tables 9/11 includes the sys,reg_bias component is not stated. HEPData lists it separately.
3. Eq. (16) (A_i definitions via helicity cross-sections) is garbled in the PDF text extraction. Prefactors (2, 2√2, 4, 4√2) should be checked against the typeset equation. The arXiv HTML version is truncated after the introduction paragraph, so it could not be used to verify.
4. Only arXiv v1 was read. The JHEP 08 (2016) 159 version was not checked for changes.
5. HEPData "Data from Figure N" location labels were not cross-checked against the figure list.
6. Jet algorithm and R: not stated in the sections read.
7. No ρ_{λλ'} notation: the density-matrix map is in terms of H_{mm'} with Eq. 12 normalisation. Normalised ρ elements are not given.
