# 1603.08791 — Search for resonances in the mass distribution of jet pairs with one or two jets identified as b-jets in proton–proton collisions at √s=13 TeV with the ATLAS detector

- Authors/Collaboration: ATLAS Collaboration; 2016 (arXiv v1 March 2016, v2 23 Jun 2016, CERN-EP-2016-066); journal ref: Phys. Lett. B per the `\AtlasJournal` macro in the fetched HTML (volume/pages not in the fetched text, not verified); INSPIRE recid 1436362; HEPData record: none (cache file `.cache/1603.08791/hepdata/NONE`: "no HEPData record")
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 3.2 fb⁻¹ with 5% uncertainty (Sec. 3); data (2015, jet trigger requiring a jet pT ≥ 360 GeV). Simulation: Pythia8 A14 / NNPDF2.3 LO for QCD dijet and for b* and Z′ signals; ATLAS detector simulation (GEANT4).
- Topology / final state: dijet, with one or two b-tagged leading/sub-leading jets (categories "1b" inclusive, "2b" double-tag). Signals: b* → bg (85% of decays, per Sec. 3) and Z′ → bb̄ (only bb̄ simulated).
- Selection summary:
  - Anti-kt R = 0.4 jets from topo-clusters; jet pT > 50 GeV; events with leading jet pT > 440 GeV (Sec. 4).
  - Jet |η| < 2.4 (Sec. 4; text says "an additional requirement is placed on the jet pseudorapidity", not explicitly per jet).
  - |y*| < 0.6 with y* = (y1 − y2)/2; m_jj > 1.1 TeV (Sec. 4).
  - b-tag working point 85% (tt̄ calibration); "1b" = at least one of the two leading jets tagged; "2b" = both tagged (Sec. 4).
  - Limits are on σ × A × BR, with A including kinematic acceptance and b-tagging efficiency (Sec. 7; Fig. 5, 6, 7 captions).
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Observable: dijet invariant mass m_jj; background fit f(z) = p1(1 − z)^p2 z^p3, z = m_jj/√s (Eq. 1, Sec. 5).
  - Benchmarks: excited b* (compositeness scale = mass; Γ ≈ 0.6% of mass); SSM Z′ (SM-like fermion couplings, BR(Z′→bb̄) = 13.8%); leptophobic Z′ (BR(Z′→bb̄) = 18.9%); Γ ≈ 3% of mass for Z′ (Sec. 3). Generic Gaussian signals with widths 15%, 10%, 7% of mass (Sec. 7, Fig. 7).
  - Reference frame / polarization quantities: not applicable (no polarization observable in the paper).
  - Limit method: Bayesian 95% credibility-level upper limits, posterior from the binned-likelihood fit; limits interpolated between mass points (Sec. 7).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- b-tagging: "The uncertainty introduced by the application of the b-tagging algorithm is the largest systematic uncertainty in the analysis." (Sec. 6). Efficiency uncertainty: "reaches 50% above 2 TeV" (Sec. 6). Measured from tt̄ in 13 TeV data for jet pT up to 200 GeV, extrapolated at high pT. No numeric value given for the mistag (c and light) uncertainties (Sec. 6: "are estimated in 8 TeV data").
- Extra b-jet JES: "The maximum uncertainty for b-tagged jets is estimated to be 6% and is conservatively applied to all pT regions." (Sec. 6).
- Untagged JES: "For untagged jets it is within the range 1–5% for jet transverse momenta greater than 200 GeV." (Sec. 6).
- r_trk-based extra JES: "The estimated relative additional uncertainty for jets with 200 < pT < 800 GeV is found to be less than 2.6%" (Sec. 6).
- JER: "For jets with pT > 50 GeV, the uncertainty is less than 2%." (Sec. 6).
- Background parameterisation: "is appended as a multiplicative factor to the nominal ansatz" (extra z^{p4 log z} term; difference between fits taken as uncertainty) (Sec. 6).
- Luminosity: 5% (Sec. 3). Included in limit setting (Sec. 7: "The systematic uncertainties associated with the uncertainty in the integrated luminosity, jet energy scale, jet energy resolution, b-tagging and alternative fit functions are all included in the limit-setting.").
- No numeric per-bin total systematic or stat/syst breakdown of the limits is given in the text.

## Caveats

- Benchmark cross sections are leading order in QCD: "excluded ... at leading-order in QCD" (Sec. 7, Fig. 5/6 captions show theory lines, values not extracted).
- Generic Gaussian limits: "These limits should be used when long low-mass off-shell tails from PDFs and non-perturbative effects on the narrow resonance signal shape can be safely truncated or neglected" (Sec. 7). Applicable only to resonances with a Gaussian-like mass shape.
- Credibility level, not frequentist CL: "The limits were obtained using a Bayesian method" (Sec. 7).
- Background fit (Eq. 1): fit p-values 0.73, 0.90, 0.66 for inclusive, "1b", "2b" (Sec. 5), without systematics.
- BumpHunter (not a limit, Sec. 5): most discrepant intervals 1493–1614 GeV ("1b") and 3596–3827 GeV ("2b"); global probability "greater than 60%" for both, without systematics. Summary: "The largest observed local excess is less than 2σ" (Sec. 8).
- Tagging-efficiency correction factors (Fig. 2) are applied to MC; data/MC b-tagging corrections derived from b-enriched samples (Sec. 4). Efficiencies for "2b" are described as "about 2.5 times lower at low mass and a factor 10 lower at high mass" relative to "1b" for Z′ events (Sec. 4).
- Level: limits are on σ × A × BR with A including kinematic acceptance and b-tagging efficiency; whether the reported A is at particle or detector level is not stated.

## Open issues

- No numeric limit values are available: HEPData has no record (cache `NONE`), and the paper contains no tables. Limit curves (Figs. 5, 6, 7) are figure-only; values were not read off plots.
- Mass range of the generic Gaussian limits is inconsistent within the paper: Abstract and Sec. 7 say 1.5–5.0 TeV; Sec. 8 (Summary) says "in the mass range 1.1–5.0 TeV".
- Sec. 8 says "The expected contribution from the b* model is excluded in the mass range 1.1–2.1 TeV", while Sec. 7 says the b* model "is excluded" without specifying observed or expected. Whether the stated b* exclusion range is observed or expected is unresolved from the text.
- The 1.1 TeV lower edges of the b* and Z′ ranges coincide with the analysis threshold m_jj > 1.1 TeV (Sec. 4). The paper does not state whether limits below 1.1 TeV are meaningful.
- Sec. 4 acceptance sentence is ambiguous: "around 20% for the b* benchmark in the '1b' category and drops with increasing mass from 9% at 1.5 TeV to 2% at 5.0 TeV for the Z′ signals for the '2b' category". The text does not clearly separate the two signals/categories; not used as a limit input.
- Terminology: Abstract uses "effective cross sections", Sec. 7 and Sec. 8 use "visible cross sections", both for σ × A × BR of the Gaussian limits. Not resolved in the text.
- Journal volume/pages not verified (only the journal name from the LaTeX macro is in the fetched text).
- Quote conventions: the source is the arXiv HTML rendering (`.cache/1603.08791/paper.html`, plain text `paper.txt`). LaTeX math tokens are rendered as Unicode in the quotes (e.g. "b*", "Z′", "σ × A × BR"), and wording is otherwise copied from that rendering.
