# 1805.09299 — Search for resonances in the mass distribution of jet pairs with one or two jets identified as b-jets in pp collisions at √s = 13 TeV with the ATLAS detector

- Authors/Collaboration, year, journal ref, INSPIRE recid, HEPData record: ATLAS Collaboration (author list not extracted); arXiv v2 (31 Aug 2018); Phys. Rev. D 98, 032016 (2018), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.98.032016; INSPIRE recid 1674532; HEPData record 83179 (ins1674532), Tables 1–26.
- √s = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity: "up to 36.1 fb-1" (high-mass selection, 2015+2016; Sec. 4); low-mass 2b selection 24.3 fb-1 (2016 only, Sec. 4). Data (no projection, no recast).
- Topology / final state: dijet; the two leading jets with ≥1 b-tag (inclusive "1b") or 2 b-tags (Z′, DM Z′ benchmarks). Benchmark signals: b*→bg (85%), SSM and leptophobic Z′→bb̄, DM axial-vector Z′→qq̄ (all 5 flavours, low-mass) or Z′→bb̄ (high-mass), and a Gaussian generic resonance (Sec. 3, 7).
- Selection summary (Sec. 4, 5):
  - Anti-kt R = 0.4 jets from topological clusters, calibrated; |η| < 2.0 for both leading jets; |y*| < 0.8 (low-mass: |y*| < 0.6).
  - Leading-jet pT > 430 GeV (200 GeV in low-mass), subleading pT > 80 GeV.
  - Triggers: high-mass inclusive jet trigger (pT > 380 GeV) for m_jj > 1.2 TeV; low-mass di-b-jet trigger (150 GeV / 50 GeV, online) for 570 GeV < m_jj < 1.5 TeV. Offline b-tag: 85% WP (high-mass), 70% WP plus online (low-mass).
  - Observable: m_jj of the two leading jets; background from a sliding-window fit of Eq. (1) (4 or 5 parameters).
- Model or observable definition (frame/convention): m_jj of the two leading jets; y* = (y1 − y2)/2 (Sec. 4). Polarization / angular frame: not applicable; no polarization or angular quantities are defined or measured (grep of full text for polarization, helicity, Collins, f_L, ρ00: no hits). Limit form as stated in captions: σ (Tables 7, 9), σ×B (Tables 8, 10), σ×A×ε×B (Tables 11, 12, 19–26). b* spin: "excited b-quark" (not stated). SSM and leptophobic Z′: vector (SSM couplings as Z; width 3% of mass). DM mediator: axial-vector, m_DM = 10 TeV, g_DM = 1.0 (Sec. 3). Gaussian: width 0–15% of m (Sec. 7).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Background fit function: "The uncertainty due to the choice of the fit function is determined by repeating the fit procedure with one additional parameter." Table 1 (Sec. 6): (0.01–1.2)% low-mass; (0.01–9)% high-mass ≥1b; (0.01–10)% high-mass 2b. Column assignment is as read from the table (see Open issues).
- Background fit parameters: Table 1: (0.2–2.7)% low-mass; (0.3–34)% high-mass ≥1b; (0.1–32)% high-mass 2b.
- b-tagging efficiency (signal, dominant per Sec. 6): "This uncertainty grows with jet pT, with a smallest uncertainty of 2% for jets with pT around 90 GeV and up to 15% for jet pT around 1.5 TeV." Table 1: (7–20)% low-mass; (3–5)% high-mass ≥1b; (10–30)% high-mass 2b.
- bb-jet trigger (low-mass only): Table 1: (1–20)%; text: "The per-jet uncertainty is estimated to be 1%–20% for jets with pT of 35–700 GeV" (Sec. 6).
- JES, JER, bJES (Table 1, Sec. 6): JES (1–1.2)% / (1.2–1.6)%; JER (0.3–1)% / (0.3–0.4)%; bJES 2.6% (both). Column assignment ambiguous (Open issues).
- Not included: "Theoretical uncertainties affecting the signal cross section are not considered." (Sec. 7). "Other uncertainties that affect only the signal normalization, including the acceptance uncertainties associated with the choice of PDF and the uncertainty in the integrated luminosity, are found to be negligible." (Sec. 6).
- The paper does not give a stat-vs-syst split for any limit. Expected bands include all systematics: "The expected limits as well as the 1σ and 2σ bands are calculated using pseudo-experiments generated from the background model by incorporating all systematic uncertainties in both the signal and background predictions." (Sec. 7).

## Caveats

- Limit type: the paper says "95% credibility-level (C.L.) upper limits on the cross section, where the 95% quantile of the posterior is taken as the upper limit", with "a flat prior ... for the signal normalization" and Gaussian priors on nuisances (Sec. 7). These are Bayesian limits, not CLs-method limits.
- Overlap of the low- and high-mass 2b selections (1.2–1.5 TeV): "For the combination the result with the better expected limit is chosen within the overlap region." (Sec. 7). Observed limits in that region therefore come from one selection, chosen on expected performance.
- Gaussian limits (Tables 11, 12, 19–26) are on σ×A×ε×B; the paper gives no separate A, ε or B, so they cannot be decomposed. The paper states "A MC-based transfer matrix is used to fold in the detector effects." (Sec. 7), while the HEPData captions for Tables 19–26 say "at particle level" (see Open issues).
- Low-mass 2b data (Table 9, 12, 23–26 combined) rely on a bb-jet trigger active only for part of the 2016 data taking: "Because the bb-jet trigger was active only for parts of the data taking period, the total integrated luminosity that the low dijet-mass sample corresponds to is 24.3 fb-1 in 2016." (Sec. 4).
- Background: no significant excess. BumpHunter p-values "0.66, 0.59, and 0.57" for the three regions (Sec. 5). These are not limits; recorded only as context.
- Figure-only, not tabulated: the LO/NLO benchmark cross-section curves overlaid in Figs. 5, 6, 7 (dashed lines) and the mass-limit crossings derived from them. Not read off plots; no values recorded.

## Open issues

1. Table 1 (Sec. 6): the JES, JER, bJES and bb-jet trigger rows have two values for three columns (low-mass, high-mass ≥1b, high-mass 2b). The column mapping is not resolved; I did not infer it. The arXiv HTML cell layout shows the same two-value rows.
2. Table 8 LUMINOSITY qualifier is "36.1 fb$^{-1}$", but Sec. 4 gives the low-mass sample as 24.3 fb-1, and Table 8 combines low- and high-mass. Whether the combined curve uses 24.3 or 36.1 fb-1 in its low-mass part is not stated. Table 9 is labelled 24.3 fb-1.
3. Table 12 (LUMINOSITY "24.3/36.1 fb$^{-1}$"): the HEPData table has two rows at Mass 1.25 TeV (observed 0.0752306887274 and 0.0764455632689 pb). Which luminosity each belongs to is not stated. Neither row is an endpoint or reference mass, so neither is copied; both are outside the primary window.
4. Mass range inconsistency: Abstract and Sec. 1 give 0.57–7 TeV; Sec. 8 Conclusion gives 0.57–5 TeV.
5. Level of Tables 19–26: captions say "at particle level"; the text says the Gaussian limits fold in detector effects with a transfer matrix (Sec. 7); Tables 11–12 captions state no level. Not resolved.
6. Limit form: Table 8 header reads "Cross section upper limit at 95% CL [pb]" while its caption says σ×B. I used the caption. For Table 9 (DM Z′, low-mass), the caption says "cross-section" and does not state whether a branching ratio is included.
7. The b* caption says "cross-section"; whether the 85% b*→bg branching ratio is included is not stated in the caption or text.
8. The text mass limits (2.6, 2.0, 2.1, 2.1, 1.03 TeV) are not tabulated; their observed/expected status and the theory curves used are not stated in the text. Not verified.
9. Tables 19–22 and 23–26 give observed limits only; no expected values tabulated for these auxiliary tables.
10. The 1.03 TeV DM limit lies just above the 1 TeV window edge; reported as-is.
