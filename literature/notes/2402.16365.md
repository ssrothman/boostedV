# 2402.16365 — Studies of the energy dependence of diboson polarization fractions and the Radiation Amplitude Zero effect in WZ production with the ATLAS detector

- ATLAS Collaboration, 2024, Phys. Rev. Lett. 133 (2024) 101802 (DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.133.101802), arXiv 2402.16365 (v3, CERN-EP-2024-045), INSPIRE recid 2762099, HEPData record ins2762099 (DOI 10.17182/hepdata.149992.v1; 45 tables, prefetched locally)
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV, integrated luminosity 140 fb^-1 (2015–2018), data (pp collisions), fiducial measurement with detector corrections in the fit
- Topology / final state: WZ → ℓνℓ'ℓ' (ℓ, ℓ' = e, μ), leptonic W and Z decays, 3 leptons + E_T^miss
- Selection summary:
  - Exactly three leptons; Z candidate = same-flavour opposite-charge pair within 10 GeV of m_Z (Sec. "Event selection")
  - Electrons p_T > 15 GeV, |η| < 2.47 (crack 1.37–1.52 excluded); jets anti-k_t R = 0.4, p_T > 30 GeV, |η| < 4.5 (Sec. "Event selection")
  - RAZ regions: p_T^WZ < 20, 40, 70 GeV (Sec. "Event selection")
  - Polarization-fraction ("00-enhanced") signal regions: p_T^WZ < 70 GeV and either 100 < p_T^Z ≤ 200 GeV or p_T^Z > 200 GeV; fiducial definition in text after Table 2 (ΔR, m_T^W > 30 GeV)
  - Polarization fractions extracted from BDT-score binned profile-likelihood fits (TMVA BDT, seven variables)
- Model or observable definition (with frame/convention):
  - Diboson fractions f_00, f_TT, f_0T, f_T0 "as defined in Ref. [12]", "interpreted as probabilities of correlated polarization states of the W and Z bosons" (Sec. Introduction). The paper does not reproduce the formula.
  - Measured in the fit: f_00 and f_0T+T0 (f_0T and f_T0 merged with SM ratio); f_TT = 1 − f_00 − f_0T+T0 (implicit).
  - Frame: "The boson polarizations are defined in the WZ center-of-mass frame." (Sec. Monte Carlo, applies to the polarized MC templates). Frame for the measured f_00 definition [verifier w3_2]: Ref. [12] = arXiv 2211.09435 (PLB 843 (2023) 137895), whose Sec. 2 uses the "modified helicity" frame with the z-axis along each boson in the diboson CM frame (see notes/2211.09435.md). Originally recorded: not stated.
  - RAZ observables: ΔY(WZ) and |ΔY(ℓ_W Z)|; D = 1 − 2 × N_central/N_sides (Sec. RAZ), N_central for |ΔY(WZ)| < 0.5, N_sides for 0.5 < |ΔY(ℓ_W Z)| < 1.5 (as written in text).

## Caveats

- Q2 scope: the measured quantity is the joint fraction f_00 (both bosons longitudinal), not a single-boson longitudinal fraction f_0 of W or Z. No single-boson f_0, A_0 or ρ_00 is reported in this paper. Include in Q2 only as a borderline entry.
- Q3 scope: the paper measures diagonal joint-state probabilities f_00, f_0T+T0 (merged), and implicit f_TT. f_0T and f_T0 are not separately measured. No off-diagonal/interference element, no angular coefficients A_0–A_7, and no Bell/entanglement observable are measured. Interference is stated to be negligible.
- Projection vs measurement: the SM values in Table 2 are predictions from MadGraph 0,1j@LO reweighted to NLO QCD and NLO EW; they are not measurements.
- Fit-dependence: the paper notes that the 2-parameter fit "may be less conservative as the ratio of f_{0T+T0} and f_{TT} is assumed to have the value predicted by the SM." Quote from Sec. results.
- RAZ TT-state subtraction: "the estimated contributions from all background processes ( \sim 10\% ) and WZ 00 , 0T , and T0 polarization states ( \sim 27\% ) are subtracted from data. The WZ 00 , 0T , and T0 contributions are normalized to the SM predicted cross sections." The TT-state depth D therefore depends on SM-normalized subtraction.
- Particle level: the paper says the fit "includes the corrections for detector efficiency and acceptance effects"; the RAZ distributions are unfolded to particle level. Predictions in Table 2 are particle-level fiducial.
- Table 1 expected yields (100–200 GeV: W_0Z_0 222±5, total expected 1640±60, data 1740; >200 GeV: 47.6±1.5 etc.) are event counts, not polarization measurements; not extracted further.
