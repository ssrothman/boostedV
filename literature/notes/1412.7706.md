# 1412.7706 — Search for pair-produced resonances decaying to jet pairs in proton-proton collisions at √s = 8 TeV

- CMS Collaboration (CMS-EXO-12-052), 2015 (arXiv v2, 3 Jun 2015); Phys. Lett. B 747 (2015) 98–119, DOI 10.1016/j.physletb.2015.04.045 (per INSPIRE); INSPIRE recid 1335501; HEPData: none (prefetch file `.cache/1412.7706/hepdata/NONE`, "no HEPData record")
- √s = 8 TeV; integrated luminosity 19.4 fb⁻¹ (quote: "as much as 19.4 ± 0.5 fb" — Sec. 1; low-mass search 12.4 fb⁻¹, Table 1); data
- Topology / final state: ≥4 jets from hadronic decays of pair-produced resonances. (i) RPV top squark t̃→qq′ (λ″312: two light-flavor jets; λ″323: one b jet + one light jet; BR = 100%); (ii) coloron gg→CC→4 light-flavor jets (BR = 100%). Particle-level generation with MadGraph5 + Pythia 6, detector simulation with GEANT4 (Sec. 4); no projection or recast.
- Selection summary:
  - Four-jet triggers; ≥4 jets with |η| < 2.5 and pT > 80 GeV (low-pT trigger) or pT > 120 GeV (higher-pT trigger) (Sec. 5). Table 1 fourth-jet pT: ">80 GeV" (low-mass), ">120 GeV" (high-mass).
  - Jets: anti-kT, R = 0.5 (Sec. 3, "anti-kT algorithm [24] with a distance parameter of 0.5 in y-φ space"). Four leading jets form three dijet pairings; pairing chosen by minimum ΔR_dijet (Sec. 5, Eq. 2).
  - Table 1 (low-mass 200–300 GeV, 12.4 fb⁻¹ / high-mass >300 GeV, 19.4 fb⁻¹): Δm/m_av < 0.15; Δη_dijet < 1.0; Δ > 70 GeV (low) / > 100 GeV (high), with Δ = (Σ|pT_i|) − m_av (Eq. 3).
  - Heavy-flavor search: at least two of the four highest-pT jets b-tagged (combined secondary vertex algorithm, medium selection; "typical values of b-tagging efficiency and misidentification probabilities of 72% and 1.1%", Sec. 3).
  - Background: four-parameter multijet fit (Eq. 4) on m_av, fit region starting at 200 GeV (low-mass) and 300 GeV (high-mass) (Sec. 6).
- Model or observable definition (frame/convention):
  - Observable: m_av = average mass of the selected dijet pair; limit on production cross section σ (Sec. 7). Stop mass in limit plots is set equal to m_av ("where the top squark mass is equal to m_av", Sec. 7).
  - Limit method: "We set upper limits on the production cross section using a Bayesian formalism with a uniform prior for the cross section" (Sec. 7); CL = 95%, posterior integrated to 0.95 (Sec. 7).
  - Reference frame / polarization quantities: not applicable (no polarization observable); not stated.
  - Signal generation grids (Sec. 4, not limits): top squarks 100 GeV to 1 TeV in 50 GeV steps; colorons "masses are generated from 100 GeV to 2 TeV".
  - Cross sections used for comparison: stop NLO+NLL (Sec. 4, Sec. 7); coloron NLO (Sec. 4).

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

Quoted from Sec. 6 (PDF p. 8–9). All are stated as affecting the signal yield; the paper says they are treated with log-normal priors as nuisance parameters.
- Integrated luminosity: 2.6% (ref. [1]). Quote: "the imperfect knowledge of the integrated luminosity (2.6%) [1]".
- Initial-state radiation simulation: 5% (ref. [28]).
- Jet energy corrections: 1–6.2% (ref. [25]).
- Jet energy resolution: 10% (ref. [25]).
- b-tagging efficiency: 2% (ref. [27]).
- Multiple pp interactions (pileup): <1.5% (ref. [46]). The HTML rendering shows "<<1.5%"; the PDF shows "<1.5%" — PDF used.
- Background-parameterization (alternate functions) uncertainty, Sec. 6: "increases with m_av from 0.3% to 0.6% in the low-mass search range, and from 0.5% to 30% in the high-mass search range." Quote: "This uncertainty increases with m_av from 0.3% to 0.6% in the low-mass search range, and from 0.5% to 30% in the high-mass search range."
- Statistical uncertainties on the limits: not stated in text.
- Theory (stop NLO+NLL, coloron NLO) cross-section uncertainty: "assigned appropriate theoretical uncertainties [31]" (Sec. 4); magnitude not stated in text.

## Caveats

- Mass ranges in Sec. 7 and the abstract are the paper's own statements; the text does not say explicitly whether they come from the observed or the expected curve (see Open issues).
- Stop limits are benchmarked against NLO+NLL cross sections; the exclusion range depends on that curve, which is figure-only here.
- Coloron limit range upper end is 835 GeV; signal samples were generated to 2 TeV (Sec. 4) but no limit above 835 GeV is reported.
- Only RPV top squarks and colorons are searched here. Prior limits cited in the Introduction (Sec. 1, not extracted or verified here): CDF RPV stops 50–90 GeV and colorons 50–125 GeV [13]; ATLAS scalar gluons 100–185 GeV [14] and 150–287 GeV [15]; CMS 7 TeV coloron dijet 250–740 GeV [16]. These are as stated in this paper.
- The paper's HTML rendering (arXiv LaTeXML) uses "Table 0.5" and "0.x" section numbers; the PDF uses "Table 1" and Sections 1–8. This note uses the PDF numbering.
- Source used: arXiv PDF (v2) at `.cache/1412.7706/paper.pdf`, text at `paper.txt`; HTML at `html.html`/`html.txt`. HTML and PDF were compared for the quoted limit sentences and matched.

## Open issues

- HEPData: prefetch file says "no HEPData record"; no tables available. Limit values beyond the text are figure-only, not tabulated; not read from plots.
- Statistical uncertainty on the limit or on the mass endpoints: not stated.
- The text states the stop mass for the limit plots equals m_av (Sec. 7); whether the excluded range is defined on the nominal or the fitted m_av is not stated.
