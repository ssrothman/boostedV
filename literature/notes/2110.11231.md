# 2110.11231 — Measurement of the inclusive and differential production cross sections, polarization angles, and triple gauge couplings in pp collisions at 13 TeV (CMS-SMP-20-014)

- Authors/Collaboration: CMS Collaboration (HEPData breadcrumb: Tumasyan, Armen et al.), 2021 (arXiv ID 2110; cached text is arXiv v2, dated 12 Jul 2022). Journal ref: JHEP 07 (2022) 032 (cached HEPData hep.json "journal_info") [verifier w2_5]. INSPIRE recid 1949191. HEPData record ins1949191 (recid 114362, version 1).
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV, integrated luminosity 137 fb^-1 (HEPData record abstract: "using 137 fb$^{-1}$ of data collected with the CMS detector"). Data.
- Topology / final state: pp -> WZ -> 3 leptons (e, mu) + MET; W and Z polarization fractions measured at reconstruction level in the signal region.
- Selection summary (SR, Sec. 0.5 and Sec. 0.4):
  - Exactly three tight light leptons with at least one opposite-sign same-flavour pair; Z leptons pT > 25 and > 10 GeV, W lepton pT > 25 GeV.
  - |M(l_Z1, l_Z2) - m_Z| < 15 GeV; M(3 leptons) > 100 GeV; MET > 30 GeV; min M(l,l') > 4 GeV.
  - b-jet veto (DeepCSV tight); jets pT > 25 GeV, |eta| < 2.5, anti-kT R = 0.4.
  - Polarization variable: cos(theta_W) (W reconstructed with MET and W-mass constraint for neutrino pz) and cos(theta_Z); fitted as joint binned likelihood on reco-level cos(theta) distributions (Fig. 10).
- Model or observable definition (frame/convention):
  - Frame: helicity (HE) frame. Quote: "results are shown in terms of the helicity (HE) frame" (Sec. 0.10). Frame for the cos variables is the HE frame per the same section.
  - Polarization angle: "the angular distance between the momenta of the \PW ( \PZ ) boson and the (negatively) charged lepton from its primary decay" (Sec. 0.10; LaTeX macros as in the arXiv HTML).
  - Born-level relations, Eq. (9) for W and Eq. (10) for Z, of the form (3/8){[..]^2 f_L + [..]^2 f_R + 2 sin^2(theta) f_0}; Z has the extra constant c = (c_L^2 - c_R^2)/(c_L^2 + c_R^2) with sin^2(theta_eff) = 0.23121 (Sec. 0.10, Eqs. 9-10 from arXiv HTML).
  - Normalization: "f_L+f_R+f_0=1" (Sec. 0.10). Independent parameters fitted: f_0 and f_LR, where "f_LR = f_L - f_R" (Sec. 0.10).
  - Eqs. (9)-(10) hold only without kinematic cuts; the paper therefore fits reco-level templates, not the quadratic distribution directly.
  - Predictions (SM columns) are from a quadratic fit to generator-level cos(theta) with no fiducial cuts (Table caption, Sec. 0.10).
  - Density-matrix mapping rho_lambda,lambda': not stated in the paper text.

## Caveats
- Observed fractions are reco-level fit results in the detector-level SR with templates from simulation, not particle-level fiducial values. The SM predictions are generator-level, no fiducial cuts. Do not compare them as like-for-like without the paper's caveat.
- The paper states the fractions are not independent (f_L+f_R+f_0=1); only f_0 and f_LR are fitted and tabulated. Individual f_L and f_R values are not tabulated.
- Charge categories (inclusive, plus, minus) are not pT bins; no pT-dependent polarization fractions are measured in this paper.
- 2D confidence regions (Fig. 11) exist in HEPData as 2 dLogL grids (tables "Polarization fraction measurements (W, 2D)" and "(Z, 2D)", locations Fig. 11 top/bottom). They are contour scans, not CI values; no values extracted. Figure-only for CI boundaries.
- Unfolded differential cos(theta) distributions ("Differential cross section: polarization observable W/Z" and charge-split variants, Fig. 16) are cross-section distributions of cos(theta) variables, not polarization fractions or density-matrix elements; not extracted.
- The W fraction f_LR for the minus category is 0.01 (observed) with a total uncertainty of -0.049/+0.055; this is recorded as given.
