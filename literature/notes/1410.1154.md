# 1410.1154 — Measurement of the W boson helicity in events with a single reconstructed top quark in pp collisions at sqrt(s) = 8 TeV

- CMS Collaboration (first author per HEPData: Khachatryan, Vardan), 2015 (arXiv v2 14 Jan 2015), JHEP 01 (2015) 053, doi 10.1007/JHEP01(2015)053. INSPIRE recid 1320561. HEPData record: 66567 (ins1320561, hepdata doi 10.17182/hepdata.66567.v1).
- sqrt(s) = 8 TeV, integrated luminosity 19.7 fb^-1 (muon and electron triggers). Data (CMS 2012). Simulation used only for templates and transfer matrix; no projection.
- Topology / final state: t-channel-enriched single-top-quark events, top -> W b with W -> l nu (l = e or mu). Exactly one isolated lepton, exactly two jets, one b-tagged. Sample also includes ttbar events with one leptonically decaying top quark (Sec. 4). Measured W helicity fractions are from the W in top decays, not from W+jets or DY.
- Selection summary:
  - Trigger: isolated muon pT > 24 GeV (muon) or isolated electron pT > 27 GeV (electron); muon candidate |eta| < 2.1.
  - Muon: pT > 26 GeV, |eta| < 2.1, I_rel < 0.12. Electron: pT > 30 GeV, |eta| < 2.5, I < 0.1. Additional loose leptons vetoed.
  - Jets: anti-kT, R = 0.5; exactly two with pT > 40 GeV, |eta| < 4.7, dR(jet, lepton) > 0.3; exactly one b-tagged.
  - MET-based: m_T(W) > 50 GeV; events with complex pz,nu solutions discarded (rejects 40% of single-top and ~50% of other-process events). pz,nu from m_W = 80.4 GeV constraint.
  - Observable: cos(theta*_l) of the reconstructed distribution, fitted with MC templates reweighted to a trial (F_L, F_0, F_R); W+jets normalization floated (beta_jets).
- Model or observable definition (frame and convention as stated in the paper):
  - Eq. (1): rho(cos theta*) = (3/8)(1 - cos theta*)^2 F_L + (3/4) sin^2 theta* F_0 + (3/8)(1 + cos theta*)^2 F_R, with F_i = Gamma_i / Gamma and F_L + F_0 + F_R = 1 (F_R derived from the constraint in the fit).
  - theta*_l: "the angle between the W boson momentum in the top quark rest frame and the momentum of the down-type decay fermion in the rest frame of the W boson" (Sec. 1). The paper does not name this frame (helicity, Collins–Soper, other): "not stated".
  - Reconstructed-level fit (transfer matrix R, Eq. 7); no particle-level unfolding stated.

## Caveats

- Signal definition includes ttbar events with one leptonically decaying top (Sec. 4), so the sample is not pure single top; the W is from top decay in both.
- Fit is at reconstructed level with generator-level reweighting through a transfer matrix (Eq. 7). Results are not stated as particle-level unfolded values.
- Combined F_R (-0.018) lies outside the muon/electron F_R interval; the paper attributes this to the sum constraint and channel weighting (Sec. 8). Quote (PDF p.11): "The behavior of the combined FR value being outside the interval of the FR in the muon and electron channels is a consequence of the".
- Best-fit anomalous couplings g_L = -0.017, g_R = -0.008 and TopFit exclusion limits (Sec. 8, Fig. 4) are out of Q2 scope; not extracted.
- Single inclusive point; no p_T or mass dependence is measured or tabulated.
