# 2604.25678 — Measurement of the Z→μμ angular coefficients in pp collisions at √s = 13 TeV as functions of transverse momentum and rapidity

- Authors/Collaboration: CMS Collaboration (HEPData breadcrumb: "Gevorgyan, Arzunik et al."); CMS note SMP-23-007; year 2026 (arXiv v2 dated 14 Oct 2026, text read from v2); journal ref: preprint (none in the fetched text); INSPIRE recid 3150032; HEPData record: ins3150032 / recid 172103, DOI 10.17182/hepdata.172103.v1 (status "finished", version 1).
- √s = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 140 fb⁻¹ (text, abstract); HEPData qualifier "140.1 fb^{-1}"; 2016–2018 data; data (reconstructed, detector level) with simulation used for templates and validation (POWHEG+MiNNLO signal, MG5_aMC@NLO NLO predictions).
- Topology / final state: Drell–Yan pp → Z/γ* → μ⁺μ⁻ (dimuon), leptonic decay, no jets used.
- Selection summary:
  - Two PF muons; single-muon HLT with p_T^μ > 24 GeV (2016, 2018) or > 27 GeV (2017), |η^μ| < 2.4; leading muon p_T > 26 (2016/2018) or 29 GeV (2017), subleading p_T > 20 GeV.
  - Muon isolation: scalar p_T sum of other objects in ΔR < 0.4 divided by muon p_T < 0.15.
  - 81 < m_μμ < 101 GeV; |y^μμ| < 2.4.
  - Coefficients binned in |y^μμ| = 0–1 and 1–2.4; p_T^μμ edges 0, 10, 20, 35, 55, 80, 120, 200, 400 GeV.
  - Jet algorithm and R: no jets in the data analysis; the aMC@NLO predictions use "the FxFx jet merging scheme with up to two partons" (Sec. 0.2) with "jet merging scale at 30 GeV" (Sec. 0.6) [verifier w2_6].
- Observable definition (CS frame; sec. 0.1, Eq. 1, Eq. 2–3):
  - Eq. (1), as printed: dσ/(dm dy dp_T dcosθ* dφ*) ∝ d³σ^unp/(dm dy dp_T) × [(1+cos²θ*) + (A₀/2)(1−3cos²θ*) + A₁ sin2θ* cosφ* + (A₂/2) sin²θ* cos2φ* + A₃ sinθ* cosφ* + A₄ cosθ* + A₅ sin²θ* sin2φ* + A₆ sin2θ* sinφ* + A₇ sinθ* sinφ*]. Measured coefficients are A_i = P_i/P_8 (sec. 0.4).
  - Frame: Collins–Soper. z bisects the angle between the proton momenta, positive toward the dilepton direction; x lies in the proton plane; y completes the right-handed system. "Target momentum" = beam proton closest in rapidity to the boson. θ*, φ* are angles of the negatively charged lepton.
  - Level: the text states Eq. (1) is the pre-FSR distribution; the coefficients are extracted from detector-level templates (sec. 0.4, Eq. 4), with FSR, acceptance and migration modelled by simulation.
  - Polarization quantities: the text says "The coefficients A₀ and A₂ parametrize the longitudinal (L) polarization fraction ... and the interference between its transverse (T) polarization states, respectively" (sec. 0.1). No f_L, f_0, or ρ₀₀ is defined or measured; their exact definition in terms of A_i is not stated.
  - Elements measured (Q3): A₀ … A₇ (all eight, in the CS frame) and the difference A₀ − A₂ (Lam–Tung test). No off-diagonal density-matrix elements, no joint diboson matrix, no entanglement/Bell observable.

## Dominant systematics (as stated by the paper, with location)

- Sources (sec. 0.5): pileup reweighting, trigger efficiency, muon ID and isolation efficiencies, detector misalignment, luminosity, prefiring, theory cross sections, MC sample size, p_T^μμ shape, PDF + α_S(m_Z) (PDF4LHC Hessian), and μ_F, μ_R scale variations (×0.5, ×2). Each source is varied independently and the A_i shift taken as its uncertainty. Total = quadrature sum, separately for positive and negative.
- "The most significant uncertainties for A₀, A₁, A₂ come from the efficiency corrections." (sec. 0.5; repeated in sec. 0.7.)
- "The coefficients A₃ and A₄ are also sensitive to the PDF + α_S(m_Z) uncertainty." (sec. 0.5.)
- "The systematic uncertainty is dominant for coefficients A₀ and A₁ for p_T < 55 GeV, and for coefficients A₂, A₃, and A₄ for p_T < 200 GeV." (sec. 0.6.) "For the other cases, the statistical uncertainty dominates." (sec. 0.6.)
- MC sample size "contributes significantly to the all coefficient uncertainties for p_T > 80 GeV" (sec. 0.5).
- A₀−A₂ uncertainty: quadrature sum of A₀ and A₂ uncertainties "with the correlation between these coefficients taken into account" (sec. 0.5). HEPData gives the A₀−A₂ values directly with their own uncertainties.
- Scale and PDF variations of the MC predictions change them "by less than 10⁻⁴" (sec. 0.5). Fit: largest parameter correlations P₀, P₂, P₈ "about 0.7" (sec. 0.4). Fit χ²/ndf "in the range 0.9–1.2" (sec. 0.4).
- Per-source numbers are not tabulated; only the qualitative statements above are given.

## Caveats

- Stat and syst are given separately in HEPData for every row; the syst column is an asymmetric pair (minus, plus). Do not symmetrise.
- The results are the fitted P_i/P_8 from a template fit; the templates and the FSR/acceptance corrections depend on the simulation (sec. 0.4). The paper says the method was validated on simulated events with "consistent with the reference values within the statistical precision".
- Level: pre-FSR definition of Eq. (1), obtained through detector-level templates; the text does not present a separate fiducial/particle-level unfolded result.
- A₅, A₆, A₇ are O(10⁻³) and mostly consistent with zero (sec. 0.6); the largest quoted deviation is 2.8σ for A₆ (sec. 0.6).
- The paper's narrative on A₀ and A₂ as "longitudinal" and "transverse-interference" quantities is qualitative; no helicity-fraction f_L, f_0 or ρ₀₀ value is given.
- Q3: no theory paper or map between A_i and ρ_λλ′ is given in this paper; the only A_i relation is A_i = P_i/P_8 (sec. 0.4).
- Covariance matrices of the fitted P_i (16 HEPData tables, one per p_T bin and rapidity bin, described "Not included in the text of the paper") were not copied; they are correlations of fit parameters, not spin-density-matrix elements.
