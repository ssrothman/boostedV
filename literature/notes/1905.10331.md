# 1905.10331 — Search for low-mass quark-antiquark resonances produced in association with a photon at √s = 13 TeV

- Authors/Collaboration: CMS Collaboration (Sirunyan, A. M. et al.), 2019; journal ref Phys. Rev. Lett. 123 (2019) 231803 (DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.123.231803); CMS note EXO-17-027; INSPIRE recid 1736729; HEPData record: none (hepdata.net ins1736729 returns 404; arXiv:1905.10331 search returns 0 hits)
- sqrt(s) = 13 TeV; integrated luminosity 35.9 fb⁻¹ ("recorded in 2016"); data (pp collisions), 95% CL upper limits; simulation used for signal shapes and resonant backgrounds
- Topology / final state: pp → Z′ + γ (ISR photon), Z′ → q q̄ reconstructed as one large-radius AK8 jet with two-prong substructure, plus one photon; localized excess in soft-drop jet mass
- Selection summary:
  - Trigger: one photon pT > 175 GeV, |η| < 3.0; offline photon pT > 200 GeV, |η| < 2.4 (Sec. Event selection, text)
  - Leading AK8 jet (anti-kT, R = 0.8) pT > 200 GeV; ΔR(jet, γ) > 2.2; photon-lepton/photon vetoes (γ pT > 14 GeV, lepton pT > 10 GeV)
  - AK4 (anti-kT, R = 0.4) jet with pT > 30 GeV passing loose CSVv2 (outside ΔR < 0.6 of AK8 jet), or pTmiss > 75 GeV → event discarded
  - Soft drop β = 0, z_cut = 0.1; N²_DDT < 0 (decorrelated N²₁ cut retaining 10% of background); signal region |η_γ| < 2.1; fit range up to 201 GeV
- Model or observable definition (frame/convention):
  - Vector resonance Z′ coupling to quarks g′_q; benchmark g′_q = 1/6 used only to generate simulated signal (not a limit). Model "assumes no interaction between the SM Z and the Z′" (text). Lagrangian and normalization of g′_q: reference [28] only (Dobrescu & Yu, Erratum, PRD 90 (2014) 079901, the erratum to arXiv:1306.2629; normalization in 1306.2629 Eq. (6)) [verifier w1_1]; not restated in this paper.
  - Limit observable: g′_q (coupling), not σ×B. Polarization/reference frame: not applicable (no spin-correlation observable).
  - Signal mass range: 10–125 GeV (not up to 1 TeV). Simulated points 10, 25, 50, 75, 100, 125 GeV; interpolated in 5 GeV steps.

## Caveats

- Limit is only on g′_q for a vector Z′ with the benchmark model of ref. [28]; no spin/parity other than vector is covered by this paper. Scope for Q1 (10 GeV–1 TeV) is met only for 10–125 GeV.
- Text says the limit is "more stringent than the indirect limits set by measurements of the Z boson and Υ meson decay widths [18]" for most of the range below 50 GeV (qualitative; no numbers in text). Indirect limits are not extracted here.
- Paper states it is "the first direct constraints on quark-antiquark resonance masses below 50 GeV obtained at a hadron collider" (Abstract) — a claim, not a number.
- Limit values for ATLAS [16] (arXiv:1901.10917) and CMS [17] (arXiv:1710.00159) appear only in Fig. 2 in this paper; not extracted (other papers, not in scope of this note).
- Table 1 normalization uncertainties marked † are floating and constrained by the final fit; the quoted percentages are the input uncertainties as tabulated, not post-fit values.
- Source text: arXiv HTML (LaTeXML) of v2 (arXiv:1905.10331v2, 03 Dec 2019). Math in the text was checked against its alttext; the quoted sentences are copied from that text.
